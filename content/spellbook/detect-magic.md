title: "Detect Magic"
level: 1
school: "Divination"
casting_time: "1 Action (or +10 mins as Ritual)"
range: "Self (30-foot sphere)"
components: "V, S"
duration: "Concentration, up to 10 minutes"
concentration: true
ritual: true
prepared: false
sculpt_spells: false
potent_cantrip: false
upcast_notes: "Cast as a Ritual directly from Spellbook without preparation."
---

For the duration, you sense the presence of magic within 30 feet of you. If you sense magic in this way, you can use your action to see a faint aura around any visible creature or object in the area that bears magic, and you learn its school of magic.
