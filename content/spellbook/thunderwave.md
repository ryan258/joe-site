A wave of thunderous force sweeps out from you. Each creature in a 15-foot cube originating from you makes a DC 15 Constitution save. On a fail, takes 2d8 Thunder damage and is pushed 10 feet away. Unsecured objects are pushed 10 feet. Emits a thunderous boom audible up to 300 feet.
