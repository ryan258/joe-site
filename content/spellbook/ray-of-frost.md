A frigid beam of blue-white light streaks toward a creature within range. Make a ranged spell attack (+7). On a hit, the target takes 2d8 Cold damage, and its Speed is reduced by 10 feet until the start of your next turn.

**2024 Potent Cantrip Rule:** If the attack misses, the target takes half damage (the speed reduction does not apply on a miss).
