A stroke of lightning forming a line 100 feet long and 5 feet wide blasts out from you. Each creature in line makes a DC 15 Dexterity save: 8d6 Lightning damage on fail, half on pass. Sculpt Spells protects up to 4 allies (auto-success, 0 damage).
