You hurl a mote of fire at a creature or object within range. Make a ranged spell attack (+7). On a hit, the target takes 2d10 Fire damage. Flammable objects not being worn or carried ignite.

**2024 Potent Cantrip Rule:** If your attack roll misses, the target still takes half of the damage rolled (no additional fire spreading).
