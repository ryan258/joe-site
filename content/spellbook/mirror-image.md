Three illusory duplicates of yourself appear in your space. Each time a creature targets you with an attack, roll a d20 to determine whether the attack targets a duplicate (6+ with 3 images, 8+ with 2, 11+ with 1). A duplicate's AC is 10 + Dex mod (+2) = 12. Destroyed on hit.
