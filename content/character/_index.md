## 🎭 Roleplay & Identity Pillars

<div class="plays-grid">

  <div class="hud-panel">
    <h3 style="font-size:1.1rem;font-weight:700;color:var(--accent-cyan);margin-bottom:8px;">Personality & Mannerisms</h3>
    <p style="font-size:0.85rem;color:var(--text-med);line-height:1.5;">
      Quietly precise. Joe usually speaks only after observing. He demonstrates warmth through practical assistance and tactical protection, with his gentlest humor and affection reserved for Cow.
    </p>
  </div>

  <div class="hud-panel">
    <h3 style="font-size:1.1rem;font-weight:700;color:var(--accent-fey);margin-bottom:8px;">Ideal: Non-Expendable Life</h3>
    <p style="font-size:0.85rem;color:var(--text-med);line-height:1.5;">
      <em>Lives are not expendable.</em> Precise, overwhelming force is justified only when it prevents greater harm. He will never treat another person as bait or disposable evidence.
    </p>
  </div>

  <div class="hud-panel">
    <h3 style="font-size:1.1rem;font-weight:700;color:var(--accent-fire);margin-bottom:8px;">Bond: Keeper of Cow</h3>
    <p style="font-size:0.85rem;color:var(--text-med);line-height:1.5;">
      Joe protects Cow from anyone who would imprison the Fey spirit or force it to reveal its lost, ancient memories. Joe's greatest nightmare is Cow falling into hostile hands.
    </p>
  </div>

  <div class="hud-panel">
    <h3 style="font-size:1.1rem;font-weight:700;color:var(--accent-amber);margin-bottom:8px;">Flaw: The System Assumption</h3>
    <p style="font-size:0.85rem;color:var(--text-med);line-height:1.5;">

    </p>
