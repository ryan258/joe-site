The 10 GP supply chain: Cow's continuity, managed like
ammunition. (J-33 opened the account; this is the standing
policy.)

### When to use it

- Continuously — the logistics play under every risk
  decision in this file: the audit's denominator is
  always "portions in the pouch."

### Mechanics

1. The accounting: each re-summoning consumes one
   10 GP incense portion (1-hour ritual, J-33) —
   current stock **two portions** per the sheet,
   floor policy **never fewer than two** (one loss
   plus one spare), target stock **four** in
   hunting-season campaigns (when J-100's stalkers
   are active, the form's destruction is a tactic
   someone may *choose*).
2. Procurement doctrine (J-148's checklist owns the
   line): every town, first day, before potions —
   incense is cheaper than everything it insures;
   and the *source* gets vetted lightly (a
   specialty component in repeated purchases is a
   pattern a hunter can watch — rotate vendors,
   J-69's counter-surveillance instinct applied to
   shopping).
3. The stock level feeds the risk math explicitly
   (play 66's soft factors, formalized): two
   portions = doctrine flying only; four = normal
   operations; *one* = Cow is grounded to toy-mode
   and pack-riding until the pouch recovers — a
   standing rule that removes the worst decision
   (flying the irreplaceable) from tired-day
   judgment.
4. The deeper line item is the sheet's: the ritual
   restores the *form* — the spirit is never at
   stake (J-33's comfort) — but the hour, the
   portion, and the *dignity* all cost, and the
   ledger's real function is making Joe pay them
   on paper *before* the field, where the price
   is a bird.

### At the table

Mechanically: "Town day, standard order: incense
first — we're at two, buying two more, different
vendor than last month. That puts us at four,
which unlocks normal flight operations for the
border job. If we ever hit one portion, Cow's
grounded by standing rule, no debate, no
'just this once.'"

Verbally: *"In the scholar's ledger, between
candles and ink, a recurring entry: *incense,
two portions, 20 gp* — the world's strangest
life insurance, paid quarterly, for a
policyholder who naps in a pocket dimension
and has never once asked what it costs to
bring him home."*
