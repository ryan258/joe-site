Joe attends the negotiation. Cow attends the room the hosts
think is private.

### When to use it

- Any parley on the other side's ground: what's said at the
  table is theater; what's said in the antechamber is policy
  (J-63 buys this with a 4th-level slot; Cow does it free
  when insertion exists).

### Mechanics

1. Insertion by whatever the venue allows: toy-mode on the
   mantel (play 26 — the premium version), the window ledge of
   the back room (play 6's frames), the eave over the kitchen
   passage (play 7's ears).
2. Coverage priorities, briefed in advance: the room the host
   *leaves to*, the messenger traffic, the armed men not at
   the table, and the person who listens at the door from the
   *inside*.
3. Joe's split attention runs on the alert-ping model (play
   18): full presence at the table, the link idling until Cow
   flags *movement, arrival, wrongness* — then one excused
   sip of wine covers the Bonus-Action look (play 19).
4. The tell that ends negotiations: the back room *emptying*
   mid-parley is men moving to positions — the two-room
   meeting's whole purpose is hearing the trap being set
   while there's still a door (J-97 executes; J-61 if there
   isn't).

### At the table

Mechanically: "I'll be at the table being reasonable. Cow's
on the kitchen-passage eave: I want the back room's traffic —
who the steward reports to between courses, and especially
if that room ever empties all at once. If it does, I spill
my wine, and that's the signal we're leaving."

Verbally: *"The baron negotiates with one scholar and, though
it never appears in any account of the evening, against one
small attentive shadow above his servants' passage — which
is why, when the trap finally moved, the scholar was already
reaching for his coat."*
