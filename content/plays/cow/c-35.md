Getting Cow through checkpoints, pat-downs, and paranoid
doors: the layered options, priced.

### When to use it

- Any controlled entry the party must pass *with* its
  sensor: gates, prisons, audiences, borders.

### Mechanics

1. Layer one, usually sufficient: **toy-mode is its own
   pass** — searches hunt weapons, contraband, and value;
   a worn toy is none of the three, and the searcher who
   handles Mr. Big finds stuffing and sentiment (no aura
   *item* to detect; a creature-detection sweep is rarer
   and is the layer-three case).
2. Layer two, for searches with teeth: **the pocket
   dimension is unsearchable** — dismissed before the
   checkpoint (a Magic action), recalled inside (within 30
   feet of Joe, J-89) — the sensor simply does not exist
   during the search. Cost: no coverage during transit,
   and the recall wants an unobserved moment.
3. Layer three, for mage-checked doors: don't. The sweep
   veto (J-88) is absolute — Cow waits outside (plays 4,
   21: a window perch inside 100 feet often covers the
   room anyway), and the meeting gets the two-room
   treatment from without.
4. The decoy option (play 36) muddies repeat searches:
   guards who searched a toy last week wave the toy
   through this week — establish the pattern with the
   decoy, spend the pattern with the real one only when
   the take justifies it.

### At the table

Mechanically: "Three doors between us and the audience:
the gate searches bags — toy-mode covers that; the hall
door pats down — pocket dimension before, recall after,
in the cloak alcove; and if the throne room has a court
mage, Cow's day ends at the hall — window perch, sixty
feet, which covers the throne room anyway through glass
older than the dynasty."

Verbally: *"The duke's security is famous: three doors,
three searches, nothing enters unexamined. The small grey
spirit passes the first as stuffing, the second as
nothing at all, and declines the third professionally —
watching the whole audience instead through a window the
security budget never mentioned."*
