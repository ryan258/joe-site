Cow reading the other side of the table: live emotional
telemetry under every parley.

### When to use it

- Negotiations, deals, and audiences — running alongside
  the two-room meeting (play 20) but pointed at the
  *table itself*: what the counterpart feels versus
  what they say.

### Mechanics

1. Station: in the room (toy-mode on the table's
   clutter, play 26; the satchel's gap, play 40) or
   at its window — inside the sonar's read of the
   principals (play 17, DM-mediated as always).
2. The telemetry's negotiation-grade products:
   *composure versus feeling* gaps (the calm voice
   over spiking fear — they need this deal more
   than they show: press); the *reaction to
   numbers* (the offer that lands as relief was
   too generous; as anger, too thin); and the
   *tell of decision already made* (boredom under
   the haggling — the theater is for someone else
   in the room: find who).
3. The channel's discipline (J-79's rule, always):
   impressions steer Joe's *questions and offers*,
   never get asserted — the play is Joe re-opening
   the third clause unprompted and watching what
   happens, not announcing what his toy felt.
4. Joe's own side gets audited too: the party's
   tells, leaks, and over-eagerness read by the
   same sonar in rehearsal (play 95's drills) —
   the barometer that calibrated the team before
   the room is why the room reads nothing off
   them.

### At the table

Mechanically: "Mr. Big sits on my papers through
this one. ...Her voice hasn't moved but the
feeling spiked when I mentioned the northern
route — that's the pressure point. And her
boredom through the price-haggling means the
decision's already made and this theater's for
the clerk. So: I re-open the northern route,
address the clerk, and we're done in ten
minutes."

Verbally: *"The trade-mistress negotiates behind
a face her rivals call quarried stone. Across
the table, a small worn toy reads the weather
behind the stone — the flinch at 'northern
route,' the yawn beneath the haggling — and the
scholar beside it adjusts his offers like a man
sailing to instruments through fog."*
