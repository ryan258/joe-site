Cow recognizing the ancient: places, things, and workings
older than the mundane record.

### When to use it

- Ruins, standing stones, old borders, nameless roads —
  sites where the party's History (+7, J-78) dates the
  *human* layers, and something older is the actual
  question.

### Mechanics

1. The instrument: a spirit whose memory predates the
   archives doesn't *study* the old places — it
   *recognizes* them (or doesn't, which is equally a
   finding: the "ancient" shrine Cow reads as
   *younger than the toy* just demoted a forgery,
   J-83's authentication at millennium scale).
2. The protocol is play 87's, site-shaped: walk Cow
   through the place (perched on Joe's shoulder — the
   anchor is the ground itself), and log the
   *texture* of the reactions: familiarity,
   unease, grief, the pull toward a particular
   stone — fragments mapped onto the site plan
   (J-140's sketch, annotated in a second color for
   the second witness).
3. The corroboration braid (the method's signature):
   Cow's recognition + History's dating +
   Investigation's physical read (+10) = three
   independent instruments on one site — where all
   three agree, the party *knows*; where they
   diverge, the divergence is the discovery (the
   road History calls Imperial that Cow knows as
   *older, re-surfaced* rewrites the map's whole
   story).
4. The respect rail extends to places: sites Cow
   reads as *still occupied* (the unease that
   means something remains) get the Fey protocol
   (play 89), not the trowel — the Keeper's
   manners are also, always, survival doctrine.

### At the table

Mechanically: "History says this waystone is
Third Dynasty. Cow says — walking it now —
*familiar, older, and the carving is a
replacement*: the stone remembers a different
mark. So the Dynasty claimed an older road, and
the original mark is our lead. Investigation on
the base for tool-marks... there. Two witnesses
agree; the archive's official story just became
the case's first lie."

Verbally: *"To the scholars, the waystone is a
catalogued antiquity, dated and done. The small
spirit riding the scholar's shoulder greets it
the way one greets an old acquaintance wearing
a new coat badly — and its unease, precisely
mapped, redraws the border of a kingdom that
was never where the books agreed it was."*
