The core combat contribution, owned in full. (J-91 introduced
it; this is the operator's page.)

### When to use it

- Every combat round Cow is deployed and the field is
  AoE-clean: the question is never *whether* Help, only
  *where*.

### Mechanics

1. The mechanics, complete: Cow's own turn (own
   initiative), Help action distracting a creature within
   5 feet — one ally gains **Advantage on their next
   attack roll** against it before Cow's next turn;
   **Flyby** makes the exit provoke nothing; 60 feet of
   fly speed puts the whole approach-Help-exit inside one
   turn with altitude to spare.
2. Target selection is the craft: Advantage is worth most
   on **low-probability, high-payload attacks** — the
   rogue's Sneak Attack (J-129's math), the paladin's
   nova round (J-131), the fighter vs the high-AC captain
   (J-133's economy). Cow's Help goes where the party's
   biggest swing meets the worst odds.
3. The window announcement is mandatory (the fix from
   J-91): "Advantage on the priest until Cow's next
   turn — whoever attacks it first, that's yours" — an
   unclaimed window is a wasted flight.
4. The exposure audit runs every pass (the 1 HP
   constitution): reach weapons, readied actions, and
   live AoE each veto a run (play 66 makes the no-fly
   call); a clean fight can sustain a Help every round —
   a bird-shaped +4-ish to the party's best attack,
   free, forever.
