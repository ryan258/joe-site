The touch-spell delivery, part two: *Fly* placed on the
martial mid-combat, by air mail.

### When to use it

- The fighter who needs to be *up there* — the enemy
  caster's ledge, the wyvern rider's altitude, the wall's
  defenders — while Joe holds position at range.

### Mechanics

1. Same delivery rule as play 48: Joe casts *Fly*
   (touch, Concentration, 10 minutes, J-54), Cow within
   100 feet of Joe delivers the touch with its Reaction —
   the fighter sprouts 60 feet of Fly Speed without Joe
   ever closing to melee range.
2. The tactical product: J-55's "flying fighter
   delivered to the enemy caster" without Joe's
   positioning cost — Joe keeps his 60-foot standoff
   (J-136), the fighter gets the sky, and Cow's transit
   is one Flyby pass through the friendly zone (minimal
   exposure; the 1 HP audit still runs, J-89 vetoes
   through live AoE).
3. Joe holds the Concentration, so the whole package
   rides his defense stack (J-46): the fighter at
   altitude is trusting Joe's CON save — brief it,
   protect it, and the fighter hugs terrain at
   safe-fall height when the fight allows (J-93's abort
   ceiling, delegated).
4. The combo's grammar generalizes: *any* touch spell
   Joe ever adds (future healing, future
   protections) inherits the delivery network — the
   spellbook's touch column is worth a re-read at every
   level (J-150's planning eye).

### At the table

Mechanically: "Brenna, you want that ledge? Granted:
casting Fly — Cow delivers the touch on a pass, one
round. Sixty feet of fly speed, ten minutes, my
Concentration — so I'm keeping my images up and you're
staying under thirty feet whenever you can spare the
altitude. Go ruin his afternoon."

Verbally: *"The war-priest chose his ledge for the
stairs it doesn't have. The fighter arrives anyway —
wings borrowed from a scholar's spellbook, delivered
to her shoulder by a toy — rising past his sermon
with her hammer already back."*
