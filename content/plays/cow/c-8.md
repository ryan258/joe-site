Tracking a moving light through darkness: the courier, the patrol,
the midnight visitor — followed by the one thing that sees them
better than they see their own feet.

### When to use it

- Someone moves at night and their destination is the case: the
  bribed guard's route, the smuggler's lantern, the visitor who
  only comes after dark.

### Mechanics

1. A lantern at night is a beacon to Darkvision 120 — Cow tracks
   from far outside the light's throw, high and behind, where the
   carrier's night-blind eyes (staring at their own flame) never
   look.
2. Doused-light discipline: when the lantern dies, darkvision
   keeps the *carrier* — the doused light is itself the finding
   (people go dark at thresholds; mark the spot).
3. The follow ends at a door, and the door gets the full
   treatment: position logged for the map, play 4's perch
   established if the night allows, and the party's morning
   spent on what the night located (plays 6–7).
4. Joe's ground-follow (J-138) pairs where streets allow —
   but pure-dark country routes are Cow's alone; the wizard
   without darkvision (J-115) stays home and takes the report.

### At the table

Mechanically: "There — the gate guard's walking out his shift
with a shuttered lantern. Cow's on him from eighty feet up and
behind. I'll be asleep like an honest man; wake me when the
light stops moving, and we'll know by breakfast whose door
takes deliveries at three bells."

Verbally: *"The guard walks his secret through streets he
believes are blind. Above and behind, the night — which was
never blind, only discreet — takes notes."*
