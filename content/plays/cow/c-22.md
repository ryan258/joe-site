Joe as the middle link: positioning so the party, Joe, and Cow
form one connected sensor line.

### When to use it

- Split operations: the entry team inside, Joe outside, Cow
  deeper inside — or the party in the valley, Joe on the
  ridge, Cow over the next one.

### Mechanics

1. The chain's arithmetic: *Message* reaches the party at 120
   feet (J-11), the link reaches Cow at 100 — Joe standing at
   the hinge point commands a 220-foot instrumented line, live
   in both directions, silent end to end.
2. Placement discipline: Joe holds where *both* legs stay in
   range — the courtyard wall between the entry team and
   Cow's interior perch; the ridge saddle between camp and
   overwatch. The hinge moves only when both ends' next
   positions are known.
3. Traffic runs by exception (the alert-ping model, play 18):
   Cow flags, Joe rides the look (play 19), Joe whispers the
   product to the team via Message — enemy movement inside
   reaches the entry team's ears within one round of
   happening, no light, no sound, no runner.
4. The chain's known failure: Joe *is* the single point — his
   silence is the team's abort signal, pre-briefed (play 75's
   dead-man logic, applied to the man).

### At the table

Mechanically: "I hold the cloister wall: Senna and Tam work
the vestry inside my Message range, Cow perches the chapel
rafters inside my link range. Anything moves in that nave,
I feel it, look, and you two hear it whispered inside six
seconds. If I go quiet for a full minute, you leave without
me — that's standing."

Verbally: *"Between the burglars and the watching dark
stands one unremarkable man against a wall — a relay made of
patience, whispering the building's secrets to the people
robbing it, at the speed of feeling."*
