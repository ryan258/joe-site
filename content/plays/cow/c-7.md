Owl hearing at the chimney, the vent, the shutter gap: conversations
extracted through the building's own openings.

### When to use it

- The meeting happens inside and the party can't be; voices matter
  more than sights; play 6 found *which* room and this play works
  it.

### Mechanics

1. Buildings leak sound upward and outward — chimneys, eave vents,
   louvres, the gap over shutters. Cow perches at the leak with
   Perception +5 to follow voices through structure; the DM sets the
   DC by construction.
2. The link carries *sound as Cow hears it* only when Joe rides
   along (play 19, within 100 feet) — so wired jobs put Joe in the
   alley below, Bonus-Action listening in shifts. Beyond the link, Cow
   carries back *remembered sensations*: tone, names repeated,
   anger, the scrape of a chest being moved — fragments, honestly
   framed (the sheet's rule).
3. Cold chimneys only — brief the obvious (a Fey spirit is not
   above forgetting fire exists when focused; the *Keeper* prices
   the perch).
4. Pair with play 20 when a wall is all that separates: toy-mode
   inside beats bird-mode outside wherever insertion exists.

### At the table

Mechanically: "The meeting's in the back room, no window. Cold
chimney, though — Cow's on the pot, I'm in the alley inside a
hundred feet, riding the ears. Owl hearing with Advantage
through one floor of old boards. Quote me the names when they
say them."

Verbally: *"The conspirators check the door, the window, the
street, and the man watching the street. High above, the
chimney listens with the oldest ears in the quarter."*
