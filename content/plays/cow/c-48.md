The touch-spell delivery, part one: *Mage Armor* placed on
an ally Cow can reach and Joe can't.

### When to use it

- The unarmored ally beyond touch range at the moment it
  matters: the prisoner about to run (J-137), the ally
  across the courtyard, the swimmer, the climber — AC 15
  for 8 hours, delivered by wing.

### Mechanics

1. The rule (2024 *Find Familiar*): when Joe casts a
   **touch-range spell**, Cow can deliver it — Cow must
   be **within 100 feet of Joe**, and uses its
   **Reaction** to touch the target as the spell is
   cast. Joe's action and slot, Cow's touch: the spell's
   range is effectively "anywhere Cow can be."
2. *Mage Armor*'s fit is perfect: willing target,
   unarmored, 8-hour duration, no Concentration — the
   pre-mission cast on the infiltrator already past the
   wall, the rescued prisoner across the yard, the
   ally who left camp before Joe woke (the dawn policy,
   J-34, now has a delivery service).
3. The choreography: position Cow at the target
   (perched on their shoulder is charming and legal),
   Joe casts within 100 feet of *Cow* (not the target —
   the tether is Joe-to-Cow), touch delivered, done.
   One round, no risk beyond Cow's transit.
4. The doctrine note: this converts Joe's 1st-level
   slot into remote protection — the escort plays
   (J-137) and split-team plays (play 40) all quietly
   upgraded.

### At the table

Mechanically: "Senna's already over the wall and the
job just got louder — Cow's on her shoulder anyway:
I cast Mage Armor from here, forty feet inside the
tether, and Cow delivers the touch. She's AC 15 for
the next eight hours and never broke stride."

Verbally: *"The burglar feels small talons settle on
