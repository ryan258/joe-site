Cow's own security case: the standing investigation into
who hunts the spirit. (J-100 wrote the protocol; this is
the file it feeds.)

### When to use it

- Continuously, at low simmer — the campaign-length case
  under all other cases: every stalker-datum collected,
  braided, and read for the pattern.

### Mechanics

1. The collection layer runs on this file's own
   plays, permanently tasked: the character ledger's
   too-long-looks column (play 86's reciprocal
   entries); the name ledger's fear-column (play
   91); the counter-tail's confirmed watchers
   (play 69); the decoy's takers (play 36's
   thief-thread); play 87's declined subjects
   (the heaviness map is also a map of what
   hunters *want*).
2. The analysis layer is Joe's craft on his own
   behalf (J-76's braid, J-140's ink, J-141's
   archives): who asks about toys, spirits, or
   the old names; whose interest repeats across
   towns (the pattern *is* the finding — one
   curious clerk is nothing; the same question in
   three cities is an organization); and the
   file's standing question — *what do they know
   that we don't know they know?* — reviewed at
   every town stop (play 93's rhythm).
3. The response ladder stays J-100's (still-cover,
   pocket, precise force, never-bait, the
   always-available 4th) — this play adds the
   *proactive* rung: identified hunters get
   *investigated to their source* (the
   paymaster, not the hired eyes — J-138, J-141,
   play 58's follow-home, aimed up the chain),
   because the sheet's fear ends only when the
   *want* ends, and wants have addresses.
4. The file's custody mirrors play 98: duplicated
   (J-145's doctrine — the counter-file is the
   one document Joe backs up before his own
   spellbook), and its successor-holders named —
   the case must survive its investigator,
   because the hunt would.

### At the table

Mechanically: "Town-stop review of the deep
file: the clerk who eyed Mr. Big in Harrenford
asked the same 'antique toys' question here —
that's two cities, so it's an organization,
and the file gets a name page. Response stays
cold: decoy stays visible, Cow stays pack-side
in town, and Senna starts tracing who pays
that clerk. We don't wait for their third
city."

Verbally: *"Somewhere, someone is patiently
assembling a file on a toy cow. They are
unaware — though the discovery is scheduled —
that the toy's thin grey keeper has been
assembling a file on *them* since before they
knew his name: names, cities, questions,
paymasters — a counter-hunt conducted entirely
in ink, by the one man in the world who reads
faster than they can look."*
