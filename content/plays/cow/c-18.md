Cow covers the arc Joe can't watch: the standing 360-degree
sensor formation, one wizard and one bird.

### When to use it

- Whenever Joe works with his attention committed — Study actions
  at scenes, spell Concentration, negotiations, transcription —
  and the room or street stays live around him.

### Mechanics

1. The geometry: Cow perches covering the approach *behind* Joe's
   task — the alley mouth at his back, the door behind the desk,
   the window over his shoulder — the arc chosen by asking
   "where would *I* approach me from?"
2. The link's alert is faster than any shout (emotion-ping of
   *approach* arrives at thought-speed) and silent — Joe turns
   pages, apparently oblivious, wholly covered (play 16's
   codebook: rain-smell, direction as landmark).
3. This is the working answer to Joe's mediocre Perception (+3):
   the wizard reads at +10 *because* something with Advantage on
   Perception owns his blind arc — division of cognitive labor,
   formalized.
4. In combat the wedge inverts into J-96's opening read: Cow
   watches the flank the fight forgot (play 59) while Joe plays
   the visible board.

### At the table

Mechanically: "I'll be under the desk-lamp with the ledgers for
an hour, which means I'm deaf and stupid — so standard wedge:
Cow's on the transom covering the door and the window behind
me. Anything crosses either, I feel it before it's six steps
in. Now leave me alone with the beautiful arithmetic."

Verbally: *"The clerk watches the absorbed scholar and
calculates his moment. He never learns how the man's head
came up, mild and unhurried, at the exact half-second the
floorboard was going to creak."*
