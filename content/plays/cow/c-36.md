An ordinary toy cow, bought to be mistaken: the duplicate
that absorbs risks the real one must never take.

### When to use it

- Whenever Mr. Big's *presence* is expected but Cow's
  exposure is unjustified: pattern-setting at checkpoints
  (play 35), theft-bait, and every day the sheet's greatest
  fear (J-100) gets a cheaper target.

### Mechanics

1. The purchase: one fist-sized toy cow, aged to match
   (scuffing a toy is an evening's work with the
   calligrapher's kit doing the paint) — cost, a few
   copper; value, everything the original doesn't lose.
2. Theft-bait is the sharp use: anyone hunting Cow (the
   sheet's stalkers, J-100's ladder) who scouts Joe sees
   *a toy cow in his pack* — the decoy rides visible while
   Cow travels parked or perched elsewhere. A stolen decoy
   is an alarm, a confession, and a tail-thread (J-138
   follows the thief home) that costs nothing.
3. Pattern work: the decoy establishes "the scholar's
   sentimental toy" at checkpoints, inns, and courts —
   searched, handled, remembered — so the real one, on the
   day it must ride, inherits a vetted identity.
4. The discipline: the decoy is *never* treated as Cow
   (no talking to it, no bond-habits at it — hosts notice
   how people handle objects), and Joe's real tell —
   affection — gets performed at the decoy deliberately,
   as cover.

### At the table

Mechanically: "The broker's been asking about 'the toy,'
so the toy he'll find is this one: aged, scuffed,
two-copper honest stuffing, riding visible in my pack.
Cow's parked. If the decoy vanishes from our room
tonight, we've confirmed the hunt, protected the real
one, and bought a thief to follow home — cheap at triple
the price."

Verbally: *"The thief's fingers close on the famous toy
at last — stuffing, seams, and a paint job one evening
old. Somewhere else entirely, in a fold of the world
shaped like being safe, the real Mr. Big dreams on,
untouched — while the thief walks his stolen nothing
straight home, unspooling the whole conspiracy behind
him like thread."*
