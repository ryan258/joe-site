Retrieval by air: the tiny evidence that must leave a place
Cow can enter and the party can't.

### When to use it

- The seal, the letter, the sample, the wax impression —
  objects of ounces inside guarded rooms with owl-sized
  entries (transoms, chimneys, the window that doesn't
  close).

### Mechanics

1. The target audit before launch: weight (ounces —
   play 41's ruling), grip (talon-compatible: paper,
   rings, cloth scraps yes; flat coins off polished wood
   are comedy), and *missability* (the lift's whole
   craft — take the thing whose absence lags discovery:
   one letter from a stack's middle beats the top one;
   the impression beats the seal itself, play 3 of the
   burglar's arithmetic).
2. The wax-impression protocol is the signature move:
   Cow can't press wax — so the lift takes the *seal*
   to a window ledge, Joe's hand does the pressing
   (link-range positioning, play 21), and Cow returns
   the seal inside the hour. Nothing is missing;
   everything is copied (J-84's forgery bench now has
   its stamp).
3. Interior navigation runs the recon stack first
   (plays 6, 13, 23): the lift flies a *known* room —
   blind lifts are how 1 HP meets a startled clerk.
4. Chain with Mage Hand at the boundary (J-17): the
   hand's 10 pounds and 30 feet hands off to the wing's
   ounces and 60-per-round — the relay (play 51) moves
   objects neither could move alone.

### At the table

Mechanically: "The signet sits on his desk while he
dines — Cow's through the transom, seal to the window
ledge, my hand does the wax, seal goes back before the
cheese course. Nothing's missing tomorrow; we're just
suddenly able to write his mail. The letters
themselves stay — absence is louder than copies."

Verbally: *"The baron's study is locked, watched, and
proud of it. Above the door, a transom no wider than a
prayer book breathes in something small — and the
baron's own authority walks out a window, presses
itself into wax on a ledge, and is home before his
wine is poured."*
