The recall as tactical insertion: Cow re-entering the world
exactly where the fight now needs it.

### When to use it

- Every un-park (play 61's second half), and any moment
  Cow's position is wrong by more than a flight's worth:
  the recall *is* a 30-foot-radius teleport with Joe as
  the origin.

### Mechanics

1. The geometry (J-89, sharpened for combat): recall
   places Cow at **any unoccupied space within 30 feet
   of Joe** — behind the wall Joe's touching, atop the
   pillar beside him, through the doorway he's holding:
   placements no flight path reaches without exposure,
   reached with none.
2. The insertion menu, by need: the Help flyby's
   launch perch pre-positioned over the target (play
   53 with the approach leg deleted — recall high,
   dive once); the wedge re-established after Joe
   repositions (play 18); the census eye dropped into
   the smoke's edge (play 57); the courier staged for
   the vial run's shortest line (play 43).
3. The action economics stated honestly: park + recall
   is **two of Joe's Magic actions** across two-plus
   turns — a real price (two cantrips' worth), paid
   for teleporting the party's sensor across a
   battlefield that would otherwise get a shot at it.
   Worth it exactly when flying the same route isn't
   survivable (the audit, always the audit).
4. Joe's own movement compounds it: the recall radius
   travels with him — move 30, recall at the far edge:
   a 60-foot repositioning of the bird in one turn,
   with Joe's body doing half the work safely on the
   ground.

### At the table

Mechanically: "Cow's been parked since the robe fell —
now I want eyes on the stable roof and the flight
crosses their whole archer line. Instead: I move to
the stable wall — thirty feet — and recall Cow
straight onto the roof edge above me. It never
crossed their sky. Watching resumes, and their
archers are still waiting for a bird to shoot."

Verbally: *"The archers watched the whole sky for
the return of the little spy — a professional,
patient, arrow-nocked vigil. The spy returned
anyway: not across the sky but simply *onto* the
roof, unfolded out of a pocket in the world by the
scholar leaning, quite casually, against the stable
wall below."*
