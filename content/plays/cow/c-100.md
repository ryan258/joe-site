The toy itself: seams, stuffing, and stitches — the physical
care of the spirit's house, and everything it protects.

### When to use it

- Regularly, in downtime — and immediately after any
  rough handling (searches, plays 27–31's cons, the
  child-loans of play 28): the campaign's most
  important object gets the care its job demands.

### Mechanics

1. The operational case, first: cover integrity is
   *physical* — a toy is convincing because it is a
   toy (worn seams, honest stuffing, a repaired ear
   with forty years of story in it, play 36's aging
   craft studied from the original); a *damaged*
   toy invites the two worst things in this file's
   world: repair by stranger hands, and disposal.
   The maintenance kit (needle, thread, matched
   patches — J-148's list, one more line) rides
   with the calligrapher's supplies, and Joe's
   precise hands do every stitch.
2. The inspection cadence: after every con
   deployment (plays 26–40 — what did strange
   hands do?); monthly otherwise (seams, stuffing,
   the button eye's anchor — logged like
   everything, because a *change Joe didn't make*
   is play 99's gravest possible entry: someone
   examined the toy, or worse, put something in
   it — sweep it, J-29, on any anomaly).
3. The fiction's deep layer, handled with the
   sheet's own care: Mr. Big is the expression of
   safety the spirit *chose* (the backstory's
   exact architecture — the bond, the toy, the
   Keeper, as one made thing) — its maintenance
   is not upkeep of equipment; it is the
   friendship's most literal act, and the DM
   should see it played that way (quietly, well,
   with the good thread).
4. And the last mechanics are the first ones (the
   file closes where the sheet begins): the toy's
   history is *unknown* — who made it, where it
   came from (Joe's whole backstory-hunt, plays
   87–91's campaign thread) — which means every
   maintenance session is also *examination* (+10
   on the maker's marks, the old repairs, the
   stitching's origin — J-76 on the case's
   oldest evidence), and the answer to the
   campaign's deepest question may one day
   surface under its Keeper's own needle,
   in a seam he has mended forty times and
   finally, at last, reads.

### At the table

Mechanically: "Quiet evening, so: maintenance.
The pawnshop week loosened the left ear —
restitched with the matched thread. Full seam
inspection... no changes I didn't make, logged.
And the maker's-mark question stays open: that
under-seam stitch pattern matches nothing in
three provinces' toy-craft, which I note again,
monthly, like a man knocking patiently on a
very old door. One day it answers."

Verbally: *"Last light: the party sleeps, the
Alarm hums its silent perimeter, and the
scholar sits cross-legged by the embers with a
needle, a thimble, and a toy cow older than
anyone knows — mending one worn ear with
surgeon's stitches, examining, as he always
does, the seam that matches no craft in any
archive — and saying, with every small pass of
the good thread, the only thing he has ever
really needed it to hear: *you are kept.*"*
