Cow as credential and counselor among the Fair Folk: the
etiquette layer for encounters with its own kind.

### When to use it

- Fey encounters of every weight — the crossroads
  bargainer, the court emissary, the wild thing in the
  hollow — where mortal manners are the hazard and the
  party's Fey passenger is the native speaker.

### Mechanics

1. The counselor function runs before the credential:
   *ask Cow first* (play 87's protocol, urgently) —
   what this kind of being values, what offends, what
   the offered bargain's shape usually hides — the
   sonar (play 17) reading a Fey counterpart is
   reading its *own language*, and the pre-briefing
   beats any Arcana check the table owns (J-77
   corroborates; it doesn't lead, here).
2. The credential function is real and double-edged:
   a mortal *keeping* a Fey spirit — by the spirit's
   own visible consent and comfort — is a
   testimonial no forgery matches (doors open;
   parley is offered where mortals alone get
   riddles)... and an advertisement (the sheet's
   stalkers include Fey interests; every court
   appearance is logged in play 86's ledger and
   weighed against J-100's counter-file).
3. The hard rails, pre-committed: Joe speaks for the
   party (the precise man, J-85's registers — Fey
   bargains punish loose words); nothing is
   accepted or promised without the beat of
   consultation (*Cow's unease at a gift is a
   veto*); and Cow is never, under any pressure,
   offered, wagered, named-in-full, or discussed
   in trade terms — the Keeper's line, absolute
   (J-100), and every Fey in the room will test
   it precisely once.
4. The relationship product compounds beyond the
   scene: Fey courts remember manners (the party
   that behaved becomes *the party that behaved* —
   a reputation worth roads, warnings, and
   winters), and Cow's standing among its kind is
   an asset Joe tends like play 143's garden —
   with the same patience and the same refusal to
   ever spend the gardener.

### At the table

Mechanically: "A crossroads lady, at dusk, offering
directions — so, protocol: nobody speaks but me,
nobody accepts anything, and first I take the
beat: Cow reads her as *old, amused, honest-
tonight*. The counsel-fragment says her kind trade
in names and keep bargains to the letter. So we
buy directions with the poem Tam knows, not with
thanks, not with names — and we leave her smiling."

Verbally: *"The lady at the crossroads has eaten
the manners of forty travelers. This party's
scholar bows at the correct depth, offers a poem
instead of a name, and never once glances at his
satchel — where something she recognized before
they rounded the bend sits very comfortably,
vouching for him in a language older than the
road."*
