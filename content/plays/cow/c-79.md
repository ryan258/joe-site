Cow performing: the deliberate charm offensive of expressive
movement, deployed at rooms that need melting.

### When to use it

- Cold audiences, tense negotiations' preambles, wary
  villages, children en masse — anywhere the party's
  human charm (J-19's warm-ups) wants a partner act.

### Mechanics

1. The sheet's own channel: Cow communicates with
   others "through expressive movement and behavior" —
   which, pointed at delight, is a *performance*: the
   head-tilt repertoire, the solemn bow, the owl
   that plays peekaboo from Joe's satchel.
2. The disarming logic is real social mechanics: a
   man with a charming animal reads as *safe* — Joe
   becomes "the scholar with the wonderful owl," a
   persona doors open for that stay shut to "the
   wizard," and the party's Persuasion inherits the
   room's warmth (Advantage-bait, J-19's argument,
   at troupe scale).
3. The reveal ladder is deliberate: toy-mode
   audiences get nothing (cover is cover); trusted
   rooms get the *animated toy* (a familiar is unremarkable
   for a wizard — Mr. Big performs, the toy stays
   secret); inner-circle rooms alone ever learn the
   two are one (the sheet's secret is J-100's crown
   jewel; the act protects it by giving curiosity
   something satisfying to find).
4. The line Cow doesn't cross: dignity is the
   spirit's own (the sheet gives Cow selfhood — the
   act is offered, never commanded), and Joe's asks
   respect it — the Keeper's whole ethic, performed
   in miniature.

### At the table

Mechanically: "This village hasn't smiled at a
stranger in a decade — so before any questions:
Cow does the act from my satchel. Peekaboo,
head-tilts, the solemn bow to the eldest. ...Now
the children are laughing and their parents are
watching *them* instead of us. I'd argue the
Persuasion that follows carries Advantage — this
room is twenty degrees warmer than the one we
walked into."

Verbally: *"The village met armed strangers at
its well and reached for its silence. It was not
prepared for the owl — who bows to the matriarch
like a courtier, plays hide-and-seek with the
miller's twins, and single-wingedly negotiates
the treaty that opens every door on the lane."*
