Mr. Big passed between party members: the mobile sensor that
covers whoever needs covering.

### When to use it

- Split operations where Joe isn't the one going into the
  interesting room: the sensor should ride with the mission,
  not the owner.

### Mechanics

1. The mechanics that make it work: the *link* is
   Joe-and-Cow at 100 feet — the *carry* can be anyone.
   Senna's satchel with Mr. Big in it is a wired operative
   whenever her path passes within 100 feet of wherever Joe
   loiters (play 22's hinge positioning, with the sensor
   walking).
2. The carried brief: Cow watches the carrier's
   surroundings, not the carrier — rear coverage for their
   negotiation (play 18's wedge, delegated), the room they
   enter (play 20 from inside a bag's gap), the feel of
   their counterpart (play 17 at handshake range).
3. Out-of-range carries fall back to fragments (play 25)
   with the carrier's own extraction as Cow's ride home —
   the brief includes the carrier's abort signals, because
   Cow may be the only witness the carrier gets.
4. The rail scales with the sheet (J-100): handing off Mr.
   Big is handing off *Cow's safety* — carriers are
   briefed (never abandon the bag, never let it be taken,
   the pocket-dimension can't be triggered by them), and
   the roster of trusted hands is short and earned.

### At the table

Mechanically: "Senna takes the meeting; I can't be seen
there. So she takes Mr. Big — satchel, gap open, and I'll
hold the fountain square, which keeps her inside my link
for the whole exchange. Cow reads her buyer at
handshake range and watches the door behind her. Senna:
the bag stays on your shoulder, whatever happens.
That's not negotiable."

Verbally: *"The buyer studies the thief across the
table and finds her unreadable. The thief's shabby
satchel, gapped an inch toward the door, reads the
buyer, the bodyguard, the exit, and the feeling of the
trap forming — and a fountain square away, a quiet man
takes dictation from the dark."*
