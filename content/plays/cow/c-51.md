Mage Hand to talon to hand: the object-movement chain that
crosses spaces neither tool crosses alone.

### When to use it

- The object is deep inside (past Mage Hand's 30 feet
  from Joe) and must travel far (past a single flight's
  safe exposure) — vault interiors, deep cells, the far
  side of wide danger.

### Mechanics

1. The chain's links, by capability: **Mage Hand**
   (J-16/17) works within 30 feet of Joe at 10 pounds —
   the *heavy, close* leg; **Cow** flies 60 per round
   at ounces — the *light, far* leg; **the party's
   hands** anchor both ends. The relay design matches
   each leg to its tool.
2. The classic sequence: Mage Hand lifts the object
   off the trapped pedestal and floats it to the
   window (30 feet, Joe outside the wall); Cow takes
   the handoff on the sill and flies the courtyard
   (300 feet in five rounds); Senna's hand receives at
   the drop point. No person entered; the object
   crossed a building.
3. The handoff craft: sills, ledges, and floors — the
   hand *sets down*, the talon *picks up* (a mid-air
   pass is a fumble looking for its moment); each
   handoff point pre-scouted (play 6) and inside the
   relevant ranges, drawn on the plan like the relay
   it is.
4. Weight is the binding constraint, audited first:
   the chain carries what its *lightest* leg carries —
   ounces, if Cow flies a leg. Heavier prizes re-plan
   around Mage Hand's 10 pounds and shorter flights
   (or the party walks in after all; the relay is a
   tool, not a religion).

### At the table

Mechanically: "The deed's on the pedestal, the
pedestal's plate-trapped, and the room's barred to
bodies. Relay: Mage Hand from the doorway lifts the
paper straight up — no plate — and floats it to the
transom sill. Cow takes the sill handoff, flies the
two courtyards, delivers to Senna at the wall.
Paper weighs nothing; nobody stepped inside; the
trap dies of loneliness."

Verbally: *"The vault's designers planned for hands,
boots, ropes, and greed. The deed leaves by
spectral palm to a windowsill, then under small
wings across two courtyards of moonlight — stolen
by a committee of things that weigh, between them,
nothing at all."*
