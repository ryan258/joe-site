If Joe falls: Cow's orders for the party's worst morning.

### When to use it

- Pre-loaded always (play 96's second order), executed
  never, ideally — the contingency for Joe dead,
  taken, or lost beyond the bond's reach.

### Mechanics

1. The orders, in sequence (image-anchored, drilled):
   **first, witness** — if Joe is *taken*, Cow
   follows high and far (play 14's trail eye on the
   worst quarry), marks the destination, and only
   then — **second, the party**: direct flight to
   the survivors or the rally, the arrival-alone
   signal (play 75) telling them everything its
   pattern can carry — *taken-and-where* versus
   *fallen* (the party's response forks entirely on
   that bit; the pattern grammar earns its drills
   here).
2. **Third, the guidance**: Cow leads the recovery
   (play 84's follow-me, at campaign stakes) — the
   party's tracker is the one member who *watched
   the abduction* and flies point back to the
   marked door.
3. The dark branch, written because it must be: if
   Joe is *dead*, the familiar bond's mechanics
   end — the sheet's fiction doesn't (the spirit
   and the toy remain; the friendship remains).
   The protocol's last order is the party's, not
   Cow's: **Mr. Big goes to the party's most
   trusted hands** (the roster from play 40),
   who keep the toy safe and the spirit free,
   per the only promise the Keeper ever asked
   of them — and the counter-file (J-100)
   passes to them with it, because the hunters
   don't stop for grief.
4. And the protocol is *told to the party in
   advance* — all of it, including the dark
   branch — because a contingency the survivors
   must improvise is not a contingency; it's a
   second disaster. One conversation, once, in
   safety: the Keeper's version of a will.

### At the table

Mechanically: "Since we're all provisioned and
nobody's bleeding — the conversation, once: if
I'm taken, Cow witnesses first, then comes to
you: direct-agitated means follow it to me;
if I'm dead, the toy goes to Senna, the
counter-file goes with it, and the promise is
the one I asked at the start — keep him safe,
ask him nothing. All right. Never again;
whose watch is it?"

Verbally: *"The party learns, one quiet
evening, that the scholar has already planned
his own worst morning — the witnessing, the
signal, the flight home, and the last small
bequest of a toy into named hands — planned
it the way he plans everything: thoroughly,
early, and so that the people he loves would
have, on the day of the fire, nothing to
decide and everything to do."*
