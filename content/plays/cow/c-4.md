The stationary overwatch: Cow parked at altitude over a place, a
person, or an approach, for as long as the question needs.

### When to use it

- Watching a door through a night, a road through an afternoon, a
  courtyard through a negotiation — anywhere a fixed eye beats a
  moving one.

### Mechanics

1. Perch selection is the craft: sightline to the target,
   background that hides an owl silhouette (foliage, gables,
   bell-tower louvres), inside 100 feet of where Joe can loiter if
   live coverage matters, and a clean exit line (Flyby covers the
   departure if it comes to that).
2. Owls are *invisible in plain sight* in a way spells aren't: no
   aura for *Detect Magic* to sweep casually (a familiar detects
   as a creature, not an item — but nobody sweeps the sky),
   no Concentration for Joe to hold, no duration.
3. The rhythm: Cow watches continuously; Joe dips in on the
   Bonus-Action look (play 19) at trigger moments the link flags —
   an emotion-ping of *arrival, movement, wrongness* is the
   doorbell, the borrowed eyes are answering it.
4. Relief schedule for long posts: even a Fey patience serves
   better in shifts — pocket-dimension rest (play 61's logic,
   J-89) between watch blocks on multi-day posts (play 39).

### At the table

Mechanically: "Cow takes the bell-tower louvre — hundred-foot
sightline to the warehouse door, dark background, and I can hold
the tavern corner inside link range. Continuous watch; I look
through on any arrival-ping. We'll have every face that touches
that door by morning."

Verbally: *"The warehouse is watched by nobody, the fence
confirms it twice. Above him, in the bell-tower's dark, two
enormous golden eyes have already memorized his knock."*
