Interior reconnaissance without entry: every unshuttered window is
a page, and Cow reads the whole street.

### When to use it

- Casing a building the party can't lawfully or quietly enter;
  confirming a floor plan; finding *which* room matters before a
  job prices itself.

### Mechanics

1. The pass: Cow flies the building's faces at window height in
   one smooth transit per face (hovering at glass is how birds get
   remembered — Flyby lets each look be a *pass*, not a perch),
   then perches (play 4) only on the face that earned it.
2. Each window is an image-frame: *lit or dark, occupied, what
   kind of room, what's on the desk*. Joe assembles the floor plan
   from frames (+10 Investigation turns window-glimpses into
   architecture, J-62's intelligence for *Dimension Door*).
3. Glass and light discipline: interiors lit brighter than the
   night outside read clearly; dark interiors need the darkvision
   pass; and a room lit *late* is a room worth a second frame.
4. Upper floors are the honest ones — nobody stages the third
   storey for visitors. Start at the top.

### At the table

Mechanically: "Four faces, one pass each at sill height, top
floor first. I want the frame series: lit, dark, occupied, and
anything desk-shaped. ...The third-floor corner room is lit at
two bells past midnight with a man burning papers. That's the
room, and now Dimension Door knows its dimensions."

Verbally: *"The counting-house presents its honest face to the
street, as counting-houses do. Its windows, interviewed one by
one by a passing shadow, tell a different set of books."*
