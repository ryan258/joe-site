The last check before a door gets opened: thirty seconds of
Fey attention through the gap.

### When to use it

- Every breach, every "it's probably empty," every door the
  party is about to trust — the habit that runs before the
  fighter's shoulder does.

### Mechanics

1. The sequence: Cow to the door's gap, sill, or keyhole
   (Tiny, silent, and a toy if seen — the approach is nearly
   free); Perception +5 works the gap;
   the emotional sonar (play 17) reads what hearing can't —
   *occupancy has a feeling* before it has a sound.
2. The product is a three-state call, codebook-clean: *still
   water* (empty as far as feeling reaches), *held breath*
   (occupied and quiet — the bad one), *hearth-warm* (occupied
   and oblivious). The party stacks differently for each
   (J-136's door doctrine takes the input).
3. Honesty rail: the feel penetrates a door's gap, not a
   dungeon — constructs feel like furniture, the undead feel
   like *wrong* furniture (DM's country, and a "no reading"
   is itself briefable), and thirty seconds of silence is not
   a guarantee. The check downgrades surprises; it doesn't
   abolish them.
4. Pair with the Mage Hand open (J-16) for the full
   no-body-in-the-doorway breach: Cow feels, the hand opens,
   the party enters a room that has already been interviewed.

### At the table

Mechanically: "Hold — door drill. Cow's at the gap...
held-breath. Occupied and quiet, which means they heard us.
So: nobody walks in — Mage Hand opens it from the hinge side
at thirty feet, and whatever's holding its breath in there
can introduce itself to an empty doorway."

Verbally: *"The ambusher waits behind the door, knife raised,
breath stopped — perfectly silent, and radiating silence the
way a stove radiates heat, to the one listener in the
hallway who reads that language natively."*
