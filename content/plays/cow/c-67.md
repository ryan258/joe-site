The complete camp security architecture: Alarm, Cow, and the
watch schedule, interlocking. (J-86 introduced the pairing;
this is the full design.)

### When to use it

- Every rest in unsecured country — the system, not the
  improvisation.

### Mechanics

1. The three layers, by failure mode: **Alarm** (J-31)
   is the tripwire — perfect within its cube, blind
   outside it, unbluffable, never tired; **Cow** is the
   judgment — Darkvision 120 and Perception +5 (Passive 15) across
   *all* approaches, capable of telling the deer from
   the scout (the layer that prevents false-alarm
   fatigue, which is the real killer of watch systems);
   **the human watch** is the response — awake, armed,
   and pre-briefed on both other layers' signals.
2. Coverage geometry: Alarm takes the *likeliest*
   approach (the trail — 20-foot cube, chosen like a
   Fireball center), Cow roosts covering the *rest*
   (high, 360 degrees, inside 100 feet of Joe's
   bedroll — the ping wakes him, J-86's confirmed
   ruling), the human watch covers the *camp itself*
   (the inward threats: the prisoner, the fire, the
   sleepwalking curse the DM is saving).
3. The escalation ladder, briefed at supper: Cow's
   *deer-ping* (fauna — nobody wakes), *rain-smell*
   (movement — the watch stands up, Joe half-wakes),
   *fire* (contact — everyone, now). Three tiers
   means the camp sleeps through what it can and
   never through what it can't.
4. The system self-tests: the first night's false
   alarms (owls know other owls; deer exist)
   calibrate tiers, not tempers — by night three the
   architecture is silent, layered, and the reason
   J-147's road doctrine promises the ambush "finds
   a wizard with every slot he owns."
