Cow over the warded evidence: the judgment layer on J-32's
tripwire, and the face the Alarm can't give you.

### When to use it

- Baited scenes and warded caches (J-32's play): the
  Alarm says *when* someone returns to the evidence;
  Cow says *who* — and the who is the case.

### Mechanics

1. The pairing's division of labor: the Alarm's mental
   ping (1-mile range) reaches Joe wherever he waits;
   Cow — perched with sightline to the warded room's
   entrance (play 4's craft) — converts the ping into
   a *face*, live via link when Joe's within 100 feet
   of Cow, by fragment when not (play 25).
2. The timing choreography: ping arrives → Joe
   uses a Bonus-Action look through Cow (play 19) inside the
   crucial first minute → the visitor is seen
   *entering*, which places them at the scene in a
   way no later interview un-says (J-142's testimony
   loves a witnessed entry).
3. The follow decision forks on the face: a known
   face gets logged and left (the case builds); an
   unknown face gets the trail eye (play 14 —
   Cow follows them home, and the tamper becomes an
   address); a *dangerous* face gets nothing risked —
   log, stay perched, let the ward's knowledge be
   the whole win (the audit, always).
4. The double-blind version for serious cases: the
   *decoy* evidence in the warded room, the real
   evidence already moved (play 36's logic applied
   to documents) — the tamperer reveals themselves
   *and* leaves with nothing, and their choice of
   what to take is itself testimony (+10 reads the
   selection, J-76).

### At the table

Mechanically: "The ledger's warded and Cow holds the
skylight across the hall — standard tamper watch. If
the Alarm pings tonight, I look through Cow inside
the minute: face on entry, that's court-grade. Known
face, we log it. Stranger, Cow follows them home.
And the ledger they'll take is the copy — the
selection they make tells us what they feared it
proved."

Verbally: *"At two bells the archive door eases
open, and the clerk — certain, checked, and
gloved — retrieves the damning ledger at last.
Above the hall, patient in the skylight's dark,
the case's final witness memorizes his face,
his gloves, his choice — and lets him carry
the worthless copy home."*
