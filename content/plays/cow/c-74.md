Watching the cache: short-term custody of the things the
party buries, hides, and comes back for.

### When to use it

- Caches with a return date: the loot that can't come
  along, the supply drop for the way back, the backup
  spellbook's interim site (J-145's storage doctrine,
  bridged) — value parked in ground that can't be
  trusted alone.

### Mechanics

1. The honest scope first: Cow guards caches on
   *operation timescales* (hours to a few days —
   play 39's tenure logic), not as permanent
   installation — the bird is the party's sensor,
   not a landmark's; long-term storage belongs to
   institutions (J-145's bank) and this play
   bridges *to* them.
2. The watch is concealment-verification plus
   custody: first, Cow observes the *burial itself*
   from altitude (did anyone else watch? the cache
   compromised at creation is the classic — play
   69's shape-of-surveillance eye, pointed at the
   party's own tradecraft); then the tiered watch
   (play 67) holds until the return or the relief.
3. The rifled-cache conversion (play 70's clause,
   promoted): a stranger at the cache is *followed
   home, not fought* (play 58) — the cache's
   contents were priced for loss the moment they
   were parked; the rifler's address is worth more
   than most of what caches hold.
4. The retrieval protocol closes the loop: Cow's
   *still water* on approach is the party's
   all-clear (a cache is the perfect ambush bait —
   against the *party* — and the sweep-before-
   approach, play 60's rally clearance, runs here
   every time, no exceptions on tired days,
   which is when it matters).

### At the table

Mechanically: "The strongbox waits in the tree
line — we can't haul it through what's next. Cow
watched the burial: nobody else did, good. Now
it holds the watch till we're back — two days,
tiers loaded, and if anyone digs, follow them
home, don't contest it. And when we return, we
wait on still-water before anyone walks up to
that tree. Caches make lovely ambushes."

Verbally: *"The strongbox sleeps under leaf
mold and patience, watched by the only guard
that leaves no tracks around what it guards.
Two days later the party returns — pausing,
tired as they are, at the treeline, until the
sky sends down one small feeling shaped like
*still water* — and digs up exactly what it
buried, unwatched, unfollowed, and unpriced."*
