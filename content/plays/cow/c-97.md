Cow's riding states on the road: the daily disposition
matrix, from wing to pack to pocket.

### When to use it

- Every travel day (J-147's doctrine gains its Cow
  appendix): the default posture question — *where is
  the bird right now?* — answered by rule instead of
  by mood.

### Mechanics

1. The four states, priced: **wing** (patrol
   circuits, play 15/J-147 — maximum coverage,
   maximum exposure: quiet country only);
   **shoulder/pack** (toy-mode or perched — sonar
   and codebook live, near-zero exposure: the
   default in settled lands); **pocket dimension**
   (zero everything — transit through danger,
   searches, weather, J-89); **detached** (plays
   4, 24, 39 — the bird on assignment, the party
   on advance briefs: mission-only).
2. The transition triggers, standing: settlements
   ahead → pack (strangers' eyes, play 36's
   decoy logic); weather turning → pocket (the
   storm outranks doctrine, play 66); contact
   likely → the constitution's call (play 94,
   usually pack-with-sonar); night camps → wing
   for the survey then the roost (plays 5, 67).
3. The rhythm serves the *bird* too (the play 95
   principle on the road): wing-hours are also
   just *flying* — a spirit that loves the sky
   gets the sky when the country's kind (DMs:
   the fiction matters; the sheet's bond is a
   friendship) — and pocket-hours are rest, not
   storage (the language the party uses shapes
   the table's whole read of what Cow is).
4. The one-word status call (play 66's habit,
   dailified): Joe announces state changes —
   "Cow's to pack," "wings up" — so the party's
   plays that assume the bird (or its absence)
   re-price in real time, all day, at zero cost.

### At the table

Mechanically: "Road day: open country till noon,
so wings up — hourly circuits, weather eye. The
border village after that: pack state before
we're in sight of the walls, decoy visible.
Storm's building west — if it breaks, pocket
till it passes. Same matrix as always; I'll
call the changes."

Verbally: *"The party's sixth member commutes
strangely: mornings as a patrol of one, high
over the road's shoulders; afternoons as a
toy in a satchel, reading the world through
feelings; storms folded harmlessly into a
pocket of elsewhere — and the whole
arrangement so practiced that the wizard's
soft 'wings up' is the only evidence a
decision was ever made."*
