Guarding the fallen and the helpless: Cow over the
stabilized ally, the sleeping wounded, the tied captive.

### When to use it

- Someone can't watch themselves — the unconscious
  ally dragged to the back room, the wounded left at
  the rally point, the prisoner who must stay put —
  and every able body is needed forward.

### Mechanics

1. The station: perched *over* the body — sightlines
  to every approach, inside link range of Joe when
  the geometry allows (the ping is the point:
  *rain-smell at the rally point* re-routes the
  whole party mid-fight), on the advance brief
  (play 25) when it doesn't.
2. What the watch actually buys: **time and truth**
   — Cow can't fight the approaching finisher
   (never could, never will), but the two-round
   warning converts "we found his body after" into
   J-134's protocol running *before* the enemy
   arrives; and for the captive, the watch is
   testimony (who approached, who freed, who
   silenced — the tampered-prisoner case solves
   itself, play 71's preview).
3. The comfort function is real and the sheet's own:
   an unconscious ally coming-to alone in a dark
   room wakes to warm feathers and the toy they
   know — a Fey spirit's presence, per Joe's whole
   backstory, is a *kindness* first; the morale
   line item is not nothing (play 78's territory).
4. The layered version adds the ritual: *Alarm*
   around the back room (J-31) plus the body watch
   — mechanical tripwire and living judgment, the
   same pairing as J-86, pointed inward at the
   party's most fragile asset.

### At the table

Mechanically: "Brenna's stable but out, and we
can't spare a body — so she gets the bird: Cow
perches the rafter over her, Alarm's on the
doorway, and I'm inside link range for the whole
push. Anything approaches that room, I know
before it's through the door, and the whole
plan turns on its heel."

Verbally: *"The fighter wakes in a dark
storeroom, alone, hurting, unarmed — and finds
two calm golden eyes and a shabby toy cow
keeping watch from the rafter, radiating,
in some language older than worry, the exact
sentence she needed: *you were never
unguarded.*"*
