The dead-man's switch: Cow arriving *alone* as the
pre-agreed alarm.

### When to use it

- Every solo or small-team excursion of Joe's — the
  meeting alone (J-85's one-shots), the infiltration
  (play 35's doors), the arrest survived (play 37) —
  the standing arrangement that makes Joe's silence
  loud.

### Mechanics

1. The arrangement, briefed once and standing: **Cow
   arriving at the party without Joe is itself the
   message** — pre-loaded meanings by flight pattern
   (play 52's grammar in its gravest key): *direct
   arrival, agitated* = Joe taken, follow Cow to him
   now; *circles first* = Joe compromised but
   playing along — shadow, don't storm; *the toy
   carried in claws* = worst case, execute J-100's
   counter-file with everything.
2. The trigger is Joe's own codebook *fire* — or the
   bond's raw event horizon (the link's end-state
   the DM adjudicates: unconsciousness, the null
   zone's boundary, distance) — meaning the switch
   fires even when Joe *can't* fire it, which is
   the entire point of dead-man design.
3. Cow's navigation home runs on the advance brief
   (play 25): the party's location as remembered
   landmarks, updated at each camp (the daily
   ritual: "home is the mill tonight" — one image,
   every evening, J-147's routine owns the slot).
4. The party's receiving discipline: the signal is
   *acted on as briefed, immediately* — the whole
   architecture's value is the pre-commitment
   (debating the meaning of the agitated bird is
   how dead-man switches die); the pattern was
   agreed in daylight so the night needs no
   parliament.

### At the table

Mechanically: "I take this meeting alone —
standing arrangement's live: if Cow arrives
without me, direct-and-agitated means come
now and follow the bird; circles mean I'm
playing a hand, shadow me only. Home is the
boathouse tonight — Cow has the image. If you
see wings, don't discuss it. Move."

Verbally: *"The meeting goes wrong in the
soundless, doorless way the worst ones do —
and three streets away, the boathouse's
evening quiet breaks around six ounces of
urgent feathers arriving *alone*: the one
sentence in the party's whole language that
has never once needed a second reading."*
