Mr. Big, loaned into a household through its smallest, least
suspected member.

### When to use it

- Domestic targets: the suspect's home, the household whose
  staff sees everything — places adults are searched and
  children are furniture. **The hard rail before all else**:
  the child is never endangered, never used as a shield, and
  never lied *about* — the sheet's bait-rule (J-100) covers
  children first of anyone.

### Mechanics

1. The mechanism is kindness with a second function: Joe
   (established as the gentle scholar, J-19's icebreakers)
   lends the toy to a child of the household — lends, with
   ceremony ("Mr. Big is very old; he must come home to me") —
   and the child carries the sensor everywhere adults talk
   over small heads.
2. Coverage is the child's day: kitchens, parlors, the cart to
   market — the household's honest layer, which is the layer
   investigations actually need (staff gossip beats master's
   theater, play 20's principle domesticated).
3. Cow's brief leans on the sheet's nature: a Fey spirit in a
   toy, carried by a child, *guarding* that child — the watch
   and the ward are the same act (emotional sonar flags the
   household's angers before they reach the nursery; that
   ping outranks the case, always, by standing order).
4. Recovery honors the ceremony: the loan ends, the toy comes
   home, the child gets a real (ordinary) toy in thanks — and
   Joe debriefs fragments of a household seen from thirty
   inches off the floor.

### At the table

Mechanically: "The steward's daughter takes to Mr. Big
immediately — the loan comes with the full ceremony, and my
standing order to Cow outranks everything: her safety before
the case, always. For four days the toy rides through every
room in that house below the sightline of every adult
secret. Then Mr. Big comes home, and I buy her the best
ordinary cow the toymaker sells."

Verbally: *"The household guards its words around the
scholar, the maid, even the new groom. Nobody in the history
of conspiracies has guarded a syllable around a six-year-old
dragging a shabby toy cow through the kitchen at soup-time."*
