The master risk doctrine: every rule about when Cow flies,
collected into the constitution the whole file obeys.

### When to use it

- Before every deployment, forever — this play is the
  file's spine, and the others are its articles.

### Mechanics

1. **Article one — the asymmetry**: Cow's value is
   almost entirely *sensory and social* (plays
   1–92); its combat ceiling is one Help window
   (play 53). Risk tolerances follow: high for
   information (recon flights in quiet country),
   near-zero for damage-adjacent work (play 63's
   bait wing is once-per-campaign shaped, not
   once-per-fight).
2. **Article two — the vetoes** (play 66's list,
   constitutional now): AoE live or likely; 2+
   idle ranged attackers; reach density; weather;
   Fey-hostile actors. Any one grounds the bird —
   and grounding is *cheap* (toy-mode and
   pack-riding preserve plays 17–19, 26–40, 76:
   most of the file works from Joe's satchel).
3. **Article three — the escape stack**: Flyby
   (the exit that provokes nothing) + 60-foot fly
   + the *fire* abort (play 25, pre-loaded in
   every brief) + the parked round (play 61,
   Joe's action always budgeted for it) — four
   layers between any surprise and the 1 HP, and
   every plan names which layer is primary.
4. **Article four — the ledger governs** (play
   93): stock levels set flight status
   mechanically, not emotionally. And **article
   five — the bond outranks the mission**: the
   sheet's greatest fear is Cow taken, not Cow's
   form broken — any situation that threatens
   *capture* (nets, cages, named hunters,
   spirit-traps) triggers J-100 immediately, and
   the case, the party's plan, and Joe's dignity
   all yield. Written down so that, on the worst
   day, nobody has to decide it fresh.

### At the table

Mechanically: "New players, the standing
constitution on the bird, once: it has one hit
point and it is irreplaceable-adjacent. It
flies for information, never for damage;
five things ground it automatically; it
always has an abort loaded; and if anything
ever tries to *capture* rather than kill it,
every other priority at this table yields.
That's not table talk — that's the character."

Verbally: *"The party's most powerful asset
weighs six ounces and dies to a thrown rock —
and is governed, accordingly, by a
constitution stricter than any kingdom's:
articles of flight and grounding, ledgers and
vetoes, all of it drafted by a careful man
against the one morning he prays never comes,
so that if it comes, his hands already know
what to do."*
