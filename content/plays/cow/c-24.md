Sustained surveillance without burning the operator: the
shift-system for watches measured in hours and days.

### When to use it

- Multi-hour and multi-day posts (play 4's perch, play 39's
  long sit): the stakeout that outlasts anyone's attention
  span — managed so it doesn't have to outlast Joe's.

### Mechanics

1. The three-tier attention model: **Cow watches always**
   (tier one — Fey patience is the resource being spent);
   **the link idles on alert-ping** (tier two — Joe lives his
   hours normally, interruptible); **borrowed eyes on demand**
   (tier three — play 19's framed looks, spent only on
   pings). Joe's actual attention cost per surveillance hour:
   minutes.
2. Joe's body management is part of the system: sleep inside
   link range with the codebook's *fire* reserved for
   wake-worthy events (the sheet says the ping reaches a
   sleeping Keeper through the bond's own urgency — DM
   confirms once); meals, cover identity, and J-147's
   routines all continue.
3. Cow's relief windows: pocket-dimension rests (play 61) at
   the target's dead hours — the 3 a.m. lull is real for
   marks and birds alike — with the Alarm ritual (J-31)
   bridging the gap when the site allows.
4. The log survives the rhythm: every ping and look inked
   with times (J-83) — a three-day watch's value is its
   *pattern*, and patterns live in the journal, not in
   anyone's tired head.

### At the table

Mechanically: "This is a three-day watch, so we run tiers:
Cow owns the warehouse around the clock, I get pings and
spend looks — otherwise I'm the boring lodger across the
street, sleeping in link range with 'fire' reserved for the
big wagon. Cow rests in the pocket during the dead hours
behind an Alarm. Day three, we read the ledger and know
their whole week."

Verbally: *"For three days the warehouse is watched by no
one the fence can see: a lodger who sleeps well, a toy on a
windowsill, and between them a quiet arrangement older than
the street — in which one party never blinks, and the
other writes everything down."*
