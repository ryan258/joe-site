Cow's flight itself as the message: visible signaling for
parties beyond every other channel.

### When to use it

- Coordinating past Message's 120 feet and the link's
  100 (J-23's thunder is loud and costs a slot): the
  split team across the valley, the ship offshore, the
  ally on the far ridge — line-of-sight distances with
  no channel but eyes.

### Mechanics

1. The code is choreography, agreed in advance (play
   16's discipline, made visible): *tight circles over
   a point* = objective here; *figure-eight* = danger,
   abort; *straight line climb* = advance now; *the
   triple stoop* = Joe's compromised, execute the
   contingency. Four signals cover most operations;
   more is a vocabulary nobody recalls under fire.
2. The physics: an owl at 200 feet is visible for a
   half-mile-plus to briefed eyes that know to watch
   the sky (and is *weather* to everyone else — the
   signal hides in plain sight better than any flag,
   fire, or mirror, which enemies also read).
3. Beyond-tether execution (play 25): the flight
   pattern rides the advance brief (*when the wagons
   reach the ford: circles over the mill*) — Cow
   becomes a condition-triggered beacon, and the
   remote team acts on schedule without a word
   crossing the distance.
4. The two-way version needs pre-placed eyes: the
   remote team's *ground* signals (cloak on a rock,
   shutter open/closed) read by Cow's pass and carried
   home as fragments — a full visual dialogue at
   valley scale, deniable end to end.

### At the table

Mechanically: "Tam's team holds the far ridge, out
of every range I own — so the sky talks: when the
column enters the defile, Cow flies tight circles
over the dead pine. That's 'now.' If it's
figure-eights instead, pack up and meet at the
mill. Tam, brief your eyes: watch the sky over the
pine from noon."

Verbally: *"The ambush hinges on a signal no
scout can intercept, no counter-mage can silence,
and no captain would think to forbid: one small
bird, turning patient circles over a dead tree —
weather, to everyone who wasn't told."*
