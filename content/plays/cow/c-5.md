Darkvision 120 makes darkness Cow's shift. The party sleeps; the
map improves.

### When to use it

- Every camp night in unknown country; every city night before a
  job; any survey the daylight version would be seen doing.

### Mechanics

1. Cow's night package is the best in the party by a margin:
   Darkvision 120 feet, Perception +5 (Passive 15), Stealth +5, and a
   sky nobody watches after dark.
2. The survey doubles as security (J-86 runs concurrently): the
   route out, tomorrow's approach, the neighbor camps — flown as
   spokes from the campsite hub, each spoke ending inside link
   range for the report.
3. Darkness limits the *detail* — darkvision is dim-light seeing
   (shades, shapes, movement; not heraldry, not faces) — so night
   surveys answer *where and how many*, and dawn answers *who*
   (play 12's timing).
4. Joe's own blindness in the dark (J-115) makes this the
   compensating habit: the party's no-darkvision wizard operates
   at night on Cow's borrowed eyes and pre-drawn night maps.

### At the table

Mechanically: "While we sleep: spokes off the camp — the ford,
the ridge line, the old road, each out to a half mile and back
through link range. Shapes and counts tonight; faces at first
light. If anything's moving in this valley after dark, I want
its silhouette in my journal by dawn."

Verbally: *"Night shuts the valley like a book. Something small
and patient keeps reading anyway, by a light the darkness
doesn't know it's shedding."*
