The Keeper's ethic as method: how Joe asks Cow for anything,
and why the restraint is the strategy.

### When to use it

- Always — the meta-play governing plays 87–91 and every
  ask in this file: the discipline that keeps the
  archive open, the bond warm, and the campaign's
  deepest resource unburned.

### Mechanics

1. The foundation is the sheet's origin story, read as
   doctrine: the spirit chose Joe *because he treated
   it as a person rather than a source* — meaning the
   bond's entire value was created by restraint, and
   is maintained the same way. Every ask is judged
   against the founding question: *is this treating
   Cow as a person or as a database?*
2. The practical rules that fall out: anchored
   questions, rarely (play 87); incidental
   collection over interrogation (play 91); consent
   before exposure (plays 63, 89's never-wagered
   rail); declines honored *and valued* (the
   heaviness map — what Cow won't discuss is the
   campaign's topology, charted by respect); and
   the asymmetry kept visible — Cow *volunteers*
   more than Joe asks, because volunteering is what
   trust does (DMs: that's the dial).
3. The strategic case, for the table's cynics: the
   coercible version of this resource is *why
   everyone hunts Cow* (the sheet's whole threat
   model) — Joe's restraint is the one extraction
   method the hunters cannot copy, which makes the
   gentle Keeper, in the coldest possible
   arithmetic, the highest-yield operator the
   archive will ever have. The ethic *is* the
   optimization; the sheet built them as one
   piece.
4. And the discipline is legible at the table: the
   DM watching Joe decline to press — log the
   decline, thank the spirit, buy the incense,
   move on — is a DM being shown exactly what
   this bond is, which is how the campaign's
   quietest mechanic becomes its most protected
   one. The best play in this file is the one
   where Joe doesn't.

### At the table

Mechanically: "Could I ask Cow about the sigil
tonight? Yes. Am I going to? No — we asked about
the seal on Tuesday, the decline was clear, and
the sigil's adjacent. It goes in the heaviness
map and we take the archive route instead —
three days slower, and the bond doesn't spend
what three days buys. Incense is stocked, the
question keeps."

Verbally: *"The hunters who want what Cow knows
have racks, chains, wards, and centuries of
technique — and will get, from the little
spirit, precisely nothing. The dry man by the
fire, who has asked it four questions in a
year and thanked it four times, gets the
fifth answer *offered* — late one night,
unprompted, like a gift — because he is the
only creature in the world who never once
reached for it."*
