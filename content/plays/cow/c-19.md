The borrowed-eyes discipline — J-90 wrote the safety protocol;
this is the *craft* of the look itself.

### When to use it

- Every time Joe rides Cow's senses: the Bonus-Action immersions
  that turn a bird's position into a wizard's information.

### Mechanics

1. The mechanics, restated once: Joe's Bonus Action, within 100 feet
   of Cow, senses through Cow until the start of his next turn. Under
   the 2024 rules he is **not** Blinded or Deafened to his own
   surroundings — the old sensory blackout is gone (J-90). The ally
   shoulder-tap and the wall at his back are still good habits, but
   they are coordination, not life support.
2. The craft is *pre-framing*: brief the question before going
   under ("I'm counting boots" / "I'm reading the desk") — an
   unframed look returns vibes; a framed look returns answers.
   The look lasts until the start of Joe's next turn; the framing
   decides what it buys.
3. Position Cow *before* the look, via codebook, not during —
   steering-while-under wastes the window on travel. Perch,
   *then* ride.
4. Extraction habit: surface, speak the finding aloud
   immediately (the party hears it, the journal gets it — and
   J-90's one-round-under rhythm resets), then re-frame the
   next question before going back down. Look, report, re-aim:
   the loop that makes a 1 HP bird into a surveillance
   apparatus.

### At the table

Mechanically: "Cow's perched over their table — framing the
first look: hands and weapons. Going under... surfaced: five
men, two swords, one wand-shaped problem in grey. Next frame:
the papers between them. Tam, shoulder-tap if anything moves
on me. Going back under."

Verbally: *"Joe's eyes empty, politely, like a house between
tenants. Somewhere across the square, behind two golden eyes
a hundred feet up, the same man is reading the conspiracy's
table like a menu."*
