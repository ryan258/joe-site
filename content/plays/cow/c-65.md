Perception +5 against the unseen: Cow as the party's
detection asset when eyes stop working.

### When to use it

- Invisible enemies (J-101's problem), obscured
  fights, the stalker in the fog — whenever the
  question is *where is it* and sight has resigned.

### Mechanics

1. The stat-block case: **Perception +5** (Passive 15) makes Cow a
   strong detection asset. Against the invisible, *hearing* is the
   operative sense; the DM rolls Cow's Perception against the
   stalker's Stealth, like any creature's check.
2. The product is a *location ping*, codebook-terse:
   landmark and movement (*the well, moving left*) —
   which feeds J-101's stack precisely: the party's
   AoE gets its point, the Web gets its square, the
   flour bomb (play 50) gets its release
   coordinates.
3. Station for the hunt: *low and quiet*, not high —
   footfalls, breath, and the brush of cloth carry
   at ground level; the hunter perches the room's
   edge at head height, still as toy-mode, and
   listens the intruder across the floor.
4. The emotional channel stacks (play 17): a
   creature hiding *feels* like held breath to the
   Fey sense — two independent detection layers,
   hearing and feeling, both immune to
   *Invisibility*'s entire premise (the spell fools
   eyes; Cow is barely using those).

### At the table

Mechanically: "It's in this room and none of us
can see it — Cow's already listening: perched low,
still, Advantage on the hearing. ...Ping: the
shelves, moving toward the door. Web across the
doorway — now the exit's silk — and the flour
goes over the shelf row. Everyone hold. It'll
outline itself in about four seconds."

Verbally: *"The assassin's spell is flawless:
no eye in the room can find her. But the room's
smallest occupant closed its eyes minutes ago —
and is reading her across the floorboards by
breath, heartbeat, and the tiny arrogant whisper
of silk on stone, patient as a ledger being
balanced."*
