Short rests in hostile structure: the hour of vulnerability,
guarded by the member who doesn't need the rest.

### When to use it

- Every dungeon short rest (J-57's Arcane Recovery and
  J-56's pivot both *want* that hour): the party at its
  most stationary, in the enemy's house, healing.

### Mechanics

1. The architecture compresses play 67 indoors: the
   Alarm cube takes the approach corridor (11 minutes
   of the rest's hour, free, J-31), Cow takes the
   *other* approach (doorway or breach the cube
   doesn't cover — there is always another approach;
   the roof plan, play 13, said where), the party's
   own watch stands down to *ready* rather than
   *walking* — which is what makes the rest restful.
2. Positioning inside structure: Cow at the corridor
   junction *outside* the rest room — hearing works
   around corners (Perception +5 at the junction covers
   two halls for one perch), the ping outruns any
   footstep, and the party's door stays shut and
   boring.
3. The rest's paperwork rides the hour (the sentinel
   *enables* it): Joe's Recovery and Memorize Spell
   (J-57, J-56), the journal's catch-up (J-83), the
   fragment-debriefs from the day's flights (play
   25) — the hour is a pit stop, and the bird's
   watch is what buys the crew's heads-down.
4. The broken rest protocol, pre-briefed: a ping
   mid-hour means *finish nothing* — the party
   stands to at tier two, and the rest either
   resumes (fauna, false) or converts to J-96's
   script with sixty seconds of warning the door
   would never have given.

### At the table

Mechanically: "We rest here — an hour, and I need
it for Recovery and a spell swap. Architecture:
Alarm on the west corridor, Cow at the east
junction where it can hear both halls, door shut.
Everyone actually rest — the building is watched.
If you hear me say 'stand to,' you have a full
minute, which is a fortune."

Verbally: *"The party sleeps an hour in the
monster's own house — actually sleeps, bandaging
and breathing — because the corridor outside is
listening to itself on their behalf, with ears
that have never once, in the whole history of
the bond, been caught resting."*
