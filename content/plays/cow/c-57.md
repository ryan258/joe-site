Cow finds the allies Joe can't see — so Joe can move until he
can. (The honest version of "Cow extends Sculpt.")

### When to use it

- Smoke, fog, walls, and chaos between Joe and his own
  people at AoE time: Sculpt Spells protects only creatures
  Joe **can see** (J-74's hard rule) — the census is how
  that clause gets satisfied.

### Mechanics

1. The rule respected first: Cow's eyes do *not*
   substitute for Joe's — no seeing-through-Cow
   targeting, no sculpting the unseen. What Cow
   provides is the **map**: where every ally actually
   is, inside the murk (link-pings by landmark, play
   16; the Bonus-Action look, play 19, when the picture's
   complex).
2. The play is *repositioning on intelligence*: knowing
   Tam is behind the left pillar, Joe moves 20 feet to
   put his own eyes on that pillar's edge — now Tam is
   seen, named, and sculpted, and the Fireball flies
   legally (J-74's count read aloud, as ever).
3. The veto product matters as much: when the census
   says allies are scattered *through* the target zone
   beyond the Sculpt budget, the AoE is off (J-74's
   discipline) — and Joe knew before the slot was
   spent, not after the apology.
4. Fast fights get the compressed version: one ping —
   *the four cups + still water* ("all four allies
   clear of the zone") — pre-arranged for exactly the
   smoke-and-Fireball moment. The codebook (play 16)
   earns its drills here.

### At the table

Mechanically: "Their smoke covers half the yard and I
lost the front line in it — census: Cow pings...
Brenna and Tam are both behind the well, clear of the
cluster. I move to the cart — my own eyes on the well's
edge, both of them visible and named: Sculpt on
Brenna and Tam, and *now* the Fireball goes into the
smoke where nobody's standing but theirs."

Verbally: *"The enemy bought smoke to blind the
wizard's fire. It blinds the fire's targets instead —
because the fire's *edges* were never drawn by what
Joe could see through the grey, but by a small
certainty above it that always, always knows where
his people are."*
