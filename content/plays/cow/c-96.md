The pre-loaded contingencies: what Cow already knows to do
when nobody can tell it anything.

### When to use it

- Written in downtime, live always — the orders that
  govern the gaps: beyond the tether, Joe unconscious,
  the party scattered, the plan dead.

### Mechanics

1. The standing set, kept short (play 25's grammar
   holds — few, simple, image-anchored): **if
   fire: home now** (the universal abort); **if
   Joe falls: play 98, in order**; **if separated
   past a day: the fallback rally, dawn and dusk
   passes** (the party's current rally always has
   an image, refreshed at each camp — J-147's
   evening slot); **if the party falls: the
   witness order** (watch, remember, follow the
   survivors or the takers — the file's last
   sensor stays a sensor); **if hunted
   specifically: the pocket refusal** (Cow parks
   only on Joe's action — so the order is scatter,
   high and alone, to the deep rally: a spirit in
   open sky is uncatchable by anything that
   can't fly).
2. The maintenance cadence: reviewed at each
   town (play 93's shopping rhythm), re-imaged
   at each camp (the rally refresh), drilled
   quarterly (play 95) — standing orders rot
   without rehearsal, and rotted orders are
   worse than none.
3. The design rule: every order ends somewhere
   *safe for Cow* — no standing order
   trades the spirit for the mission (play 94's
   article five, compiled in) — because orders
   execute exactly when Joe isn't there to
   veto, which is exactly when the constitution
   must hold by itself.
4. The party knows the set (the spoken-aloud
   rule, play 95): allies who know what the bird
   will do can build their own worst-day plans
   on it — the standing orders are load-bearing
   for six people, not two.

### At the table

Mechanically: "Review, since we're provisioned:
the standing set is five orders — abort, my
fall, separation, the party's fall, and the
hunt-scatter. Rally image tonight is the
boathouse; deep rally is still the Harrenford
mill. Everyone knows the set? Then that's the
skeleton every worst case lands on. New rally
image tomorrow at camp."

Verbally: *"The plans men make aloud die with
the first arrow — every soldier knows it. In
this party the deepest plans were never spoken
at all: five old orders, carried in six ounces
of patience, refreshed nightly with a single
picture of a boathouse — so that on the night
everything else fails, one small survivor
already knows, in order, exactly what the
world should do next."*
