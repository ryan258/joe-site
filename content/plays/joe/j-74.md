The feature is automatic; the *counting* is the skill. One misdeclared
ally is a friendly-fire incident with Joe's name on it.

### When to use it

- Every Evocation save-spell cast near allies — which is most of them.
  The count happens *before* the casting is declared, every time.

### Mechanics

1. The budget: **1 + spell level** creatures chosen from those in the
   area that Joe **can see** — *Burning Hands* 2, *Web*-no (not
   Evocation!), *Fireball* / *Lightning Bolt* 4, *Ice Storm* 5.
2. The two failure modes: allies Joe *cannot see* (behind the pillar,
   invisible, in fog) cannot be sculpted — position or wait; and
   allies in excess of the budget mean the shape or the spell is
   wrong — re-aim, upcast (+1 protected per level), or pick another
   play.
3. Non-Evocation spells never sculpt: *Web* catches friends. Keep the
   two lists separate in your head — Sculpt applies to the Savant
   school only.
4. Table craft: keep a literal card — "FB/LB: 4 · Ice Storm: 5 · BH: 2"
   — and read the protected names aloud at every casting (play 1's
   habit, promoted to law). The DM hearing the names *before* dice hit
   the table is what makes the precision real.
