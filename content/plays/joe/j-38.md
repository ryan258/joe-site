Thirty feet of instant elsewhere, as a Bonus Action.

### When to use it

- Joe is grappled, Restrained by a creature, surrounded, or cornered —
  teleportation ignores all of it.
- Escaping without provoking Opportunity Attacks (teleporting is not
  leaving reach by movement).

### Mechanics

1. 2nd-level, **Bonus Action**, self: teleport up to 30 feet to an
   unoccupied space Joe **can see**.
2. Teleporting ends a grapple and escapes the Restrained condition from
   effects like a net or a creature's grip (not from *Web* itself while
   still in webs — teleport *out of the cube* and it is moot).
3. Opportunity Attacks trigger on leaving reach with movement;
   teleportation never provokes.
4. Same-turn spellcasting: after a Bonus Action spell, Joe's Action can
   only be a **cantrip** — Misty Step + Fire Bolt is legal, Misty Step
   + Fireball is not (play 40).
5. It needs line of sight — smoke, darkness, and *Fog Cloud* jam it.
   Keep a seen destination in mind at all times; that habit is the play.

### At the table

Mechanically: "The ogre's got me grappled — Bonus Action, Misty Step,
30 feet to the ledge I can see behind it. The grapple ends, no
Opportunity Attack, and I still have my Action: Fire Bolt, 2d10, down
at it."

Verbally: *"The ogre's fist closes on a man who is now somewhere with
better sightlines. 'Thank you for holding still,' Joe says from the
ledge."*
