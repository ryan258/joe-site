Many weak enemies, priced per head: the loadout and sequence for
swarms, packs, and mobs.

### When to use it

- Enemy count is high and individual HP is low — goblin mobs, rat
  tides, skeleton crews — where save-AoE buys three kills per die.
- Prep signal: rumors of "dozens" are a *Memorize Spell* trigger
  (play 56) the rest before.

### Mechanics

1. **The bench to swap in**: *Burning Hands* (play 28 — 3d6 cone,
   the 1st-level slot that hits four), *Ice Storm* (play 59 — the
   wave-breaker), with *Fireball* already the centerpiece (8d6 kills
   most sub-15-HP creatures on a *successful* save).
2. **Sequence**: chokepoint first if one exists (*Web* — a horde in
   a corridor is a queue, and queues take Lightning — play 95);
   *Fireball* the densest 20 feet; *Ice Storm* the reinforcement
   ground (the difficult-terrain rider is anti-horde by design);
   *Burning Hands* the leakers that reach Joe; *Thunderwave* (play
   22) as the surrounded button.
3. **Sculpt arithmetic scales with chaos**: hordes envelop allies —
   the counts (2/4/5, play 74) get spent every single cast. Read
   names aloud; hordes are where the discipline pays.
4. **Cantrip economy in the gaps**: Potent Cantrip half-damage
   (play 6) kills 4-HP creatures on a *miss*. Nothing in a horde is
   safe from a man who cannot roll low enough to deal zero.

### At the table

Mechanically: "'Dozens', they said — so at the rest I swapped in
Burning Hands and I'm carrying the Ice Storm today. Plan: they funnel
at the bailey gate — Web it, Fireball the press behind, Ice Storm
lands on the second wave's ground, and anything that swims through
all that gets a cone at my toes. Sculpt names every cast; stay near
me and you're in the safe column."

Verbally: *"The horde comes on like weather, and Joe answers in kind
— silk, fire, hail, frost — a scholar working through the plural
with every conjugation he owns."*
