An alarm pointed backward — at evidence, at bait, at the room you left.

### When to use it

- Leaving a crime scene, cache, or suspect's room that someone may
  revisit — the *revisit* is the evidence.
- Baiting: leave the interesting object in the warded cube and see who
  comes for it.

### Mechanics

1. Same ritual as play 31: 20-foot cube, 8 hours, mental ping within 1
   mile.
2. Ward the evidence room after examining it. A mental ping during
   dinner tells Joe *someone returned* — and roughly when, which is
   testimony no witness can give.
3. The ping does not say *who*: pair it with Cow posted where the ward's
   entrance is visible (plays 5 and 86), so the ping cues Joe to look
   through Cow's eyes for the face.
4. Choose mental, obviously — the audible version teaches the tamperer
   to be careful.

### At the table

Mechanically: "Before we leave the archive, ritual Alarm on the cube
around the forged ledger's shelf, mental. Cow roosts in the skylight
across the hall. If anyone comes back for that ledger tonight, I get
the ping and Cow gets the face."

Verbally: *"Joe leaves the ledger exactly where he found it, slightly
squarer than he found it, and the room holds its breath behind him."*
