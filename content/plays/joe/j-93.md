The full air-superiority package: untouchable altitude, 150-foot
delivery radius.

### When to use it

- Open-field fights against grounded enemies whose ranged threats are
  dead, spent, or never existed — verify *before* ascending; the
  audit is the play's admission price.
- Sieges and mass skirmishes where the party's job is attrition from
  angles the enemy can't answer.

### Mechanics

1. *Fly* on self (play 54): Concentration, 60-foot fly speed. Climb
   to **60–90 feet** — above thrown-weapon reality, inside *Fireball*'s
   150-foot range with margin.
2. Each round: move to the next cluster's overhead, *Fireball* (8d6,
   DC 15, Sculpt 4 if any allies strayed under). Cantrips between
   worthy clusters — altitude makes even Fire Bolt serene.
3. The stack's keystone is the Concentration: one lucky arrow, one
   failed CON save (+3), and Joe is a 6d6–9d6 falling-damage event.
   *Mirror Image* before ascending (play 46), Heroic Inspiration
   banked for the save (play 71), and an abort ceiling — drift over
   soft terrain, never over the pike square.
4. Slot honesty: this spends a 3rd on *Fly* plus 3rds on *Fireballs*
   — a two-Fireball run costs the whole 3rd-level ledger. It buys the
   field; make sure the field is worth the ledger (play 73).

### At the table

Mechanically: "Their archers broke last round — now. Fly, Mirror Image
already up, ascending to seventy. This turn and next: Fireball on the

pushes under me. If anything hits me up here, Inspiration's for the
Concentration save."
