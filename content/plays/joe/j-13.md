A sound where Joe isn't — the cheapest guard-mover in the game.

### When to use it

- A patrol needs to be somewhere else for thirty seconds.
- The party needs a distraction that costs no slot and leaves no trace.

### Mechanics

1. Cantrip, Action, range 30 feet, duration 1 minute. Create a sound —
   footsteps on gravel, a dropped blade, a voice calling for help — at
   any volume, from a whisper to a scream.
2. A creature that uses the Study action can make an Intelligence
   (Investigation) check against **DC 15** to recognize the illusion.
3. Guards who merely hear it get no automatic check — they react to what
   they heard. Where they go is the DM's call; make the sound something
   they *must* check (an intruder, an officer's voice, breaking glass).
4. Sound only for this play — the image version is plays 14 and 15.

### At the table

Mechanically: "Minor Illusion, 30 feet down the east corridor: the sound
of a window breaking and someone dropping onto flagstones. I want the
patrol to check it."

Verbally: *"Glass shatters in an empty hallway. Joe, flat against the
opposite wall, mouths the count of the guards' footsteps as they pass."*
