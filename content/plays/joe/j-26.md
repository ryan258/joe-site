The same spell, pointed at gravity.

### When to use it

- Enemies must descend or climb stairs, a ramp, or a ladder to reach
  the party.
- Covering a retreat up or down a level.

### Mechanics

1. Cast *Grease* (play 25) on the stairs: difficult terrain plus DEX
   save DC 15 or Prone — and a creature that goes Prone on stairs
   usually slides or tumbles to the bottom (DM's call; falling rules
   commonly apply, 1d6 per 10 feet).
2. A ladder under *Grease* is close to unclimbable: climbing is already
   half Speed, the square is difficult terrain, and a failed save means
   a fall.
3. One 1st-level slot, no Concentration, 1 minute: that is often the
   entire pursuit, solved.
4. Joe's own party crosses it too — cast it *behind* the party, not
   under them.

### At the table

Mechanically: "As we fall back up the stairwell — Grease on the flight
below us. Difficult terrain, and Dexterity save DC 15 or Prone, which on
stairs means they're going back down the hard way."

Verbally: *"The pursuit hits Joe's arithmetic at the fourth step: boots,
grease, gravity — in that order."*
