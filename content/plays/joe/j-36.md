**Spellbook only.** The best use of *Invisibility* is often on someone
else.

### When to use it

- The party's rogue or scout can do more with unseen movement than Joe
  can — better Stealth, better hands, better judgment inside.
- Joe needs to stay outside as the coordinator (Message relay, Cow
  overwatch, Counterspell umbrella).

### Mechanics

1. Same casting as play 35, but the touched target is the specialist.
   Joe holds Concentration from a safe position — which means Joe's
   safety *is* the mission's safety; he stands somewhere boring.
2. Coordinate by *Message* (play 11): 120 feet, whispers both ways. The
   specialist reports; Joe navigates them using Cow's aerial picture.
3. The same end conditions bind them: one attack or spell and they are
   visible in the middle of wherever they are. Brief them on it twice.
4. Upcast at 3rd level to make it two creatures if the job needs pairs
   — entry and lookout.

### At the table

Mechanically: "Invisibility on Senna, I'll concentrate from the tavern
across the street. Message keeps us linked — she whispers what she
sees, I whisper the route Cow mapped. If she so much as slaps someone,
it ends."

Verbally: *"Joe sits with an untouched drink, eyes half closed, playing
a burglar across the street like a piece on a board only he can see
all of."*
