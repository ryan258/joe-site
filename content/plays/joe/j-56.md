The prepared list is not fixed — it has a one-spell hinge that swings
every Short Rest.

### When to use it

- Intelligence has changed the problem: tomorrow is a fog-caster, a
  corridor fortress, a flooded mine — and the right tool is in the book
  but not on the sheet.
- After a fight proves a prepared spell dead weight for this arc.

### Mechanics

1. Memorize Spell (Wizard 5): when Joe finishes a **Short Rest**
   studying his spellbook, he can replace **one** prepared spell (level
   1+) with another Wizard spell from the book.
2. The book's bench: *Thunderwave*, *Chromatic Orb*, *Burning Hands*,
   *Detect Magic*, *Find Familiar*, *Alarm*, *Invisibility*,
   *Scorching Ray*, *Gust of Wind*, *Lightning Bolt*, *Ice Storm* — see
   the **Spellbook only** plays for what each buys.
3. Doctrine: scout first (plays 5, 63), *then* pivot — the swap is
   worth most when it answers a *known* problem. A blind swap is just
   different guesswork.
4. Long Rests re-deal the whole hand (change any number of prepared
   spells); Memorize Spell is specifically the mid-day correction.

### At the table

Mechanically: "Cow's flyover says the approach is one long ravine —
corridor country. During this Short Rest I use Memorize Spell: drop
Arcane Eye, prepare Lightning Bolt."

Verbally: *"While the others eat, Joe reads. When he closes the book,
one of his thoughts has a different shape than it did at breakfast."*
