The signature play. *Fireball* into a melee scrum that would normally be
off-limits, with Sculpt Spells carving allies out of the blast.

### When to use it

- Three or more enemies are clustered inside a 20-foot radius.
- Allies are mixed into that cluster — this is exactly the situation
  Sculpt Spells exists for, so do not wait for a "clean" shot.
- The fight is worth a 3rd-level slot. Against two weakened enemies, use
  the wand or a cantrip instead.

### Mechanics

1. Action: cast *Fireball* (3rd level). Range 150 feet, 20-foot-radius
   sphere.
2. Sculpt Spells: choose up to **4** creatures in the area (1 + the
   spell's level). They automatically succeed on the save and take **no
   damage**.
3. Everyone else makes a Dexterity saving throw vs DC 15: 8d6 fire on a
   failure, half on a success.
4. Upcast at 4th level: 9d6, and Sculpt protects up to **5** creatures.
5. *Fireball* ignites flammable objects in the area that aren't being
   worn or carried — say so before the DM has to remind you.

### At the table

Mechanically: "I cast *Fireball* at 3rd level, centered on the point
between the two ogres — 20-foot radius. Sculpt Spells: Brenna, Tam, and
the prisoner automatically succeed and take no damage. Everyone else
saves against DC 15. Rolling 8d6."

Verbally: *"Joe traces one precise line in the air, measuring the room
like an equation. The fire blooms around his friends without touching
them — every inch of it accounted for."*

Precision is the character: Joe chose Evocation because exact force
protects people. Narrate the blast as controlled, never wild.
