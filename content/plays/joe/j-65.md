Auto-hit is a damage floor no armor raises.

### When to use it

- AC 19+ enemies, where Fire Bolt at +7 hits 45% of the time —
  Potent Cantrip halves misses, but the wand never misses at all.
- Cursed luck nights. The wand does not roll; the wand cannot be cold.

### Mechanics

1. *Magic Missile* darts **hit automatically** — no attack roll, no
   save. One charge is 3d4+3, a flat **10.5** every time. Fire Bolt
   (2d10 at +7, counting crits and Potent Cantrip's half-on-miss)
   averages **10.45 at AC 12**, **9.08 at AC 17**, and **8.53 at AC
   19**. So the crossover is not AC 17 — it sits at roughly **AC 12**,
   and on raw expected damage the wand matches or beats Fire Bolt
   against essentially any armored target.
   What keeps the wand from being the default is scarcity, not math:
   7 charges regaining `1d6 + 1` at dawn against a cantrip that is
   free forever. Read the *round*, not just the armor — spend charges
   when the damage has to land now, and let Fire Bolt carry the
   ordinary rounds even when it is the slightly worse average.
2. The counter exists: the *Shield* spell **negates Magic Missile
   entirely**. One enemy reaction eats the whole charge — against a
   caster with 1st-level slots, lead with something else and let an
   ally bait the *Shield* out first.
3. Darts kill concentration cheaply — see play 66.
4. Invisible or unseen targets are illegal — darts need a visible
   creature. Cow's eyes don't help; *Joe* must see it (play 44 clears
   the fog for exactly this).
