The sky is only an advantage until the wizard prices it.

### When to use it

- Winged enemies are staying out of the martials' reach and diving at
  their leisure.

### Mechanics

1. Range beats altitude: *Fire Bolt* 120, *Fireball* 150, *Ice Storm*
   **300** — almost nothing flies above Joe's reach. The party's
   problem is rarely Joe's problem; say so and take over the target.
2. *Ray of Frost* the wings: Speed -10 on a flyer shrinks its dive
   circuit; on creatures that must move to stay aloft, ask the DM
   what -10 does to their glide.
3. Grounding wins fights: *Web* between anchor points catches low
   passes (Restrained = Speed 0 = a flyer **falls** to the web's
   mercy); readied actions punish dives — ready the cantrip for "when
   it comes inside 60."
4. Enemy *Fly*-casters are a *Counterspell*/*Dispel Magic* problem
   (plays 4, 52): end the spell and gravity files the rest (their
   fall, their damage).
5. Joe's own *Fly* (play 54) contests the altitude directly when the
   enemy insists — but read play 93's Concentration warnings first.

### At the table

Mechanically: "It keeps circling at eighty feet like that's safe.
It's not: Ice Storm reaches three hundred. And if it dives again, my
readied Ray of Frost trims ten off its Speed mid-pass — ready it now."

Verbally: *"The wyvern owns the sky the way a debtor owns his coat.
Joe begins the repossession from the comfort of the ground."*
