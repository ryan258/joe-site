Sources are grown, not found: the long game of small favors,
safe channels, and never burning anyone.

### When to use it

- Recurring city play — building the network that makes plays
  138–140 start warm instead of cold, case after case.

### Mechanics

1. **Recruit by kindness, keep by reliability**: the
   lamplighter, the ferrywoman, the archive clerk — paid in
   Prestidigitation's comforts (play 19), small real coin, and
   *problems actually solved* (Joe fixes a source's rat
   problem or lifted-goods problem once; the source is his
   for years). The sheet's warmth-through-practical-help is
   literally the mechanic.
2. **Channels that don't burn sources**: *Message* is the
   perfect dead-drop — 120 feet, silent, no meeting, no
   letter to intercept (play 11); scheduled walk-bys replace
   visits; Cow carries the physical when physical must move
   (play 87) — a bird visits everyone and betrays no one.
3. Protection is the product Joe actually sells: sources talk
   to the man whose network never has accidents — which means
   counter-surveillance on *their* behalf (play 138's audit,
   run for the source), and the iron rule from the sheet:
   **no source is bait, ever** — the Ideal is also just good
   tradecraft.
4. The garden's yield compounds silently: each source is one
   node of passive Perception the party doesn't pay actions
   for — the network *is* the city-wide Alarm spell, and its
   ping is a boy with a chalk mark saying "the lamplighter
   wants tea."

### At the table

Mechanically: "Before we leave town, rounds: the ferrywoman
gets her dock-thief problem solved — that's an afternoon and
it buys five years — the clerk gets tea and no questions this
month, and everyone gets the same standing terms: chalk the
lamppost, I come to you, nobody ever visits your door. The
city watches itself; I just tend the watchers."

Verbally: *"There is no spy-master's ledger, no purse of
dirty silver — just a scholar who fixes small problems for
small people and drinks a great deal of tea; and somehow,
whenever something moves in the quarter after dark, a quiet
word reaches him before the echo dies."*
