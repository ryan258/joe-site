The Evoker's stress test: what happens when the signature school's
favorite type stops working.

### When to use it

- Fiends, fire elementals, red-scaled anything — Arcana or one wasted
  Fire Bolt says "resistant" or "immune."

### Mechanics

1. The fallback ladder, in order: **force** (wand — resisted by
   almost nothing), **cold** (*Ray of Frost*, *Ice Storm* — but
   check: fire-things often resist cold's opposite... no, they
   often *take it normally or worse*; Arcana confirms), **lightning**
   (*Lightning Bolt* — play 48), **bludgeoning** (*Ice Storm* again),
   **thunder** (*Thunderwave*).
2. *Chromatic Orb* is the permanent answer when prepared (play 24) — the
   diamond is bought, so the type-shopping option is available without
   another purchase after the next preparation swap.
3. Sculpt Spells doesn't care about damage type: the protective count
   works on *Ice Storm* and *Thunderwave* exactly as on *Fireball* —
   the Evoker's real signature survives the type-switch intact.
4. Control never resists: *Web*, *Grease*, *Gust of Wind* deal no
   typed damage at all. Against the immune, Joe becomes the control
   wizard he always partly was, and the party's weapons do the
   arithmetic.
5. Efficiency note: resistance halves — against merely-resistant
   enemies in a perfect cluster, a sculpted *Fireball* at half is
   still 4d6 to each; do that math before abandoning the slot.
