**Spellbook only.** A corridor of wind that makes approaching Joe a
career.

### When to use it

- A bridge, dock, ladder-top, or corridor must be held against creatures
  that need to walk it.
- Flying pests: the DM may rule the line disrupts Tiny fliers — ask.

### Mechanics

1. Same casting as play 44: 60-by-10 line from self, Concentration 1
   minute.
2. Approach tax: creatures in the line pay **2 feet per 1 foot** moving
   toward Joe — a Speed-30 enemy makes 15 feet of progress, before the
   save.
3. Strength save vs DC 15 immediately when cast, or when ending their turn in
   the line, or be pushed 15 feet back: fail the save and they slide backward.
4. On a narrow bridge, the push has edges to work with — a shoved
   creature's fall is the bridge's contribution.
5. Bonus Action re-aim tracks enemies trying to angle around the line.

### At the table

Mechanically: "I stand at our end of the rope bridge and cast Gust of
Wind straight down it. Everything currently on the bridge saves against DC 15
immediately (pushed 15 feet back on failure), and must save again if they
end their turn in the line. They also pay double movement toward me."

Verbally: *"The bridge becomes an opinion Joe is expressing at sixty
feet, and the raiders discover they do not hold it strongly enough."*
