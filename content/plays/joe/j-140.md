A fresh scene degrades by the minute. The full crime-scene
standard operating procedure, in order, before anyone helpful
ruins everything.

### When to use it

- The party arrives at a scene — a body, a break-in, a burned
  workshop — while it's still *a scene* and not yet a story.

### Mechanics

1. **Freeze it** (minute one): nobody past the threshold; the
   most careful ally on the door (the crowd *is* suspects and
   witnesses — someone watches *them*, faces first, play 79's
   channel from the doorway); Cow to a vantage for the
   wide-shot memory (play 88's silent witness, in reverse).
2. **The overview pass** (minutes two–ten): Joe walks the
   *edge*, not the middle — entrances, exits, sightlines,
   what's *missing* (the absence is usually the motive) —
   Investigation running as narration ("state intent and
   method," the sheet's law), touching nothing, Prestidigitation
   marks floating over each exhibit (play 18's numbering).
3. **The magic sweep** (minutes eleven–twenty-one): ritual
   *Detect Magic* (free, play 29) — auras on the body, the
   locks, the *walls* (play 30's localization) — because the
   mundane-looking scene with one Enchantment aura on a chair
   is a different case entirely.
4. **The deep reads** (the balance of the hour): Study actions
   in triage order — most-perishable first (wounds before
   weather changes them, prints before the crowd's feet, warm
   ash before cold) — each finding *inked before the next
   look* (memory contaminates; the journal doesn't, play 83).
5. **Seal and bait on exit**: *Alarm* on the room, mental
   (play 32) — because the one visitor a sealed scene always
   eventually receives is the person who needs to check what
   was found.

### At the table

Mechanically: "Nobody in — Tam owns the door and watches the
crowd, not the room; faces now, names later. Cow's on the
armoire. I walk the edge first: two exits, forced lock,
and the desk has a dust-shadow where something square is
gone — that absence is exhibit one. Ritual Detect Magic while
I sketch. Wounds get Studied before anything else changes
them. And when we leave, the room gets an Alarm — scenes like
this always get one more visitor."

Verbally: *"The neighbors see a tall man walking slowly
around the edge of a tragedy, apparently doing nothing. The
room sees the only visitor it will ever have who asks it
questions in the right order — and writes down its answers
before they fade."*
