**Spellbook only.** Getting a person *out* — a witness, a hostage, a
defector — without a chase.

### When to use it

- Extraction of a willing person from a watched building or camp.
- The moment after a rescue, when the alarm has not yet sounded and the
  exit still exists.

### Mechanics

1. Cast on the extractee (touch, Concentration 1 hour): the frightened
   witness is the one who most needs to be unseeable; the party can
   fight, the witness cannot.
2. Escort formation: the visible party walks a plausible route; the
   invisible witness walks with Joe, one hand on his shoulder so he
   knows where they are (an invisible companion is invisible to Joe
   too — track by touch).
3. Pursuers who search use the Search action against the witness's
   position, and Invisible imposes Disadvantage on Perception checks
   relying on sight; noise discipline still decides everything.
4. Chain into play 61 (*Dimension Door*) if the exit collapses: Joe
   plus one willing creature, 500 feet, instantly.

### At the table

Mechanically: "Invisibility on the clerk before we walk out — she holds
my shoulder, we take the servant stairs while the rest of you leave
loudly through the front. If it goes wrong I still have Dimension Door
for the two of us."

Verbally: *"The most valuable person in the building leaves it as an
absence: a door that opens for no one, a stair that creaks once."*
