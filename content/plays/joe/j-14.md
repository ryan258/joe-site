A crate, a barrel, a low wall — conjured exactly where Joe needs to not
be seen.

### When to use it

- Caught in the open during infiltration with seconds to hide.
- Creating a hiding spot for the party's scout in a bare corridor.

### Mechanics

1. Cantrip, Action, 30 feet, 1 minute: an image of an object no larger
   than a **5-foot cube** — a crate, a boulder, a curtain of shadowed
   wall.
2. Joe crouches behind it and Hides: Dexterity (Stealth) +5. To the
   casual eye the object is real.
3. Physical interaction reveals it (a hand passes through); the Study
   action vs DC 15 sees through it, and then it appears transparent to
   that creature.
4. It is an image only — it blocks sight, not arrows. Treat it as
   concealment, never as armor.

### At the table

Mechanically: "Before the sentry rounds the corner — Minor Illusion: a
stack of grain sacks against the wall, 5-foot cube. I duck behind it and
Hide. Stealth: 17."

Verbally: *"The sentry's lantern light slides across a stack of sacks
that was not there at dusk and will not be there at dawn. Joe breathes
through his mouth and counts."*
