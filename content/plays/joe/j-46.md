The duplicates exist to absorb exactly the hits that would break *Web*
or *Fly*.

### When to use it

- Joe is concentrating on the spell the plan depends on and enemies
  have noticed.
- Before wading anywhere risky *while* concentrating — cast it in the
  same fight, images first if enemies act between Joe's turns.

### Mechanics

1. Concentration breaks on a failed CON save (DC 10 or half damage,
   whichever is higher) — Joe's CON save is **+3**, so the real defense
   is never rolling it.
2. *Mirror Image* (no Concentration — it stacks) redirects attacks on a
   d6 (play 41): every redirected hit is a Concentration save that
   never happens.
3. Layer order for a concentration turn: *Web* (Action, turn one),
   *Mirror Image* (Action, turn two), Free *Shield* held for whatever
   still gets through, Heroic Inspiration (play 71) banked for the one
   save Joe must roll.
4. Positioning is the free layer: 60 feet from the nearest enemy, out
   of the archers' easy lines. The best save is +∞: not targeted.

### At the table

Mechanically: "The Web is the whole plan, so my turn is Mirror Image
and repositioning behind the pillar. Attacks have to find me, then hit,
then fail the d6 redirect roll, then beat AC 20 if I Shield — and if a hit
still lands, I've got Heroic Inspiration for the Constitution save."

Verbally: *"Joe builds a small bureaucracy between the enemy and his
spell: three false scholars, a pane of hard air, and a filing system
for bad luck."*
