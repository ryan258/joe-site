+5 hands for the moments when evidence must change pockets.

### When to use it

- Palming the key, lifting the letter, planting the marked coin,
  swapping the real ledger for the copy (play 84 makes the copy).
- Only when the target is *attended* — unattended objects are Mage
  Hand's department (play 17), and the spectral hand takes no risk.

### Mechanics

1. Dexterity (Sleight of Hand) at **+5**, contested by the mark's
   Perception (or a DC the DM sets). Failure has a *social* blast
   radius — plan the caught-story before the attempt.
2. Stack the deck first: Prestidigitation's sensory nudge across the
   room (play 13's sound in a doorway), an ally's distraction, the
   crowded moment. Distraction converts to Advantage at most tables.
3. The swap beats the theft: an object that *seems still there* is not
   missed, and a forged stand-in (play 84) buys days, not minutes.
4. Joe's frame: he lifts *evidence*, not valuables — the sheet's
   principles hold ("never use another person as expendable
   evidence"), and DMs notice which kind of thief they're rewarding.

### At the table

Mechanically: "While Tam asks him for directions, I brush past — Sleight
of Hand, plus five, to swap his seal-ring pouch for the weighted copy.
A 19. He'll discover it at the worst possible time, which is the
point."

Verbally: *"Joe's apology for the bump is sincere, complete, and one
seal-ring long."*
