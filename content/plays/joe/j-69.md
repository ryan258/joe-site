The mundane item that does two spells' work for one silver piece.

### When to use it

- **Slick**: a *Grease*-shaped problem when slots are precious — a
  square that needs to be slippery-adjacent and flammable.
- **Splash**: a fire-vulnerable or regenerating enemy (trolls) the
  party wants pre-soaked (play 10 runs the full combo).

### Mechanics

1. **Pour** (Action): cover a level, 5-foot square. Ignited — by any
   fire, Joe stocks *Fire Bolt* — it burns for **2 rounds**, dealing
   **5 fire** to any creature entering the area or ending its turn
   there.
2. **Throw** (Action): improvised ranged attack, range 20/60, at the
   DM's ruled bonus (commonly Dex + prof for a flask: +5). On a hit the
   target is oiled for 1 minute; any fire damage before it dries adds
   **+5 fire**.
3. The pour costs no attack roll and no save — pre-lay it at the
   chokepoint *before* the fight and it is terrain, laid during the
   fight it is a slow round. Prep beats improvisation.
4. Carry two flasks; they are 1 sp each and weigh a pound. This line
   is the shopping list.

### At the table

Mechanically: "Before they breach: I pour the oil across the doorway's
square. When the first one's astride it — Fire Bolt at him: 2d10, plus
he's standing in burning oil, 5 fire now and 5 more if he stays."

Verbally: *"The doorway shines faintly, patient as an unpaid debt, and
the first raider through pays it in full."*
