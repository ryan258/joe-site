**Spellbook only.** Sculpt Spells is not a Fireball feature — it is an
every-Evocation feature.

### When to use it

- The perfect line runs *through* the party's front line — which it
  usually does, because the melee is where the enemies are.
- Any Evocation save-spell landing on mixed company: *Burning Hands*,
  *Ice Storm*, *Thunderwave* all sculpt the same way.

### Mechanics

1. Sculpt Spells: when casting an **Evocation** spell that affects
   other creatures Joe can see, choose up to **1 + the spell's level**
   of them — they automatically succeed on the save and take **no
   damage** (when the spell normally halves on success).
2. *Lightning Bolt* at 3rd: protect **4**. The line through the melee
   is now legal: both fighters and the two hirelings walk out dry.
3. The count is a hard budget — five allies in the line means the play
   is one ally short of legal. Count *before* declaring, upcast to 4th
   (protect 5) when the count demands.
4. Say the protected names out loud every time; the table's trust in
   Joe's precision is a resource this feature builds.

### At the table

Mechanically: "The line goes straight through our front line — that's
the point. Lightning Bolt, Sculpt Spells on Brenna, Tam, Senna, and the
sellsword: they auto-succeed and take nothing. The four hobgoblins
around them save against DC 15 for 8d6."

Verbally: *"The lightning threads the melee like a scribe's pen through
a margin, italicizing every enemy and touching none of the text."*
