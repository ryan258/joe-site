Sixty feet up, out of reach, raining precision.

### When to use it

- Open-sky or high-ceiling fights against ground-bound enemies with
  weak ranged options.
- Joe must cross or occupy vertical terrain: cliffs, towers, sinkholes.

### Mechanics

1. 3rd-level, Action, touch, **Concentration up to 10 minutes**: the
   target gains a **Fly Speed of 60 feet**.
2. Altitude doctrine: hold 60+ feet up — outside most melee threats and
   many thrown weapons — and inside Joe's own ranges (*Fire Bolt* 120,
   *Fireball* 150).
3. The failure mode is the whole risk: if Concentration breaks, the
   spell ends and Joe **falls** (1d6 per 10 feet — 6d6 from doctrine
   altitude). Fly only when the archer threat is cataloged, keep
   *Mirror Image* up (play 46), and bank Heroic Inspiration for the CON
   save.
4. Upcast: +1 creature per slot level — a flying fighter delivered to
   the enemy caster is a different, excellent spell.

### At the table

Mechanically: "No archers left — now it's my sky. Fly on myself,
concentrating, up 60. From here: Fire Bolt every round, Fireball if
they bunch, and nothing down there can answer."

Verbally: *"Joe ascends with his hands folded behind him, a scholar
pacing a gallery, and the battlefield reorganizes itself into a
diagram he can finally mark."*
