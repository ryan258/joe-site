Same slot, same dice, different shape. Choosing correctly is a whole
play.

### When to use it

- Every time a 3rd-level damage slot is about to be spent. Make the
  call consciously; the wrong shape wastes 8d6.

### Mechanics

1. Both: 8d6, DEX save vs DC 15, half on success, Sculpt protects 4.
2. **Fireball** (150-foot range, 20-foot radius): clusters, rooms,
   courtyards. It wraps corners of the area and does not care where Joe
   stands. The radius also finds allies easily — hence Sculpt.
3. **Lightning Bolt** (self, 100-by-5 line): files, corridors, bridges.
   It requires Joe to *stand on the geometry* — mobility before casting
   is part of the cost — but slips between allies a sphere would catch.
4. Tiebreakers: enemies past 100 feet → Fireball; enemies between Joe
   and a wall he wants to paint → Bolt; flammable terrain he does or
   does not want lit → fire ignites, lightning ignites objects too
   (both light flammables; neither is "safe" for the library).
5. If neither shape covers 3+ enemies, neither is the play — that slot
   is *Counterspell* or *Fly* money (plays 4 and 54).

### At the table

Mechanically: "Five of them, but strung out along the wharf, not
clustered — that's a line, not a sphere. I move to the bollard to align
it: Lightning Bolt, not Fireball."

Verbally: *"Joe reads the enemy's formation the way he reads a page:
first the shape, then the sentence. Tonight the sentence is straight."*
