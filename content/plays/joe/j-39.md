The wizard is on the balcony. Nobody saw stairs.

### When to use it

- A ledge, balcony, rooftop, or window within 30 feet solves a problem:
  vantage, escape, a lever, a locked-from-inside door.
- Crossing gaps — chasms, moats, collapsed bridges — up to 30 feet.

### Mechanics

1. Bonus Action, self, 30 feet, must **see** the destination and it
   must be unoccupied. Height is just distance.
2. Opening doors from the wrong side is the quiet classic: teleport
   through the barred window, lift the bar, admit the party. Cheaper
   than any knock.
3. Cross-check the landing: "unoccupied space" Joe can see — a
   30-foot-up ledge he cannot actually see the surface of is not a
   legal target. Cow's report (play 5) or a thrown light fixes that.
4. The return trip is not included. Never teleport up without knowing
   the way down; *Fly* (play 54) and *Feather Fall* do not share this
   sheet — gravity is not on Joe's prepared list.

arrow slit's ledge — Bonus Action, Misty Step up to it. Action to lift
the bar. You're welcome."
