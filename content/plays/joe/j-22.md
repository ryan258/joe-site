**Spellbook only.** The panic button for when melee reaches the wizard
anyway.

### When to use it

- Two or more enemies are adjacent to Joe and *Misty Step* alone would
  leave them free to charge the ally behind him.
- Joe is grappled: the grappler gets shoved out of grapple range on a
  failed save.

### Mechanics

1. Same casting as play 21: 15-foot cube from self, CON save DC 15, 2d8
   thunder, pushed 10 feet on a failure.
2. A push ends a grapple the moment distance exceeds the grappler's
   reach.
3. Sequencing: Thunderwave (Action) to clear the ring, then walk away
   with the freed movement — or *Misty Step* is illegal that turn only
   if you cast a *leveled* Action spell, so Thunderwave + Misty Step in
   one turn does not work. Pick one: shove and walk, or teleport and
   cantrip (play 40).
4. Prep reality: this is an emergency spell competing for prepared
   slots. Swap it in for dungeon days full of tight rooms.

### At the table

Mechanically: "I'm surrounded, so — Thunderwave. All three save against
DC 15: 2d8 thunder and shoved 10 feet on a failure. Then I move 30 the
other way."

Verbally: *"The scrum around Joe detonates outward, and the scholar
walks out of the ring of sprawled bodies at an even pace, already
thinking about the next problem."*
