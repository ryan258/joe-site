*Ice Storm*'s forgotten half: 2d10 of it is **bludgeoning**, and
skeletons are Vulnerable to exactly that.

### When to use it

- Bone-heavy undead in numbers — the classic crypt fight.

### Mechanics

1. Skeletons take **double damage from Bludgeoning**: *Ice Storm*'s
   hail (2d10 bludgeoning + 4d6 cold, and the bludgeoning is what
   increases by 1d10 when upcast) lands its first half twice on
   them — the 4th-level slot suddenly out-values a same-slot Fireball
   against this crowd.
2. *Thunderwave*'s 2d8 thunder isn't bludgeoning — but its shove
   breaks bone formations off ledges and into each other; ask the DM
   about brittle-fall rulings.
3. Undead generally: many are immune to Poison and to Exhaustion, not
   to fire, cold, or force — Joe's core kit works where the poisoner's
   doesn't. Arcana (+7) names the specific immunities before slots get
   spent (play 77's discipline).
4. Mindless undead fail no Insight and tell no lies — the investigation
   angle inverts: *who raised them* is the case, and the necromancy's
   school signature (*Detect Magic*, play 29) plus the corpses'
   provenance (Study, play 76 — grave soil, embalming, uniform buttons)
   is the evidence chain.

### At the table
