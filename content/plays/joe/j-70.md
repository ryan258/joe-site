Six resets happen every morning. Missing one is the only way Joe loses
a fight before breakfast.

### When to use it

- At the end of every Long Rest, run the list. Two minutes of real
  time; write it on an index card behind the sheet.

### Mechanics

1. **Wand**: regains **1d6+1** charges at dawn (max 7) — roll it, note
   the total.
2. **Free Shield**: Magic Initiate's free casting resets on the Long
   Rest — it exists again; play 3 is loaded.
3. **Heroic Inspiration**: Human Resourceful — regained on every Long
   Rest if not held; play 71 decides its spending.
4. **Arcane Recovery**: available again today (once per day, on a
   Short Rest — play 57). Not spent now; re-armed now.
5. **Prepared spells**: a Long Rest can swap any number — is today's
   list right for today's known problems? (Play 56 handles mid-day; now
   is the free full re-deal.)
6. **Mage Armor timing** decision (play 34) and **consumables count**:
   incense portions (play 33), potions (play 68), oil (play 69). The
   diamond (play 24) is bought — confirm it is still on Joe's person,
   not lent, pawned, or left in a saddlebag.

### At the table

Mechanically: "Morning routine: wand rolls... five charges. Free Shield
back, Inspiration back, Recovery re-armed, prepared list stands except
I'm swapping Grease for Invisibility — town job today. The diamond's
in my coat; if today's threats point to resistances, Chromatic Orb is
ready for the next preparation swap."

Verbally: *"Joe takes inventory the way monks take vows: daily, before
speaking to anyone, and with complete sincerity."*
