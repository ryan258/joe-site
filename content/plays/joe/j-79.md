Joe's Insight is +3. Joe's Insight with a Fey spirit watching the same
face is a different instrument.

### When to use it

- Interviews, negotiations, and confessions where the truth-value of a
  *person* (not a document) is the question.
- Whenever Cow can plausibly be present — on the table as a toy (play
  88), on a sill, in Joe's coat.

### Mechanics

1. Joe rolls Wisdom (Insight) at **+3** — honestly mediocre; treat it
   as one sensor, not the verdict.
2. Cow is the second sensor: within 100 feet the link carries
   **emotions, images, remembered sensations** — a Fey spirit's read of
   a liar's feeling-tone, arriving as impressions. Mechanically this is
   DM-mediated color, not a die; the play is *asking* — "does Cow feel
   anything off about her?" — and letting the DM decide what the bond
   yields.
3. Divide the channels: Joe attends to the *content* (which is really
   Investigation territory — contradictions, dates, method: +10 on
   what was *said*), Cow attends to the *feeling*. The party's high-
   Wisdom member is channel three (play 12 adds a fourth).
4. Never report Cow's impressions as facts to the table-in-fiction:
   fragmentary clues, DM-controlled, per the character sheet's own
   rule. "Cow is uneasy" is evidence to *weigh*, exactly like a smudged
   ledger.

### At the table

Mechanically: "While she gives her account I'm doing two things:
Insight, plus three — a 14 — on her delivery, and checking with Cow
through the link: what's the *feeling* in the room when she mentions
her brother?"

Verbally: *"The witness talks to the tall scholar and never once looks
at the shabby toy cow on the mantel. One of them believes her."*
