The invisible eye attends every meeting Joe attends — checking the
rooms the host didn't offer to show.

### When to use it

- Negotiations, hostage exchanges, and audiences on the other side's
  ground.
- Any conversation where the real answer is in the back room.

### Mechanics

1. 4th-level, Action, **Concentration up to 1 hour**: an invisible
   floating eye with **Darkvision 30 feet** that sends Joe its visual
   feed.
2. Move the eye up to 30 feet as an action — it passes through a gap
   as small as **1 inch**, under doors and through keyholes; it cannot
   pass through solid barriers.
3. Launch it before the meeting, park it in the corridor behind the
   host's chair: the reinforcements, the signal-watcher, the second
   exit — Joe sees them all while nodding politely.
4. The Concentration is the tell: a caster opposite Joe may notice him
   holding a spell (and Joe drops *Web*-readiness while holding it —
   one Concentration at a time). Weigh the eye against the fight that
   might start.
5. Same slot as *Dimension Door* — a parley that might go wrong poses
   the day's 4th-level question crisply: *see* trouble coming, or
   *leave* when it arrives. Cow (play 5) is the free second opinion.

### At the table

Mechanically: "Before we're shown in, I cast Arcane Eye and slide it
under the audience-hall door. While we talk, it's drifting the service
corridor — I want a count of guards we weren't introduced to. I'm
concentrating the whole meeting."

Verbally: *"Joe listens to the baron with complete attention. So does
the nothing floating in the pantry."*
