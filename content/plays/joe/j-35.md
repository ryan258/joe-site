**Spellbook only.** One hour of not being seen, spent carefully.

### When to use it

- Joe must cross watched ground: a courtyard, a checkpoint, a camp.
- Reconnaissance where Cow's owl form would be conspicuous (indoors,
  daylight, wary archers).

### Mechanics

1. 2nd-level, Action, touch, **Concentration up to 1 hour**. The target
   and everything it wears and carries is Invisible.
2. The spell **ends immediately** after the target makes an attack
   roll, deals damage, or casts a spell. Walking, listening, reading,
   opening doors, and pocketing objects are all safe.
3. Invisible ≠ silent: guards still hear footsteps and opened doors.
   Move at half pace, use Stealth (+5) when it matters, and remember
   dogs smell.
4. Concentration means one hit or failed CON save ends it — do not plan
   routes through rooms where Joe can be bumped, and take the exits Cow
   scouted first (play 5).
5. Budget the hour: in, objective, out — with ten minutes of margin.

### At the table

Mechanically: "Invisibility on myself, concentrating. I cross the yard
at half speed, Stealth 16 for the footsteps, and I'm going straight to
the records room — no touching anyone, no casting, or it drops."

Verbally: *"Joe walks through the middle of the garrison like a fact
nobody has checked yet."*
