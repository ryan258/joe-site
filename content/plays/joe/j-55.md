The spell is touch-range and upcastable: it is climbing gear for the
whole plan.

### When to use it

- The objective is up: a tower window, a cliff ledge, an airship, a
  floating keep.
- Ten minutes is enough: Fly is an *ascent*, not a lifestyle.

### Mechanics

1. Cast at 3rd on the party's best solo operator, or upcast — 4th level
   covers **two** creatures (Joe's highest slot: one casting, two
   fliers).
2. The 10-minute Concentration clock starts at casting: brief, ascend,
   act, descend, with margin. Synchronize with *Invisibility* only by
   choosing — both are Concentration and Joe holds **one** at a time.
3. Landing discipline: everyone is on the ground, or at safe-fall
   height, before minute nine. A spell that ends at altitude is an
   attack Joe made on his own party.
4. Cheap alternative check (the ladder rule): does *Misty Step* (30
   feet, self) or rope-and-Athletics solve it? Fly is for real height
   or multiple climbers.

### At the table

Mechanically: "The window's 80 feet up. Fly at 4th level on Senna and
me — 60-foot fly speed each, I'm concentrating, ten minutes. We're in
and back on the roof by minute six or we're climbing down like
honest people."

Verbally: *"Two figures rise up the tower's dark face, quiet as errata
being corrected."*
