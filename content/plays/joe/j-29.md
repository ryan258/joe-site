The zero-slot ritual that runs on every loot pile, every suspicious
room, every new NPC's parlor.

### When to use it

- After every fight, before dividing loot; on entering any room worth
  investigating; before buying anything expensive.
- Whenever the question is "is any of this magical?" — which is always.

### Mechanics

1. Ritual (via Ritual Adept: no slot, no preparation; casting time is
   1 action + 10 minutes for the ritual = **about 10 minutes**),
   Concentration up to 10 minutes once running.
2. While it runs, Joe senses the presence of magic within **30 feet**.
   As a Magic action, he can see a faint aura around any visible magical
   creature or object in range and learn its **school of magic**.
3. Blocked by 1 foot of stone, 1 inch of common metal, a thin sheet of
   lead, or 3 feet of wood or dirt — see play 30 for using that.
4. School knowledge is analysis fuel: an Illusion aura on a wall, an
   Enchantment aura on the mayor — follow with Arcana (+7, play 77) and
   Investigation (+10) to say what it *means*.

### At the table

Mechanically: "Before anyone pockets anything: ritual Detect Magic, no
slot, eleven minutes. Then I sweep the pile — anything glowing, and what
school?"

Verbally: *"Joe reads the room the way other men read a contract:
slowly, twice, and with particular attention to what someone hoped he'd
skim."*
