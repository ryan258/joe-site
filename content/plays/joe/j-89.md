The dismissal is a defensive spell that costs nothing: Cow, removed
from the board until the board is safe.

### When to use it

- **Before** every foreseeable fight, blast, or trap check — 1 HP
  does not attend battles.
- Smuggling Cow through searches (a pocket dimension beats any
  pat-down), and shielding Cow from the *Detect Magic* sweep play 88
  fears.

### Mechanics

1. As a Magic action, Joe **temporarily dismisses** Cow to a pocket
   dimension; as a Magic action later, he recalls Cow to any
   unoccupied space within **30 feet**. No slot, no cost, no limit.
2. While parked: no link, no senses, no clock running on Cow's
   safety. The spirit waits, housed and content — the fiction the
   sheet gives it ("present / pocket dimension" is even the sheet's
   own temp-state vocabulary).
3. Doctrine ordering with the fight-opener: *dismiss Cow, then Mage
   Armor check, then initiative*. Recall afterward for the Help-flyby
   work (play 91) only if the field is genuinely clear of
   incidental-AoE risk.
4. The recall is also *deployment*: any unoccupied space within 30
   feet of Joe, placement Joe's choice — past the bars of a cell Joe
   stands beside, onto the ledge above, behind an enemy inside the
   radius. The exit from the pocket is a small teleport, and small
   teleports are never worthless.

### At the table

Mechanically: "Sounds like fighting through that door — Magic action,
Cow goes to the pocket dimension first. We'll have the reunion when
nothing in the room does area damage. And at the checkpoint
yesterday, note that they searched a man carrying no toy cow at all."

Verbally: *"Joe touches the worn little face once, and Mr. Big is
somewhere kinder for a while — a fold in the world shaped exactly
like being safe."*
