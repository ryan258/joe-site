Teleporting *forward* — because the enemy backline assumed the wizard
would never come to them.

### When to use it

- An enemy caster, commander, or archer nest is 30 feet past a front
  line Joe cannot walk through.
- The party's melee is stuck and someone must break the standoff.

### Mechanics

1. Bonus Action, teleport 30 feet to a seen unoccupied space *behind*
   the enemy line — then the Action cantrip lands on their squishiest
   asset at point-blank Advantage-free but unobstructed range.
2. Follow-the-map discipline: pick the arrival square with an exit —
   pillar cover, a doorway — because Joe just volunteered to be their
   problem.
3. This play spends next turn's safety: budget the return (*Misty Step*
   is one casting; the way back is walking or play 59's bigger brother).
4. Best executed the round the enemy commits — their reserve is what
   punishes it, so go when the reserve is already engaged.

### At the table

Mechanically: "Their commander's shouting orders behind the shield
wall, 25 feet back. Bonus Action Misty Step to the cart behind him,
Action Ray of Frost — hit, 2d8 cold and his Speed drops 10, so he can't
easily reach his own line's protection."

Verbally: *"The shield wall holds magnificently, protecting the empty
ground where the wizard used to matter."*
