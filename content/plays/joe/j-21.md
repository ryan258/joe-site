**Spellbook only.** Damage plus 10 feet of forced movement, centered on
Joe. Sometimes the 10 feet is the whole point.

### When to use it

- Enemies adjacent to Joe next to a ledge, pit, well, window, or open
  water.
- Breaking a grapple pile or clearing space around a downed ally.

### Mechanics

1. 1st-level, Action, self: a **15-foot cube** originating from Joe.
   Each creature in it makes a Constitution save vs DC 15.
2. Failure: **2d8 thunder** and pushed **10 feet** straight away from
   Joe. Success: half damage, no push.
3. Unsecured objects fully in the cube are also pushed 10 feet, and the
   spell emits a boom audible **300 feet** away (see play 23).
4. Upcast: +1d8 per slot level. The push does not scale; the push was
   always the product.
5. Falling damage from the shove (1d6 per 10 feet) is a bonus the enemy
   rolls, not Joe.

### At the table

Mechanically: "Both cultists are between me and the shaft. Thunderwave —
Constitution save DC 15 or they take 2d8 thunder and get pushed 10 feet
back. That's into the shaft."

Verbally: *"Joe claps his hands once, like a man closing a ledger, and
the air itself files the two of them under the floor."*
