The kit sorts cleanly: what needs sight, what needs a point, what
needs nothing.

### When to use it

- Something unseen is in the room — heard, felt, or deduced — and the
  party is swinging at Disadvantage against guesses.

### Mechanics

1. **Needs a visible target — jammed**: the wand (*Magic Missile*
   wants a visible creature), *Ray of Frost*/*Fire Bolt* attacks (roll
   at Disadvantage against an unseen target's position), *Counterspell*
   (must see the casting).
2. **Needs only a point in space — works**: *Fireball*, *Ice Storm*,
   *Thunderwave*, *Burning Hands* target points and areas, not sights.
   Saturating the square the footprints end in is full-damage legal.
3. **Doesn't care at all — shines**: *Web* and *Grease* can control an
   invisible creature, but neither condition ends Invisibility. Silk,
   grease, dust, and water may reveal its location at the DM's ruling;
   Restrained still cancels its attack Advantage.
4. Detection first: dust, flour, water underfoot; Cow's hearing and
   Fey senses (ask the DM what the link feels). *Detect Magic* senses
   magical effects within 30 feet, but its aura is visible only around
   a creature or object Joe can already see.
5. Doctrine: one area spell to flush, control to pin, then normal
   targeting once "invisible" degrades to "outlined."

### At the table

Mechanically: "The dust jumped by the doorway — Web fills that corner,
anchored floor to lintel. If it's in there, the silk shows us where,
Restrained, and we've traded its Advantage away. Then Fireball has a
point to aim at."

Verbally: *"You can't see it. Joe doesn't try. He fills the room with
things it cannot help but touch, and reads the shape of the
disturbance like handwriting."*
