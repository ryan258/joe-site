Someone else's fire: the Evoker's professional opinion of an
uncontrolled blaze, applied to getting everyone out.

### When to use it

- Rescues and escapes through structure fires; arson scenes
  while they're still scenes.

### Mechanics

1. Smoke kills first and blinds second: low is air, high is
   lies — and **Gust of Wind** (swapped in, play 44) is a
   60-foot corridor of breathable, *visible* passage, re-aimed
   each turn as a Bonus Action. In a burning building it's the
   best spell on the sheet.
2. Structure reads are Investigation (+10): which beam sags,
   which floor's drumming hollow, where the fire *wants* to go
   next (fire climbs, follows draft, eats the dry) — the Study
   action, at speed, is the difference between the exit and
   the news.
3. The kit's odd fits: *Web* is flammable (never under the
   party here); *Grease* likewise suspect; **Thunderwave blows
   doors** off jammed frames (objects pushed — the stuck door
   is an object) and *Mage Hand* opens the hot latch nobody's
   palm should (play 16's logic, thermal edition).
4. Fire Bolt has one job inside: **backburn and breach** —
   igniting a controlled vent path is real firefighting (DM
   physics), but mostly Joe's fire stays holstered in a fire;
   the professional carries water discipline in a flood.
5. Cow does not enter (1 HP, smoke, updrafts — parked, play
   89); *Arcane Eye* (play 63) is fireproof, smokeproof
   reconnaissance if the slot's standing.

### At the table

Mechanically: "Stay under the smoke line and behind me — Gust
of Wind is our hallway: sixty feet of air, and I re-aim it at
each turning. Investigation says the east stair's gone —
floor's drumming — so it's the servant's passage. That door's
jammed and hot: Thunderwave takes it off its hinges from six
feet back."

Verbally: *"The house is being eaten in great orange
paragraphs, and through it walks a man who has read fire's
entire bibliography — holding open, with one steady breath of
conjured wind, the single sentence of air his friends walk
inside."*
