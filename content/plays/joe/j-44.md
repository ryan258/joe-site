**Spellbook only.** The counterspell for weather, fog, and poison air.

### When to use it

- *Fog Cloud*, *Stinking Cloud*-style vapors, natural fog, or smoke is
  blinding the party or poisoning the room.
- An enemy is using obscurement to break Joe's line of sight (which
  jams *Misty Step*, *Counterspell*, and every attack roll he owns).

### Mechanics

1. 2nd-level, Action, self, **Concentration up to 1 minute**: a line of
   wind **60 feet long, 10 feet wide** blasts from Joe.
2. The line **disperses gas and vapor** and extinguishes candles,
   torches, and unprotected flames in it.
3. When cast, each creature in the line must make a Strength save vs DC 15 or
   be pushed 15 feet away from Joe along the line. A creature that ends its turn
   in the line must also make this save. In the line, moving closer to Joe costs
   double movement (2 feet per 1 foot).
4. As a **Bonus Action** on later turns, Joe can change the line's
   direction — one casting can sweep a whole room clear quadrant by
   quadrant.
5. Prep reality: nobody prepares this daily; swap it in when the enemy
   or environment is known to fight with air (play 56's pivot).

### At the table

Mechanically: "Their caster's fog is blinding the whole front line —
Gust of Wind straight through it, concentrating. The 60-foot line
disperses the fog in its path, and I'll Bonus Action sweep it left next
turn."

Verbally: *"Joe exhales, and sixty feet of the enemy's carefully
purchased confusion files itself out the far window."*
