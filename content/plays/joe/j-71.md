One reroll per day, refunded every dawn. Hoarding it has a price;
wasting it has a bigger one.

### When to use it

- Priority one: a **failed Concentration save** holding the spell the
  fight depends on (*Web*, *Fly* at altitude).
- Priority two: the failed save that ends Joe's turn-taking (*Hold
  Person*, a dragon's Wisdom effects).
- Priority three: the clutch Investigation or the Fireball-dodge — the
  d20 that decides a scene.

### Mechanics

1. Heroic Inspiration: when Joe makes **any d20 Test**
   (attack roll, check, or save), he can expend it to **reroll**, and
   must use the new roll.
2. Human Resourceful: Joe regains it whenever he finishes a **Long
   Rest** — it is income, not treasure. An unspent Inspiration at the
   next dawn was a free reroll donated to nobody (it does not stack;
   he can only hold one).
3. Decision rule: spend it on any failure whose cost exceeds "one
   turn." Never spend it on damage rolls it can't touch or checks the
   party can retry.
4. Announce the bank at the fight's start (to yourself): "Inspiration
   is for the Web save." Pre-commitment beats mid-round bargaining.

### At the table

Mechanically: "The hit's 14 damage, so my Concentration save is DC 10
— rolled a 7, that drops the Web... no. Heroic Inspiration: reroll.
15. The Web holds."

Verbally: *"For one measured breath, Joe declines the universe's first
draft and requests the second."*
