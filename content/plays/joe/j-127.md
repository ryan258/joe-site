Dead magic, antimagic fields, suppression wards: the room where
the sheet stops working — and what's left is the man.

### When to use it

- Warded vaults and prison blocks, beholder-adjacent rumors,
  any place Arcana or a fizzled cantrip announces "magic is
  off here."

### Mechanics

1. **Detection before commitment**: a *Detect Magic* sweep
   reads *no auras where auras should be* — a nothing that is
   itself a finding (play 30's logic); a sacrificial cantrip at
   the threshold is the cheap canary. Map the zone's *edge* —
   the fight wants to happen on Joe's side of it.
2. What stops inside: spells, ongoing spells (*Mage Armor* —
   AC drops to 12), the wand, the *familiar bond's* traffic
   (DM's call — assume Cow's link crosses the boundary poorly
   or not at all; brief Cow *before*, play 87's advance-
   instruction habit). What doesn't stop: daggers (+5, play
   67), oil (play 69), Investigation (+10), the plan.
3. Fight the doorway, not the room: enemies inside a null zone
   are safe from Fireball — enemies *at its edge* are not, and
   most null-dwellers must eventually come to the edge. Joe's
   entire kit works at 60–150 feet from a line on the floor;
   patience is a siege engine.
4. If the job's *inside*: it's a mundane heist — the full
   non-magical column (Stealth +5, Sleight of Hand +5, forged
   papers from play 84, oil, rope, wits), with magic staged at
   the boundary for the exit. Joe without spells is still the
   party's best detective; the zone never turned *that* off.

### At the table

Mechanically: "My test cantrip died at the third flagstone —
the ward starts there, and I've chalked the line. So: inside,
I'm a man with a knife, a lamp, and the best Investigation in
the province, and that's sufficient — the vault work is hands
anyway. Everything that hates us gets fought *here*, at the
chalk, where my whole spellbook resumes."

Verbally: *"Past the chalk line, Joe is nobody's archmage —
just a dry, careful man who reads rooms for a living. It says
something that the guards, given the choice, still preferred
him on the other side."*
