Not a combat play — the reason the party finds the door, the trap, and
the lie before they cost anything. Cow scouts, *Arcane Eye* penetrates,
Investigation +10 interprets.

### When to use it

- Before committing the party to any unknown space: buildings, ruins,
  camps, negotiations.
- When evidence exists but its meaning doesn't — Joe's job is
  interpretation, per `Joe's Investigation Framework.md`.

### Mechanics

1. **Cow scouts** (already summoned; re-summoning costs a ritual hour
   and one 10 GP incense portion — two are in inventory). Owl stat
   block: Fly 60 feet, Darkvision 120 feet, Flyby means no Opportunity
   Attacks when leaving reach. Within 100 feet Joe can use his Bonus Action to
   see and hear through Cow's senses until the start of his next turn (and Joe
   is no longer blinded/deafened to his own surroundings). Cow reports in emotions,
   images, and remembered sensations — fragments, not sentences.
2. **Cow assists**: in combat Cow cannot attack but can take the Help
   action — fly in, grant an ally Advantage on their next attack, and
   Flyby out untouched. Weigh it against the risk: Cow has 1 HP.
3. **Arcane Eye** for what Cow can't survive: 4th-level slot,
   Concentration up to 1 hour, an invisible floating eye with Darkvision
   30 feet that moves 30 feet per action and squeezes through a 1-inch
   gap. Map an entire complex from safety.
4. **Interpret**: Study action, Intelligence (Investigation) at **+10**
   (Expertise). D&D Beyond displays Passive Investigation 20, but the
   2024 rules define no automatic Passive Investigation floor — it is
   a convention a DM may adopt, never something Joe claims. State
   intent and method, then roll when asked.
5. Supporting cast: *Detect Magic* and *Alarm* as rituals (no slot, no
   preparation needed), *Find Familiar* as a ritual to re-summon Cow.

### At the table

Mechanically: "Cow flies a slow circuit of the courtyard at rooftop
height — Darkvision 120. I'll use my Bonus Action to look through Cow's eyes
and I want to know entrances, sentries, and light sources. When we find
the study, I'm using the Study action on the desk — Investigation +10."

Verbally: *"Joe sets the little toy cow on the windowsill and something
unfolds out of it that was never quite inside it. 'Go look,' he says,
gently. 'Just look.'"*

State intent and method, never "I search for clues." The framework
document is the long-form guide; this play is its field application.
