Wings plus the link: small objects and simple messages, delivered over
walls the party can't cross.

### When to use it

- Moving the key, the note, the antidote vial, the signet across a
  siege line, a river, a locked estate — anything an owl can carry
  and a window admits.
- Contacting a separated ally when *Message* (120 feet) can't reach
  but a flight can.

### Mechanics

1. Cow flies **60 feet per round** — a quarter mile in under a minute
   — carrying what an owl's talons manage (ounces: notes, vials,
   keys, rings; the DM's ruling is the load limit).
2. Beyond the 100-foot link Cow operates on *instructions given in
   advance*: brief simply, in images — "the window with the red
   curtain; the woman with the grey braid; drop, return." Complex
   contingencies do not survive translation.
3. The recipient needs to *expect* an owl, or the delivery is an
   incident — arrange the signal beforehand (play 11 sets it up
   inside 120 feet; prior agreement sets it up anywhere).
4. Risk pricing: Cow has **1 HP** and Flyby only beats melee reach —
   an archer or a hawk prices the route. Night flights, high
   approaches, and play 33's incense ledger (two portions) frame the
   go/no-go.

### At the table

Mechanically: "The cell window's forty feet up the seaward wall and
we're not. Cow takes the cell key up at full fly speed, night
approach, drops it through the bars to the hands that are expecting
it, and comes straight back. Total exposure: about ninety seconds."

Verbally: *"A small shape crosses the harbor dark, unremarked among
the gulls — a toy cow's dream of wings, running an honest errand
through a dishonest town."*
