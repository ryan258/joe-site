The fighter who shoves and drags is a delivery service for Joe's
terrain. Sign the compact at camp, not mid-fight.

### When to use it

- An ally who grapples/shoves (Athletics-heavy martial) plus any
  standing Joe control zone: Web, Grease, oil, the ledge.

### Mechanics

1. The compact's clauses, agreed in advance: Joe's zones go where
   the grappler can *use* them (near the front line's edge, not
   across the map); the grappler's shoves point *into* them (a
   shove is 5–10 feet of enemy relocation — into Grease's save,
   into Web's cube, off play 118's ledge, onto play 69's oil).
2. The two-step: fighter shoves **Prone**, *then* the enemy's in
   Grease — standing costs half Speed *and* re-triggers the
   square's economics; some enemies simply stop getting up,
   which is a polite word for what's happening.
3. Joe's end of the bargain: **Sculpt Spells means the grappler
   works inside Joe's AoE rent-free** (plays 1, 74) — the fighter
   who fears no friendly Fireball grapples in places no sane
   martial stands. That trust is the compact's real currency.
4. Web note for the grappler's own hands: dragging a body *into*
   the cube means entering it — allies save vs Joe's own silk
   like anyone. Throw, shove, or herd; don't walk it in.

### At the table

Mechanically: "Brenna, tonight's compact: my Grease lands ten
feet behind wherever you're fighting — your shoves have an
address now. You put them down in it; the floor keeps them
down; and my Fireballs will never once include you. Standard
terms."

Verbally: *"The captain shoves the mercenary backward with a
crash — into ten feet of floor that Joe prepared like a legal
filing — and the man spends the rest of the fight learning to
stand in front of witnesses."*
