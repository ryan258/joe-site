Martial burst rounds are a resource. Joe's control decides *when*
they happen and *whether* they land.

### When to use it

- An ally has a big once-per-fight round loaded (smite money,
  action surge, rage opener) and is waiting on a clean shot at
  the right target.

### Mechanics

1. Joe manufactures the window: **Web the target** (Restrained:
   Advantage on attack rolls against the target, and the target can't leave the
   melee — plays 2, 129) or **frost the target's exits** (play
   8's kiting, reversed: the enemy stays in the nova's reach) —
   then calls it: "window open, this round."
2. The Counterspell umbrella covers the window (play 4): the
   round the paladin commits everything is exactly the round
   the enemy caster spends *Shield*, *Blur*, or worse — Joe's
   reaction is booked against it, announced in advance.
3. Sculpt-secured approach: the nova-martial charging through
   Joe's planned Fireball lane does it fearlessly (play 130's
   currency again) — the burst and the blast can share a round
   *and* a target when the count's declared.
4. The discipline is *waiting*: a window forced early (Web on a
   passed save, umbrella spent on a cantrip) wastes the ally's
   once-per-fight — build the window on Joe's *good* round,
   not his first one.

### At the table

Mechanically: "Hold the smites one round — my Web lands first.
...Failed save: he's Restrained, Advantage on everything, he's
not going anywhere, and my reaction is parked on his shaman's
first syllable. Window's open. Spend it all."

Verbally: *"The paladin waits, hammer low, while Joe sets the
table — silk on the warlord, a held word against the shaman —
and then the window opens and holy violence files through it
all at once, on time, uninterrupted."*
