The party has slept safely every night since Joe learned this ritual.

### When to use it

- Every rest in unsecured territory. This is standing policy, not a
  decision — like *Mage Armor* (play 34).
- Watch schedules built around it let the party's low-Perception
  members (including Joe, +3) take safer shifts.

### Mechanics

1. Ritual (no slot, no preparation; 1 minute + 10 ritual), duration **8
   hours** — exactly one Long Rest. Warded area: a **20-foot cube**
   within 30 feet; cover the approach, not the camp.
2. Choose **mental** or **audible** when casting. Mental: a ping wakes
   Joe if he is within 1 mile — it wakes him from sleep. Audible: a
   hand-bell sound for 10 seconds, heard within 60 feet, wakes everyone.
3. It triggers on any Tiny-or-larger creature touching or entering the
   cube; designate known creatures (the party) to ignore.
4. Two castings (22 minutes of ritual time) cover both approaches to
   most camps. Cow on overwatch (play 86) covers the third dimension.

### At the table

Mechanically: "Before we sleep: ritual Alarm, mental version, on the
20-foot cube covering the trailhead — party designated as ignored. It
pings me awake if anything Tiny or larger crosses it in the next eight
hours."

Verbally: *"Joe strings nothing, hides nothing, hammers nothing. He
simply tells that stretch of trail to remember him if anyone walks it,
and goes to sleep."*
