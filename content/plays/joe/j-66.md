Multiple guaranteed hits into a concentrating caster: the cheapest
concentration-breaker Joe owns.

### When to use it

- An enemy caster is concentrating on the fight-shaping spell (*Hold
  Person* on the fighter, *Spirit Guardians*, a summon) and
  *Counterspell* is too late or spent.

### Mechanics

1. Each dart that damages a concentrating caster forces a Constitution
   save (DC 10, or half the damage if higher — darts deal ~3.5, so DC
   10). **Table ruling required**: darts landing simultaneously may be
   ruled one combined save instead of one per dart. Ask once, note the
   answer, plan accordingly.
2. Under the per-dart ruling: 3 darts = 3 saves at DC 10. A +3 CON
   caster fails each roughly 30% — about a 2-in-3 chance at least one
   save breaks the spell, from one charge, with no roll from Joe.
3. Under the combined ruling: one save vs 3d4+3 total (still DC 10) —
   weaker; consider *Scorching Ray* (play 42) whose three rays are
   unambiguously separate.
4. Add charges to add darts if the spell being held is worth 2–3
   charges. It usually is.

### At the table

Mechanically: "Their priest is concentrating on that guardian spell —
wand, two charges, four darts, all into her. DM, we ruled darts force
separate saves: that's four DC 10 Constitution saves, and the spell
drops on any failure."

Verbally: *"Four lights strike the priest like a run of hard questions,
and somewhere in the middle of them, her prayer loses its place."*
