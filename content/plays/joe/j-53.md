The rescue cast: an ally is Charmed, Held, or bound by a spell, and Joe
ends it from 120 feet.

### When to use it

- An ally is under *Hold Person*, *Charm*-effects, *Slow*, or any
  ongoing spell — this beats damaging the enemy concentrator when the
  concentrator is protected.
- Post-combat: curses-that-are-spells, magical sleep, bindings.

### Mechanics

1. Target the **ally** (creature) with *Dispel Magic*: every spell of
   3rd level or lower on them ends outright. *Hold Person* (2nd) ends,
   no roll, from 120 feet.
2. Contrast with the other route — breaking the enemy's Concentration
   via damage — which is probabilistic (their save) and needs targeting
   the caster. Dispel is deterministic and needs targeting the friend.
3. Multiple effects on one ally all end at once (≤3rd), which is the
   bargain of the fight.
4. It does not cure conditions that are not spells: poison, disease,
   petrification, and curses from items are other tools' problems. Name
   the source before spending the slot — Arcana +7 again.
