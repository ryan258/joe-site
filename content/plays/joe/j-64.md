The Wand of Magic Missiles is a spell slot bank with its own currency.
The doctrine is written on the character sheet; this is the play.

### When to use it

- Reliable damage needed without spending a slot — the wand's whole
  reason to exist.
- Per `joe-character.md`: 1 charge normally, 2–3 for urgent burst,
  preserve the final charge except to prevent a worse outcome.

### Mechanics

1. Action: expend **1 charge** to cast *Magic Missile* — **three
   darts**, 1d4+1 force each, range 120 feet, **auto-hit**, split or
   stacked among visible targets.
2. Extra charges: +1 dart per additional charge (3 charges = 5 darts).
3. Capacity **7 charges**; regains **1d6+1 daily at dawn**. Expected
   income ≈ 4.5/day — spending 3–4 charges a day is sustainable
   forever.
4. The destruction clause: if the **last** charge is expended, roll a
   d20 — on a **1**, the wand crumbles. Hence doctrine: the seventh
   charge is not money, it is the wand's life; spend it only against a
   worse loss.
5. No attack roll, no save, no Concentration check to use: it works
   while **Grappled**, while **Prone**, and while out of slots. It
   does *not* work while Joe is Blinded — each dart needs a creature
   Joe can see — unless some other sense lets him see the target.

### At the table

Mechanically: "I'm saving slots — wand, one charge: three darts, all
into the ogre, 3d4+3 force, no roll needed. That leaves four charges,
and I don't touch the last one."

Verbally: *"Three points of light leave the wand in file, polite and
punctual, and arrive exactly where Joe sent them. He is already
re-counting what remains."*
