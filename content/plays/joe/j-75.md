The subclass pays a spellbook dividend on a schedule. Plan the picks
like the milestones they are.

### When to use it

- At every level that unlocks a new slot tier: the next is **level 9**
  (5th-level slots), then 11 (6th). One free Evocation spell each, plus
  the two-per-level normal picks to plan around them.

### Mechanics

1. Evocation Savant: whenever Joe gains access to a **new level of
   spell slots**, add one **Evocation** Wizard spell of a castable
   level to the spellbook **free**. Ledger so far: *Burning Hands*,
   *Scorching Ray*, *Gust of Wind*, *Fireball*, *Ice Storm* (the
   count `joe-character.md` documents).
2. Level 9's free pick is 5th-level Evocation: *Cone of Cold* (the
   sculpted 60-foot cone, protect 6) and *Bigby's Hand* (Evocation!
   control + damage + Concentration) are the shortlist to research
   before the level, not after.
3. Free picks should buy what gold-copying prices highest: copying a
   5th-level spell costs ~50 GP and 5 hours — the Savant pick is worth
   its level in gold, literally. Spend the *paid* picks on
   non-Evocation utility Savant will never cover (*Counterspell* was
   this; the next are things like *Banishment*-alikes).
4. Update `joe-character.md`'s spellbook section the session the pick
   lands — the project's audit history (see `findings.md` #4) is why
   the count stays trusted.
