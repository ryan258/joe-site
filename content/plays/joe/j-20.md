Navigation and coordination symbols that expire on their own.

### When to use it

- Mapping a maze, marking cleared rooms, or leaving route signs for a
  trailing ally.
- Marking targets or doors during a timed operation.

### Mechanics

1. Prestidigitation makes a small mark or symbol appear on an object or
   surface for **1 hour** — and up to three at once.
2. Convention beats improvisation: agree on symbols in advance (arrow =
   route, X = cleared, ! = hazard, C = Cow checked it).
3. The 1-hour expiry is a feature — the marks betray nothing to anyone
   following more than an hour behind.
4. For permanent records, ink and parchment (play 83's supplies) after
   the fact; the marks are working memory, the journal is storage.

### At the table

Mechanically: "At each junction I mark the left post with a glowing
arrow, three marks running at a time, renewing as we go. If we have to
run, we follow the arrows out."

Verbally: *"Behind the party, soft sigils glow at knee height, patient
as breadcrumbs that know to disappear."*
