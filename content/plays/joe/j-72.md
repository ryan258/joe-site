Three spells that cost nothing but minutes. The party's logistics run
on them.

### When to use it

- Always, structurally: *Detect Magic* on finds (play 29), *Alarm* on
  rests (play 31), *Find Familiar* on Cow's maintenance (play 33).
- Whenever the party debates "is it worth a slot?" — for these three,
  the answer is that the question is void.

### Mechanics

1. Ritual Adept (Wizard 1): Joe can cast any ritual-tagged spell **from
   his spellbook** as a ritual **without preparing it**, at +10 minutes
   casting time and **no spell slot**.
2. The current ritual shelf: *Detect Magic* (10 min total), *Alarm*
   (11 min — its base casting time is 1 minute), *Find Familiar*
   (70 min, consumes 10 GP incense).
3. Time is the real cost: ten-odd minutes is safe in a secured room and an
   eternity in a live dungeon. The doctrine: ritual when the clock is
   friendly, spend the slot when it is not (*Detect Magic* casts in
   one Action for a 1st-level slot — if prepared, which it currently
   is not; a slot-cast requires preparing it first).
4. Every future ritual scribed into the book joins the shelf for free
   — weight ritual-tagged spells accordingly when copying (play 75's
   planning).

### At the table

Mechanically: "We hold the room ten minutes? Then it's free: ritual
Detect Magic, and while it runs I'll ritual-cast Alarm on the door
behind us. Zero slots. The day's magic budget hasn't started being
spent."

Verbally: *"Given ten quiet minutes, Joe produces magic the way a
careful clerk produces receipts: unhurried, unglamorous, and somehow
always covering exactly what was needed."*
