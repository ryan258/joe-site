The budget lockdown: one 1st-level slot and a cantrip put one enemy
functionally out of the fight.

### When to use it

- One dangerous melee enemy, not worth *Web*'s Concentration or a
  bigger slot — the duelist, the ogre, the knight-commander's second.
- Slots are scarce and rounds are not.

### Mechanics

1. *Grease* under the target (play 25): Dexterity save vs DC 15 or
   **Prone**, in difficult terrain, no Concentration.
2. Each round after: *Ray of Frost* (+7, 2d8 cold, **Speed -10**).
3. The arithmetic of standing still: standing from Prone costs half
   their Speed; the remaining half is halved again by difficult
   terrain and shrunk by the frost. A Speed-30 enemy who fails the
   Grease save has roughly **5 feet** of real movement — and ending
   their turn in the square is another save.
4. Potent Cantrip keeps the frost paying half even on misses; the
   wand substitutes when the d20 goes cold. Total cost per additional
   round: one cantrip.

### At the table

Mechanically: "Grease under the champion — failed, he's Prone in it.
Every round he spends half his movement standing, half of what's left
wading, minus ten from Ray of Frost. He's a statue that takes 2d8 a
turn. Move on to the others; he'll keep."

Verbally: *"The duel everyone feared becomes a man learning to skate,
gravely, in front of his own army, while frost files him smaller."*
