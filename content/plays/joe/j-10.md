Starting-gear oil turns a cantrip into a cantrip and a half.

### When to use it

- A tough single target worth two party actions or two of Joe's rounds.
- Before a fight starts — oiling an ambush square is free.

### Mechanics

1. Throwing a flask of oil is an attack (improvised, range 20/60): on a
   hit the target is covered in oil for 1 minute.
2. If the oiled target takes **any fire damage** before the oil dries,
   it takes an extra **5 fire damage** from the burning oil.
3. Joe's follow-up *Fire Bolt* (2d10 + 5) triggers it, and so does any
   ally's fire damage — hand the flask to whoever has the better throw.
4. Alternative: pour oil on a 5-foot square; ignited, it burns for 2
   rounds, dealing 5 fire damage to any creature entering or ending its
   turn there.

### At the table

Mechanically: "Round one, Tam splashes the oil flask on the troll. Round
two, my Fire Bolt hits — 2d10 plus 5 from the burning oil."

Verbally: *"Joe watches the oil soak into the troll's hide with the
patience of a man reading the last line of a proof. Then he supplies
the flame."*
