The action-economy rule, mastered: reposition and still hurt someone.

### When to use it

- Every turn Joe needs both a teleport and damage.
- Escaping melee while punishing the enemy who cornered him.

### Mechanics

1. The 2024 rule: on a turn Joe casts a spell as a **Bonus Action**, his
   Action that turn cannot cast a **leveled** spell — but a **cantrip**
   with a casting time of one action is fine.
2. Legal turn: *Misty Step* (Bonus Action) + *Fire Bolt* or *Ray of
   Frost* (Action) + full 30 feet of movement on top. Illegal turn:
   *Misty Step* + *Fireball*.
3. Sequencing freedom: Action and Bonus Action can come in either
   order — cantrip first from the bad position, then teleport out; or
   teleport to the sightline first, then shoot.
4. Ray of Frost as the pairing (Speed -10) makes the escape stick: Joe
   teleports 30 away and the pursuer walks 20.

### At the table

Mechanically: "Ray of Frost the knight first — hit, 2d8 cold, his Speed
drops 10. Now Bonus Action Misty Step 30 feet back through the portcullis
gap. That's legal: cantrip plus Bonus Action spell."

Verbally: *"Frost, then vacancy. The knight is left holding a charge
that arrives nowhere, ten feet slower than his pride."*
