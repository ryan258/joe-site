+7 to turn "something magical" into a proper noun with known rules.

### When to use it

- A spell effect, rune, construct, planar oddity, or magical phenomenon
  needs identifying — *before* the party touches, dispels, or fights
  it.
- Paired with *Detect Magic*'s school report (play 29): school + Arcana
  is usually the full name.

### Mechanics

1. Intelligence (Arcana) at **+7**: recall magical theory, identify
   spells from their effects and components, know traditions, planes,
   and famous workings.
2. Feed it inputs: the school from *Detect Magic*, the components seen
   (play 4's trigger discipline — what did the caster say and hold?),
   the residue Studied at +10. Each observed detail is Advantage-bait
   or DC-reduction the DM can honor.
3. Downstream spending: the name prices the response — *Dispel Magic*
   auto-ends ≤3rd (play 52), *Counterspell* priorities (play 58), or
   "we do not fight this; play 61."
4. Joe's Scholar expertise is Investigation, not Arcana — on ambiguous
   evidence-shaped questions ("what does this ritual chamber *do*?"),
   ask whether Investigation's method-based framing applies; +10 beats
   +7 when both honestly fit.

### At the table

Mechanically: "The circle's aura read as Conjuration, the chalk mix
smells of sulfur and silver — Arcana, plus seven: I want the specific
ritual, its target, and what breaks it. 21."

Verbally: *"Joe circles the diagram once and names it like an old
acquaintance he'd hoped had left the country."*
