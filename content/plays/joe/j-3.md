Joe is a d6 wizard who is genuinely hard to kill: *Mage Armor* AC 15,
*Shield* to 20 as a reaction, *Mirror Image* on top when focused.

### When to use it

- *Mage Armor*: cast before any day where danger is reasonably expected
  (8-hour duration, 1st-level slot). This is standing policy, not a
  decision.
- *Shield*: reaction, when an attack roll hits with a margin of 5 or
  less against current AC — ask the DM "does a 17 hit?" and react to the
  answer. Also auto-negates *Magic Missile*.
- *Mirror Image*: when Joe is the target of the round — multiple enemies
  closing, or protecting Concentration on *Web* or *Fly*.

### Mechanics

1. *Shield*: Reaction when hit by an attack or targeted by *Magic
   Missile*. +5 AC (15 → 20) until the start of Joe's next turn,
   including against the triggering attack.
2. The **first** *Shield* each day is free: Magic Initiate (Wizard)
   grants one casting per Long Rest with no slot. It is always prepared
   and never uses one of the 11 Wizard preparation slots. After the free
   one, spend 1st-level slots.
3. *Mirror Image*: Action, three duplicates, no Concentration, 1 minute.
   Each time an attack roll hits Joe, roll a d6 for each remaining duplicate.
   If any of the d6s rolls a 3 or higher, one duplicate is hit instead of Joe
   and is destroyed (duplicates have AC 12).
4. Order of operations under pressure: *Mirror Image* on your turn,
   *Shield* on their turn. Because *Mirror Image* triggers only when hit,
   you roll to redirect first; if the attack is not redirected to a duplicate,
   you can still use *Shield* (reaction) to boost your AC to 20 and block it.

### At the table

Mechanically: "Reaction — *Shield*. That's the free Magic Initiate
casting, no slot. My AC is 20 until my next turn, so the 18 misses."

Verbally: *"The axe stops a hand's width from Joe's face, ringing off
air gone hard as glass. He notes the angle of the blade like a man
reading a sentence he expected."*

Track the free casting explicitly ("that's my free one for today") so
slot accounting stays honest.
