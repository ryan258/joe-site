Darkvision 120, wings, and a mind on Joe's frequency: the perfect
sentry, parked over the camp.

### When to use it

- Every night watch in open country, layered with *Alarm* (play 31):
  the ritual covers the trail, Cow covers everything the cube
  doesn't.
- Watches where the party's low-Perception members (Joe, +3) hold a
  shift — Cow is the shift's real eyes.

### Mechanics

1. Cow roosts high with **Darkvision 120 feet** and a raptor's senses
   — the Owl stat block's watch numbers beat every human in the party
   at night.
2. The link carries **within 100 feet**: Cow wakes Joe with emotion
   and image — *alarm, direction, size* — faster than any shout, and
   silently, which matters when the answer is "let them pass."
3. Joe can spend a Bonus Action to see through Cow's eyes for the detailed
   look (the borrowed-eyes protocol, play 90, governs the risk).
4. Housekeeping: keep the roost inside 100 feet of Joe's bedroll;
   agree the image-vocabulary in advance (*fire = urgent, water =
   moving past, stone = holding still*) — a language of remembered
   sensations is the bond's native grammar, per the sheet.

### At the table

Mechanically: "Watch order, with amendments: Alarm covers the trail,
Cow takes the high pine — darkvision 120 — and I keep my bedroll inside
the hundred-foot link. If anything crosses, I get the image before it
gets to fifty feet."

Verbally: *"The camp sleeps under two watchers: a spell that never
blinks, and a small Fey patience wearing feathers, dreaming with one
eye on the treeline."*
