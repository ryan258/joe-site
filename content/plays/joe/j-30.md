The blocking materials are a measuring instrument.

### When to use it

- The sweep senses magic but the source is hidden — behind panels, under
  floors, inside chests, on a person.
- Hunting a hidden magical compartment, cache, or trap.

### Mechanics

1. *Detect Magic* is blocked by 1 foot of stone, 1 inch of metal, thin
   lead, or 3 feet of wood — so a signal that appears and disappears as
   Joe walks maps the *edges* of the blocking material.
2. Grid-walk the room: sense at 30 feet is a sphere around Joe; where
   the aura cuts out, something thick (or lead-lined) intervenes. A
   lead-lined drawer is itself a finding.
3. Cross-reference with Investigation (+10): the aura says *where* and
   *what school*; the Study action says how the compartment opens and
   whether opening it is a trap.
4. Concentration is 10 minutes — one casting covers a whole floor of a
   house at a deliberate walking pace.

### At the table

Mechanically: "The aura vanishes when I stand here and returns two steps
east — something's blocking line to the source. I'm mapping where it
cuts out, then Study action on that wall section, Investigation plus
ten."

Verbally: *"Joe paces the study like a surveyor, and the house
reluctantly confesses the shape of its hollow places."*
