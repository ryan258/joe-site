The duel inside the duel: protecting Joe's big spell from their
reaction.

### When to use it

- An enemy caster with a visible reaction habit is standing within 60
  feet while Joe casts the spell the round depends on.
- The enemy counterspells Joe's *Fireball* — and Joe's reaction is
  still unspent.

### Mechanics

1. Joe casts *Fireball* (Action). The enemy caster reacts with their
   *Counterspell* targeting Joe. Joe now reacts with **his**
   *Counterspell* targeting *their* counterspell — legal: their
   counterspell is a spell being cast within 60 feet.
2. 2024 resolution: the enemy makes a **Constitution save vs DC 15**;
   on a failure their *Counterspell* fizzles and Joe's *Fireball*
   lands.
3. Cost accounting: Joe spends the Action spell *and* a 3rd-level
   reaction to push one spell through — worth it when the sculpted
   Fireball is the round, not worth it for a *Grease*.
4. Prevention is cheaper: cast the big spell from *beyond 60 feet* of
   the enemy caster when possible (Fireball reaches 150) and their
   counterspell never triggers at all.

### At the table

Mechanically: "I cast Fireball — she counterspells — I counterspell her
counterspell. Constitution save, DC 15... failed. Her counter fizzles
and my Fireball resolves as cast, Sculpt on our two."
