A 120-foot whisper only the target hears, and they can whisper back.

### When to use it

- Coordinating a split party, an ambush, or a heist without sound.
- Feeding lines to the party's negotiator mid-conversation.
- Talking to someone under guard without the guards knowing.

### Mechanics

1. Cantrip, Action, range 120 feet. Point at a creature; it alone hears
   the message and can reply with a whisper only Joe hears.
2. It travels around corners and through obstructions — but is blocked
   by 1 foot of stone, 1 inch of common metal, a thin sheet of lead, or
   3 feet of wood.
3. Joe must know the target is there; he does not need to see it.
4. The verbal component is a whisper — Stealth (+5) covers the casting
   in quiet rooms; state that you are casting under your breath.

### At the table

Mechanically: "I cast Message at the guard captain across the plaza —
only she hears it: 'The east gate, at the bell. Come alone.' She can
whisper a reply only I hear."

Verbally: *"Joe's lips barely move. Somewhere across the plaza, a woman
stiffens as a quiet, precise voice arrives just behind her ear."*
