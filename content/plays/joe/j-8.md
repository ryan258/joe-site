Speed control as defense: an enemy at Speed 20 in difficult terrain
never reaches the backline.

### When to use it

- A melee bruiser is walking toward Joe or the party's ranged allies.
- Terrain, *Grease*, or distance already costs the enemy movement — the
  -10 stacks with all of it.

### Mechanics

1. Ranged spell attack +7, 60 feet, 2d8 cold; on a hit the target's
   Speed is reduced by 10 feet until the start of Joe's next turn.
2. Then move Joe's full 30 feet away. A Speed-20 enemy that must Dash to
   close gets no attack; one that attacks cannot close.
3. Potent Cantrip: even on a miss, half damage — the chip damage keeps
   coming while the enemy walks the treadmill.
4. Repeat every round. This play loses to ranged enemies; check what the
   target is holding first.

### At the table

Mechanically: "Ray of Frost, 17 to hit — 2d8 cold and his Speed drops by
10. Then I move my full 30 back. He can Dash or swing, not both."

Verbally: *"Frost crawls up the brute's legs, and Joe steps backward at
a scholar's unhurried pace — he has done the arithmetic of the man's
stride and found it short."*
