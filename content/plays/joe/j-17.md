Keys off hooks, evidence off desks, potions across battlefields.

### When to use it

- The object is visible, under 10 pounds, and walking to it is the
  dangerous part.
- Retrieving evidence without disturbing the scene around it.

### Mechanics

1. Cantrip, Action, 30 feet, 1 minute: the hand picks up the object and
   carries it back at 30 feet per Magic action.
2. It cannot pick locks or pockets by itself — a guarded or worn object
   is a Sleight of Hand problem (play 81), not a Mage Hand problem. An
   unattended key ring on a hook is exactly a Mage Hand problem.
3. Mid-combat: float the healing potion to the ally who needs it (they
   still spend their own Bonus Action to drink — play 68).
4. The hand is visible; in stealth, time the fetch to the patrol gap
   found by Cow (play 86).

### At the table

Mechanically: "The key ring is on the hook behind the sleeping jailer.
Mage Hand lifts it — under 10 pounds — and floats it back to me, 30 feet,
around the bars."

Verbally: *"The keys cross the room at reading pace, unhurried, as if
they had always intended to leave."*
