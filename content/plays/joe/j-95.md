**Spellbook only** (the *Lightning Bolt* half). The follow-up *Web*
was built for: massed damage into Restrained targets rolling their
saves at Disadvantage. Expect to trade the trap for the damage.

### When to use it

- The *Web* (play 2) caught two or more enemies and the fight's
  arithmetic wants them dead faster than escape-check attrition.
- The control has done its work — this is the cash-out, not the
  opener.

### Mechanics

1. Standing state: enemies **Restrained** in the web cube —
   **Disadvantage on Dexterity saves**.
2. *Lightning Bolt* (8d6, DC 15) down the line of them: each rolls
   the save at Disadvantage — the failure rate against DC 15 climbs
   toward certainty. Sculpt exempts up to 4 tangled allies (play 50).
3. The material clause: *Lightning Bolt* **ignites flammable objects
   in its line**, and webs are flammable — RAW, the bolt most likely
   burns the trap away (survivors take play 2's 2d4 burn and come
   free). Ask the DM once and note the ruling. Either way the timing
   is sound: the saves are rolled at Disadvantage *before* the webs
   burn — this is a finisher that spends the trap, not a loop.
4. Line them up when casting *Web* in the first place: a corridor web
   is *already* a Lightning Bolt target queue. The combo is decided
   at the control cast, one round before the damage cast — that is
   what "lead with control" buys (the sheet's own principle).
5. Joe holds the *Web* Concentration *through* his own bolt — no
   check needed (his casting doesn't damage him), just don't get hit
   between.

### At the table

Mechanically: "Three of them are Restrained in the web across the
corridor — which means Disadvantage on Dex saves. Lightning Bolt
straight down it: DC 15 at Disadvantage each, 8d6 — and yes, the bolt
ignites my own webs, so the trap burns. That's the trade; they rolled
at Disadvantage first."

Verbally: *"The silk holds them like specimens for exactly as long as
it needs to. Then the corridor turns white, the web burns to drifting
ash, and the argument is over either way."*
