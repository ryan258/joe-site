The investigator's field kit that weighs nothing.

### When to use it

- Every crime scene, ruin, and document Joe processes.
- Preserving or revealing physical evidence before it degrades.

### Mechanics

1. Cantrip; effects last up to 1 hour; up to **three** non-instantaneous
   effects active at once. Relevant modes: clean or soil a 1-cubic-foot
   object, chill/warm/flavor material, light or snuff a small flame,
   create a small mark or symbol, produce a minor sensory effect.
2. Clean the grime off an inscription before Study; chill a body or a
   sample to slow decay for the hour; snuff the lantern before the
   ambush; light the workroom candles without touching anything.
3. Mark exhibits: a small glowing symbol on each examined object keeps
   the scene ordered — A, B, C in floating sigils.
4. Then the real work: Study action, Investigation +10 (play 76). The
   cantrip prepares the evidence; it never replaces the check.

### At the table

Mechanically: "Prestidigitation: I clean the mud off the boot print's
edge, chill the wax seal so it stops softening, and put a small glowing
'1' over each. Now I use the Study action on the print — Investigation."

Verbally: *"Joe processes the room like a man shelving a library, each
clue washed, chilled, and numbered in soft light."*
