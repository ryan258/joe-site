Round one, pre-decided: the sixty seconds where fights are usually
won, spent by procedure instead of panic.

### When to use it

- Initiative just got rolled and nobody surprised anybody. (Ambushes
  run play 97 or the fight's own logic; this is the *default*.)

### Mechanics

1. **Read (free)**: count enemies, find the caster, find the cluster,
   find the chokepoint. Ten seconds of looking is the whole Evoker
   skill ceiling — geometry is the class resource (play 49).
2. **Declare the reaction policy** (play 58): caster present →
   Counterspell watch; else Shield watch.
3. **Cow's disposition** (plays 89/91): AoE risk → parked; clean
   field → first flyby queued for the martial's turn.
4. **The turn itself, by priority**: control that changes the fight
   (*Web* at the chokepoint — play 2) → damage that changes it
   (sculpted *Fireball* on the cluster, if the cluster is round-one
   ripe — play 1) → position + cantrip when neither is on (play 40's
   legs, play 6's floor). Round one almost never spends the 4th.
5. **Say the plan in one line** ("Web the door, my reaction's for
   their priest, Senna gets Cow's help on the ogre") — the script's
   real output is the party fighting one fight instead of four.

### At the table

Mechanically: "Initiative — okay, script: eight of them, one robe in
the back, nice tight file in the arch. Reaction's on the robe. Cow's
parked — that robe smells like AoE. My turn: Web the arch, front
four caught. Everyone else, the two loose ones are yours."

Verbally: *"While the raiders are still shouting themselves brave,
Joe has already read the room twice and filed it: exits, ranks,
one man in robes who is about to have a very supervised evening."*
