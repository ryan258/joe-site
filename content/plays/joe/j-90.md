Seeing through Cow is Joe's best sensor, activated in a moment.

### When to use it

- The detail moment: identifying the face at the window Cow flagged,
  reading the sentry count on the wall-walk, confirming the room is
  empty before play 62's teleport.
- Still best used from a safe position, even though it no longer renders
  Joe vulnerable to ambush.

### Mechanics

1. Within **100 feet** of Cow, Joe uses his **Bonus Action** to see through
   Cow's eyes and hear through Cow's ears until the start of his next
   turn — gaining the Owl's **Darkvision 120** and vantage.
2. No sensory blackout: under 2024 rules, Joe is **not** Blinded or Deafened to
   his own surroundings while sharing Cow's senses. He can process both
   environments simultaneously, making scouting far safer.
3. Protocol, therefore: though safer, Joe still communicates his focus
   ("Tam, I'm looking through Cow — thirty seconds") to keep the party coordinated.
   An ally's hand on his shoulder remains the physical contact cue, and Joe can
   end the connection instantly.
4. Rhythm for extended overwatch: look one round, surface one round —
   even without sensory loss, multitasking in a fight is distracting. For anything
   longer, *Arcane Eye* (play 63) exists precisely to see remotely without
   taxing your action economy.

### At the table

Mechanically: "Cow's circling the manor. Bonus Action: I'm seeing through
Cow until the start of my next turn. I still have my senses here, and I still
have my Action. ...Three men on the roof, crossbows, watching the gate, not the
garden. Surfacing."

Verbally: *"Joe's eyes go politely elsewhere, the look of a man
remembering a page — while eighty feet up, the night unrolls beneath
a Fey heartbeat, in detail no human eye is owed."*
