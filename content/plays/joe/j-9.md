The same slow, spent on someone else's pursuer.

### When to use it

- An ally is fleeing, carrying a downed friend, or breaking away from
  melee with a chaser on their heels.
- The party is disengaging and one enemy is fast enough to catch them.

### Mechanics

1. Target the pursuer, not the biggest threat: +7, 60 feet, 2d8 cold,
   Speed -10 until the start of Joe's next turn.
2. A pursuer at Speed 20 cannot catch an ally at Speed 30 who Dashes.
3. If two enemies pursue, slow the faster one and let terrain handle the
   other, or escalate to *Web* (play 2) or *Grease* (play 25).

### At the table

Mechanically: "Brenna's running — I put Ray of Frost on the wolf chasing
her. Hit: 2d8 cold and its Speed is 30 minus 10, so she outpaces it."

Verbally: *"'Keep running,' Joe says, conversationally, and the wolf's
paws crackle with rime mid-stride."*
