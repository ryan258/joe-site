**Spellbook only.** The same three rays, dealt across the room.

### When to use it

- Two or three weakened enemies each within one ray of dropping —
  cleanup without spending three turns.
- Finishing what the sculpted *Fireball* started.

### Mechanics

1. Same casting as play 42, but rays split among up to three targets:
   one ray each, +7, 2d6 fire.
2. The math of finishing: a ray averages 7 — aim it at enemies the
   *Fireball* left in the 1–7 HP band. Ask the table who looks
   "bloodied and staggering" before allocating.
3. Allocation happens as you cast — call all targets, then roll each
   attack. No re-aiming rays after seeing results (table-dependent;
   agree with the DM once and note it).
4. This is the round-two half of the classic one-two: play 1's Fireball
   softens the cluster, the spread rakes the survivors.

### At the table

Mechanically: "Three of them are barely standing after the Fireball —
Scorching Ray, one ray each: 18, 14, 20. That's 2d6 fire to each; I
expect that clears the room."

Verbally: *"Joe closes the engagement the way he closes an argument:
three short proofs, no remainder."*
