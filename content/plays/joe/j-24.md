**Spellbook only and component-ready** — the 50 GP diamond is bought and in
inventory, but Joe must prepare the spell before casting it. Once prepared,
this is the answer to resistances.

### When to use it

- The enemy resists fire and cold — Joe's cantrip types — and a slot is
  worth spending on single-target damage.
- Any fight where picking the damage type is the difference: acid,
  poison, thunder, or lightning on demand.

### Mechanics

1. 1st-level, Action, range 90 feet, ranged spell attack +7. Choose
   acid, cold, fire, lightning, poison, or thunder: **3d8** of it.
2. **The orb leaps**: if two or more of the damage d8s show the same
   number, the orb splits to a different target within 30 feet of the
   first — make a new attack roll and damage roll. Under 2024 rules,
   the orb can leap a maximum number of times equal to the level of the
   spell slot expended (so at 1st level, it can leap at most once). It can
   hit each target only once per casting.
3. Upcast: +1d8 damage per slot level, and the maximum number of leaps
   increases by 1 per slot level. More dice also make rolling matching numbers
   (and thus leaping) more likely.
4. Component: a diamond worth 50+ GP, **not consumed** — a one-time
   purchase, made and recorded in `joe-character.md`. Keep it on Joe's
   person, not in the party bag: no diamond, no *Chromatic Orb*.
5. Potent Cantrip does not apply — this is a leveled spell; a miss is a
   miss. That is the price of choosing the type.
