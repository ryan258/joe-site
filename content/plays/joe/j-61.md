Five hundred feet, no line of sight required, plus one passenger. The
emergency exit that ignores the map.

### When to use it

- Joe (and one other) must be elsewhere *now*: a collapsing room, a
  lost fight, a closing trap, a hostage in hand (see play 37's chain).
- Escaping Restrained, grappled, swallowed, or manacled — teleportation
  ignores all of it.

### Mechanics

1. 4th-level, Action, range **500 feet**: teleport to a spot Joe can
   **see**, can **visualize**, or can **describe by distance and
   direction** ("200 feet straight up" is legal; so is "the courtyard
   we crossed at noon").
2. Bring **one willing creature** of Joe's size or smaller, carrying
   its gear, within 5 feet.
3. The failure clause: arriving in a space occupied by a creature or
   object deals **4d6 force** to each traveler and the teleport
   **fails** in place. Describe destinations conservatively — known
   open ground, not "hopefully past the wall."
4. It is Joe's only no-line-of-sight movement: through the ceiling,
   out of the *Forcecage*-adjacent problem, past the flooded level.
5. One 4th-level slot — the same one as *Ice Storm* and *Arcane Eye*.
   Days with a known extraction risk are days that slot stays parked.

### At the table

Mechanically: "The ceiling's coming down and Tam's next to me —
Dimension Door: both of us to the ledge above the entrance, the one we
scouted this morning. I can visualize it exactly; it was empty rock."

Verbally: *"Joe takes Tam's arm like a gentleman suggesting they step
into the next room. The next room is four hundred feet away and
daylit."*
