**Spellbook only.** When the fight is already inside 15 feet, sweep it.

### When to use it

- Multiple weak enemies (rats, cultists, skeletons) inside cone range
  and no ally in the arc.
- Tight tunnels and small rooms where *Fireball*'s 20-foot radius would
  cook the party — the cone is aimable in a way the sphere is not.

### Mechanics

1. 1st-level, Action, self: a **15-foot cone**. Each creature in it
   makes a Dexterity save vs DC 15 — **3d6 fire** on a failure, half on
   a success. Flammable objects in the cone ignite.
2. Sculpt Spells works here too: protect up to **2** creatures (1 + 1)
   in the cone if an ally is engaged.
3. Upcast: +1d6 per level. Past 2nd level, *Fireball* is almost always
   the better spend if geometry allows it.
4. Prep reality: swap it in for confined-space days (sewers, mines,
   ship interiors) where *Fireball* keeps being unusable.

### At the table

Mechanically: "They're stacked in the tunnel mouth — Burning Hands, the
cone catches all four. Dexterity save DC 15, 3d6 fire, half on a
success. Tam's at the edge, so Sculpt Spells: he's untouched."

Verbally: *"Fire fans from Joe's spread fingers and stops precisely at
the line of Tam's shoulder, as if it had read the order twice."*
