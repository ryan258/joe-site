Once a day, a Short Rest hands back four levels of spent slots.

### When to use it

- The **first** Short Rest after real expenditure — never save it for a
  hypothetical better rest; an unused Recovery at day's end is four
  spell levels donated to nobody.

### Mechanics

1. Arcane Recovery, once per day when Joe finishes a Short Rest:
   recover expended slots with a **combined level of 4** (half wizard
   level, rounded up), none 6th+ (moot at level 7).
2. Menu: one 4th; one 3rd + one 1st; two 2nds; one 2nd + two 1sts; or
   four 1sts — any mix totaling 4 levels or less.
3. Buy back what the day still needs, not what was spent: caster
   enemies ahead → a 3rd (Counterspell money); attrition day → 1sts
   (Shield money); the 4th only when a second *Ice Storm* or *Dimension
   Door* is genuinely the afternoon's shape.
4. Pairs with Memorize Spell (play 56) — one Short Rest, both features:
   re-tool the list *and* refund the slots. That rest is a full pit
   stop; argue for it when the party debates pushing on.

### At the table

Mechanically: "We're resting? Then two things: Arcane Recovery — I
take back one 3rd and one 1st-level slot, that's my four levels — and
Memorize Spell to swap Grease out for Gust of Wind. Ready."

Verbally: *"Joe spends the rest doing what looks like nothing and is
actually accounting."*
