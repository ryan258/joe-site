The play underneath every other play: what happens when someone
comes for Cow. Joe's bond, pre-decided, so the worst moment is the
most rehearsed one.

### When to use it

- Anyone shows knowing interest in Mr. Big — a too-long look, the
  wrong question, a *Detect Magic* aimed tableward.
- The abort signal fires during any Cow deployment (plays 86–91),
  or capture/search is imminent.

### Mechanics

1. **Threat ladder, pre-agreed with Cow in images**: *wrong look* →
   Cow goes toy-still and stays (play 88's cover is the first
   wall); *wrong question* → parking (play 89 — the pocket
   dimension defeats every search and seizure ever devised);
   *hands on Mr. Big* → see step 3.
2. **Joe's counter-investigation runs immediately** (plays 76–80,
   85): who asked, who sent them, what do they already know — the
   sheet's backstory makes every Cow-seeker a lead *toward the
   toy's origin*, and Joe hunts leads. Fear, converted to
   fieldwork.
3. **If it comes to force**: the Ideal holds even here — *precise*
   force. Sculpted spells around the toy (never AoE at whoever
   holds Mr. Big — 8d6 does not distinguish silk from spirit-house);
   *Web* the snatcher (a Restrained thief drops nothing and goes
   nowhere); *Dispel Magic* against binding or scrying magic aimed
   at Cow; the 4th-level slot is *always* available for this —
   *Dimension Door* with Mr. Big in hand outranks every other use
   on the sheet (play 73's budget carries a permanent asterisk:
   the Keeper's slot).
4. **The lines Joe will not cross are part of the protocol**: no
   person used as bait to draw the seekers out, no torture of the
   captured thief — interview protocol instead (play 85), because
   the thief is *evidence*, and Joe is gentle with evidence.
5. **After-action**: incident, method, and inference recorded in
   ink (play 83's journal discipline); the campaign-long file on
   who hunts Cow grows by one entry, and the party is briefed. The
   greatest fear, managed like everything else Joe manages:
   thoroughly, quietly, and in writing.

### At the table

Mechanically: "The broker just asked about 'the toy' by name — that's
ladder step two: Magic action, Cow's parked in the pocket dimension
before I answer him. Now the counter-file opens: Insight on his
delivery, and afterward I'm tracing who told him. If his hands had
gotten there first: Web, then questions, and my Dimension Door was
never for me."

Verbally: *"The broker mentions the toy, and something in the tall
scholar goes very still and very precise — not anger; *procedure*.
The little cow is already somewhere no key fits. 'Now,' Joe says,
taking out his pen, 'let's talk about who sent you.'"*
