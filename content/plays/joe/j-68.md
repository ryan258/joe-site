One Potion of Healing, one Bonus Action, one correct moment.

### When to use it

- Joe is under one-hit HP against enemies who have hit him once
  already — drink *before* the hit that drops him, not after the math
  gets exciting.
- Never during safety: out of combat, the party's healer or a rest is
  cheaper.

### Mechanics

1. 2024 rule: **both** drinking a potion and administering (feeding) it to
   another creature within 5 feet of you require a **Bonus Action**.
2. Potion of Healing: **2d4+2** HP (avg 7). It will not out-heal a
   round of focused fire — it buys margin, not victory.
3. Bonus Action economy: it competes with *Misty Step* on the same
   turn. Since using a potion is a Bonus Action but not a spell, it does not
   restrict your Action spellcasting; you can feed a potion (BA) and still
   cast a leveled spell like *Fireball* (Action) on the same turn.
4. Downed allies: Joe can use his Bonus Action to pour the potion into a
   downed fighter — 2d4+2 and they're conscious with their full turn coming,
   while Joe still has his Action free. Mage Hand cannot administer it
   (feeding requires real hands); it can only deliver the bottle (play 17).
5. Restock policy: one is zero. Buy a second potion in every town that
   sells them.

### At the table

Mechanically: "Tam is down — I move adjacent, use my Bonus Action to feed him
the potion for 2d4+2 HP. He's conscious. I still have my Action: I cast Fireball
at the ones who did it."

Verbally: *"Joe drinks like a man annotating an emergency — quickly,
and without letting it interrupt the sentence he was aiming."*
