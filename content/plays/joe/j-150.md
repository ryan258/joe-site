Fifth-level slots are one milestone past next: the planning
play for the biggest single jump the sheet has left.

### When to use it

- Now, in study evenings — because level 9's choices (one
  free Savant spell, two picks, new preparation math) reward
  the wizard who arrives with a list.

### Mechanics

1. **The Savant dividend** (play 75's cadence pays out):
   5th-level slots trigger one free Evocation spell — the
   shortlist to research in-world: *Cone of Cold* (8d6 in a
   60-foot cone, **Sculpt protects 6** — the crowd-signature
   scaled up) versus *Bigby's Hand* (Evocation's control
   masterpiece: a 3rd damage/shove/grapple/wall *every
   round* on one Concentration — the thinking-wizard's
   pick, and this sheet thinks).
2. **The two paid picks** aim where Savant doesn't (play
   144's rule): the perennial candidates — *Wall of Force*
   (the argument-ender: no save, no damage type, the
   ultimate Library-Rule spell, play 120) and a
   telepathy/scrying-shaped tool for the investigation
   engine (*Rary's* or scry-adjacent, campaign depending) —
   priced against what enemy books have donated by then.
3. **One 5th slot changes the day's shape** (play 73's
   budget grows a penthouse): upcast *Fireball* at 10d6,
   six-ray *Scorching Ray*... or the flagship new spell
   — but the *doctrine* holds: the top slot is the day's
   thesis statement, parked till the fight that is the day
   (plays 59–63's logic, one floor up).
4. And the quiet compounding: prepared count rises with
   level (the hinge widens, plays 56, 70), Arcane
   Recovery's refund grows to **5 levels** (a whole 5th —
   or a 4th and a 1st — back at the first rest, play 57),
   and Cow, Mr. Big, the journal, and the file continue
   exactly as they are — some things on this sheet were
   never waiting on a level.

### At the table

Mechanically: "Two milestones out, and the list's already
drafted: the free Evocation is *Bigby's* unless the
campaign turns into crowds, the paid picks are *Wall of
Force* and whatever the next enemy spellbook donates, and
Recovery starts refunding five levels a rest. I've been
reading for it since Harrenford. Wizards don't get
surprised by their own birthdays."

Verbally: *"In the lamplight after the others sleep, Joe
studies spells he cannot cast yet the way he studies
everything — early, thoroughly, and with a bookmark already
placed at the page where the future is scheduled to
arrive."*
