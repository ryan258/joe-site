Ten square feet of floor that costs enemies their turn economy.

### When to use it

- Melee enemies must cross a known square to reach the party.
- An enemy spellcaster or archer needs to be knocked Prone (Prone ranged
  attacks have Disadvantage; standing costs half their Speed).

### Mechanics

1. 1st-level, Action, range 60 feet: a **10-foot square** becomes
   greased **difficult terrain** for 1 minute. No Concentration.
2. Creatures in the area when it appears, or that **enter it or end
   their turn there**, make a Dexterity save vs DC 15 or fall **Prone**.
3. Prone: their melee attacks have Disadvantage, attacks against them
   from within 5 feet have Advantage, and standing costs half their
   movement — through difficult terrain, they are effectively parked.
4. No Concentration means it stacks freely under *Web* (play 27) or
   alongside anything Joe is concentrating on.
5. The grease is flammable in most rulings — ask the DM before counting
   on ignition; RAW 2024 does not burn it automatically.

### At the table

it's difficult terrain either way. It's not Concentration."
