Losing gracefully is a discipline. This is the party's walk-away,
with Joe as rearguard architect.

### When to use it

- The fight audit fails: the second wave, the caster you can't
  counter, the ally down and stabilized but not walking. Someone
  says the word; everyone runs the script.

### Mechanics

1. **Call it early**: retreats fail when called at 20% HP instead of
   50%. The moment the win-condition dies, say the word (agree on
   the word *before* the campaign needs it).
2. **Seal the ground**: *Web* across the pursuit lane (play 2), or
   *Grease* on the stairs behind (play 26) — control spends better
   on exits than on victories. No slots left → the oil pour (play
   69) and Minor Illusion's fake rearguard (play 15).
3. **Slow the fast ones**: *Ray of Frost* on whichever pursuer will
   catch the slowest ally (play 9). This is Joe's cantrip every
   round until the gap holds.
4. **Move the immovable**: *Misty Step* carries only Joe — the
   downed ally travels by fighter's shoulders; Joe's job is buying
   the shoulders time. The 4th-level break-glass: *Dimension Door*
   takes Joe plus the one person who *cannot* run (play 61) — spend
   it when that is the actual shape, not for Joe's own skin while
   the party still runs.
5. **Rally per the map**: the fallback point was named at dawn (the
   script assumes play 96's habit generalized); Cow overwatches the
   route (play 86) and confirms the rally clean.
