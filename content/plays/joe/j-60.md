**Spellbook only.** Dropping the weather directly on the melee, allies
included — included, then excluded.

### When to use it

- The main scrum *is* the target: enemies and allies interlocked, and
  the party's melee could use the enemy standing in difficult terrain.
- Up to five allies in the cylinder — Ice Storm's Sculpt budget is the
  biggest Joe has.

### Mechanics

1. As play 59, centered on the melee: Sculpt Spells protects **5**
   named allies — auto-success, no damage.
2. The terrain rider is not sculpted: allies stand in difficult terrain
   too. Timing answer: allies already *in* melee don't need to move;
   the enemies who want to escape, flank, or reinforce do. The rider
   punishes movement, and the side that needs movement is theirs.
3. Call the cylinder's edge carefully — 20-foot radius is smaller than
   it feels; the archers at the scrum's rim may be out.
4. This is the level-7 flagship expenditure: one 4th-level slot per
   day. Spend it on the fight that decides the day.

### At the table

Mechanically: "Ice Storm, centered on the melee. Sculpt Spells — Brenna,
Tam, Senna, the paladin, and the dog: automatic success, zero damage.
The six gnolls around them: DC 15 Dex, 2d10 plus 4d6, and the ground is
difficult terrain, so nobody's disengaging from our fighters."

Verbally: *"Hail hammers the scrum flat — except for five clean, dry
silhouettes, standing in the storm like footnotes the sky agreed to
respect."*
