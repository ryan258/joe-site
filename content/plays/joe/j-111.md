The wizard is the worst grab in the building, and everyone should
know the drill before something with a mouth finds out.

### When to use it

- Anything with tentacles, tongues, engulf, or swallow is on the
  field — ropers, toads, purple-worm-shaped rumors, chain devils.

### Mechanics

1. Joe's personal ladder (pre-decided): **Misty Step** ends any
   grapple, no contest, Bonus Action (play 38); **Thunderwave**
   shoves the grappler off (play 22, when the slot mix favors it);
   the Athletics escape (+2 Dex... Acrobatics) is the option of last
   resort — a wizard doesn't win contested Strength checks, he
   declines the contest.
2. Swallowed is grappled's endgame: **inside**, there's no line of
   sight for teleports out (Misty Step needs a *seen* destination) —
   which is why the drill is *never get swallowed*: the reaction to
   a grab by a swallower is spending the escape **immediately**, not
   next round. Grabbed → gone, same turn, every time.
3. For allies in the grip: *Web* the grappler (Restrained sets its
   Speed to 0 — it can't drag the fighter anywhere), Ray of Frost
   likewise ends the dragging-away play, and the wand needs no roll
   against a monster whose hands are full.
4. Reach-grapplers get the range treatment: the entire threat has a
   radius, and Joe's kit starts at 60 feet. Stand outside the
   problem; it's free.

### At the table

Mechanically: "Its tongue is a grab — so, standing drill: if it
takes me, I Misty Step the same turn, no debate. Tam, if it takes
you, I Web it — Speed zero, it can grip you but it can't leave with
you, and then we disassemble it at leisure."

Verbally: *"The toad's tongue finds only the memory of a wizard.
'No,' Joe says, from thirty feet to the left, in the tone of a man
declining a second invitation he didn't accept the first time."*
