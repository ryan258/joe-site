Ongoing enemy magic is a structure. *Dispel Magic* is the demolition
permit.

### When to use it

- A standing enemy effect is shaping the fight: *Spirit Guardians*
  grinding the melee, a *Darkness* blinding the archers, a summoned
  wall.
- After the enemy caster dies or flees, their non-instantaneous spells
  linger — clean-up is automatic.

### Mechanics

1. 3rd-level, Action, range 120 feet: choose one creature, object, or
   magical effect. Any spell of **3rd level or lower** on it **ends,
   no roll**.
2. Higher-level spells: Intelligence check at **+4** vs DC 10 + the
   spell's level (a 5th-level effect is DC 15). Upcasting Dispel raises
   the auto-end threshold instead (4th-level Dispel auto-ends 4th).
3. One target per casting: a creature (ends spells *on* it), an object,
   or one effect. Aim at the effect doing the most rounds-per-round of
   work.
4. Identify first when unsure: Arcana +7 (play 77) to name the spell
   tells Joe whether it auto-ends or needs the check — and whether it
   is worth 3rd-level money at all.

### At the table

Mechanically: "That spinning blade-ward has hit us three rounds
running. Dispel Magic at the effect — if it's 3rd level or lower it
just ends; if it's higher I roll Intelligence plus four against DC ten
plus its level."

Verbally: *"Joe looks at the enemy's masterpiece, finds the load-bearing
sentence, and deletes it."*
