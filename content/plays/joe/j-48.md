**Spellbook only.** *Fireball*'s damage in a straight line — for when
the enemy insists on standing in a row.

### When to use it

- Enemies in a corridor, on a bridge, in a shield wall, or otherwise
  collinear with Joe.
- The cluster is against a wall Joe cannot get a *Fireball* radius
  around without catching allies the Sculpt count can't cover.

### Mechanics

1. 3rd-level, Action, self: a line **100 feet long, 5 feet wide**. Each
   creature in it makes a Dexterity save vs DC 15 — **8d6 lightning**
   on a failure, half on a success.
2. It ignites flammable objects in the line — **your own *Web*
   included**; webs are flammable, so a bolt through them likely burns
   the trap away (see play 95 before firing into it).
3. Sculpt Spells: protect up to **4** creatures in the line (1 + 3).
4. Positioning is the whole spell: Joe moves *first* to put the line
   through the most spines. The 30 feet of movement before casting is
   part of the aim.
5. Prep note: currently the book carries it, the sheet does not. Swap
   it in for corridor country — mines, sewers, castles.

### At the table

Mechanically: "I sidestep 15 feet so the doorway, the sergeant, and
both pikemen line up — Lightning Bolt down the corridor. Dexterity save
DC 15 for all four: 8d6 lightning, half on a success."

Verbally: *"Joe adjusts his position like a man aligning a ruler, and
then the corridor happens all at once."*
