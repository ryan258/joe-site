**Spellbook only** (the *Invisibility* half). The heist's bookends: an
entrance nobody saw and an exit that doesn't use the map.

### When to use it

- The job where being *traced* is worse than being caught: stealing
  back Cow-related evidence, burgling a rival collector, the archive
  visit that never happened.
- Exfiltration under pursuit when the exits are held.

### Mechanics

1. Entry: *Invisibility* (play 35) walks Joe — or with a 3rd-level
   upcast, Joe and the specialist — through the front approach. One
   Concentration; budget the hour.
2. The pivot: the moment the objective is in hand, *Invisibility* has
   done its work. If the exit is clean, walk out and save the 4th.
   If it isn't — alarms, dogs, a closed gate —
3. Exit: *Dimension Door* (play 61): Joe plus one willing creature,
   **500 feet**, to the rally point scouted at noon (visualized,
   known, empty). Casting it ends *Invisibility* (casting a spell
   breaks it) — irrelevant, because the caster vanishes as the spell
   betrays him.
4. The discipline is *sequencing*: never spend the 4th on entry
   (walls have far sides you haven't seen — play 62's intelligence
   rule), and never let the hour expire inside. The rally point is
   chosen before anyone turns invisible; Cow watches it (play 86's
   posture) so it is *known* empty at exit time.

### At the table

Mechanically: "Invisibility at 3rd — Senna and me — through the garden
front. If we walk out, fine. The moment it goes loud: Dimension Door,
both of us, to the boathouse platform — Cow's been on its roof all
evening confirming it stays empty. They'll search a house that two
people were never in and no one ever left."

Verbally: *"The collector's guards spend the night guarding an
absence. Five hundred feet away, two absences divide the evidence by
lantern light."*
