*Web* to freeze the enemy's position, then exploit Restrained — or burn
the webs on purpose.

### When to use it

- Enemies are approaching through a chokepoint (door, corridor, bridge),
  or melee threats need to be kept off the party's backline.
- Lead with this when control changes the encounter — often better than
  damage on round one.

### Mechanics

1. Action: cast *Web* (2nd level). Range 60 feet, 20-foot cube,
   Concentration up to 1 hour. The webs need anchoring surfaces (two
   walls, floor and ceiling) or the layer collapses.
2. Creatures in the webs when they appear, or that enter them, make a
   Dexterity save vs DC 15 or are **Restrained**: Speed 0, attacks
   against them have Advantage, their attacks and Dexterity saves have
   Disadvantage.
3. Escape costs an enemy its action: Strength (Athletics) check vs DC 15.
4. Follow-up, two branches:
   - **Keep the webs**: attack with *Ray of Frost* (+7, 2d8 cold, target's
     Speed -10) or the wand. Fire would burn the webs away.
   - **Burn them**: the webs are flammable. Any 5-foot cube exposed to
     fire burns away in one round, dealing 2d4 fire damage to creatures
     that start their turn in the flames. A *Fire Bolt* into the webs
     trades the control for damage — do it when the Restrained enemies
     are nearly done anyway, not before.
5. Restrained enemies make Dexterity saves at Disadvantage — a sculpted
   *Fireball* into the web cube next round is the premium combo (it does
   end the webs).

### At the table

Mechanically: "I cast *Web* filling the corridor, anchored wall to wall —
Dexterity save DC 15 for the guards, Restrained on a failure. I'm
concentrating on it."

Verbally: *"Joe doesn't raise his voice. 'You'll want to stop there.'
The corridor fills with grey silk behind the words, as if the building
itself agreed with him."*

Announce the Concentration out loud so the table tracks it — losing it to
an unnoticed hit is how this play dies.
