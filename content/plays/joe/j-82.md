+5, with air support. Joe sneaks like a man with a map, because he
always has one.

### When to use it

- Approaches, tails, and exits where being unnoticed beats being
  unseen-by-magic (*Invisibility* is loud in the ledger; Stealth is
  free).
- Group infiltration where Joe is *not* the weak link — at +5 he is
  usually mid-pack; position him accordingly.

### Mechanics

1. Dexterity (Stealth) at **+5** to Hide or move unnoticed; the 2024
   Hide action wants Three-Quarters Cover or better, out of enemy
   line of sight — Minor Illusion *manufactures* the cover (play 14).
2. The force multiplier is intelligence: Cow flies the route first
   (play 5), so Joe sneaks past the sentries he *knows about*, on the
   timing the patrol gap *measured*. Stealth checks you never roll are
   the best ones.
3. Sound discipline beats the die: soft ground, slow pace, no metal,
   Message instead of whispers (play 11), Prestidigitation to snuff
   the lantern *before* crossing its light (play 18).
4. When the party's plate-wearers make group stealth a farce, split
   the roles: the quiet half infiltrates, the loud half is the
   scheduled distraction (play 23).

### At the table

Mechanically: "Cow's circuit says the wall-walk is empty for about a
minute after the bell. On the bell: we cross the yard, Stealth — I
rolled 17 — hugging the granary's shadow, and I snuff the gate lantern
with Prestidigitation as we pass it."

Verbally: *"Joe crosses the yard like an errand nobody would question,
at the exact minute nobody is there to question it."*
