**Spellbook only.** Damage now, difficult terrain after — the 4th-level
slot that shapes the next round too.

### When to use it

- A large enemy group at long range (up to 300 feet) that must then
  *cross ground* to matter.
- Slowing a charge, a reinforcement wave, or a cavalry line.

### Mechanics

1. 4th-level, Action, range **300 feet**: a cylinder **20-foot radius,
   40 feet high**. Each creature in it makes a Dexterity save vs DC 15:
   **2d10 bludgeoning + 4d6 cold** on a failure, half on a success.
2. Hailstones turn the area into **difficult terrain until the end of
   Joe's next turn** — the enemies who survive spend the next round
   wading, not charging.
3. Sculpt Spells: protect up to **5** creatures (1 + 4).
4. Upcast at 5th (future): +1d10 bludgeoning. The terrain rider does not
   scale; the range and the slow were always the sale.
5. Damage-per-slot honesty: a 4th-level *Fireball* (9d6) out-damages
   it. Cast *Ice Storm* for the 300-foot reach and the terrain, not the
   dice.

### At the table

Mechanically: "Their second wave is forming up across the field — 280
feet, still in my range. Ice Storm on the muster point: Dexterity save
DC 15, 2d10 bludgeoning plus 4d6 cold, and that ground is difficult
terrain through my next turn. The charge arrives late and thinner."
