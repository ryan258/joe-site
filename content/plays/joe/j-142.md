The case goes to court, the council, or the guild tribunal —
and evidence only counts if it *arrives* credible.

### When to use it

- Presenting findings to any authority whose verdict matters:
  magistrates, nobles, guildmasters, the party's own patron.

### Mechanics

1. **Chain of custody is a thing Joe already built**: dated
   journal entries (play 83's ink discipline), findings logged
   *at the scene* (play 140's order), copies cited to folio
   (play 141) — the file convinces before Joe speaks. This is
   why the discipline plays exist; today they're the case.
2. The presentation runs play 80 at full dress: exhibits in
   dependency order (each one making the next inevitable), the
   Persuasion (+3) carried by the evidence's own Advantage,
   the party-face handling the *politics* while Joe handles
   the *facts* — two speakers, two jobs.
3. **Live authentication is theater that wins**: performing the
   forgery detection *in front of* the tribunal — the ink test,
   the letterform comparison, the seal's re-heat scar (play
   83) — converts "the scholar claims" into "the court saw."
   Ask for the demonstration; never just assert the
   conclusion.
4. Cross-examination cuts both ways: the opposing account gets
   Joe's collation treatment in real time (play 139's
   too-smooth test) — and Cow, toy-still in the gallery
   (play 88), reads the room's *feeling* when each exhibit
   lands: who relaxes, who stops breathing. The next case
   starts in that gallery.

### At the table

Mechanically: "Exhibits go in this order: tide table, then the
manifest copy — cited, folio forty-one, they can send a clerk —
then I do the ink demonstration live on the will itself, with
the court's own lamp. Tam handles the barons' feelings; I
handle the sequence. And Cow's on the gallery rail: when the
seal scar shows, I want to know whose face stops moving."

Verbally: *"The advocate expected a wizard and prepared for
fireworks. What takes the floor instead is a quiet man with a
ledger's momentum — and by the third exhibit the courtroom
understands that the fire was never going to be literal, and
that someone in this room is already caught."*
