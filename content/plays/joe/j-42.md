**Spellbook only.** Three attack rolls, one target, when a single big
save-spell is the wrong tool.

### When to use it

- Single tough target with low AC — three chances at 2d6 beats one
  save-for-half.
- Enemies with Legendary-style save resilience or great Dexterity
  saves: attack rolls sidestep the save entirely.

### Mechanics

1. 2nd-level, Action, range 120 feet: **three rays**, each a separate
   ranged spell attack at +7, each dealing **2d6 fire** on a hit.
2. All on one target: 6d6 potential, resolved as three independent
   attacks — partial hits still pay, unlike an all-or-nothing save.
3. Each hit is a separate damage instance: against a concentrating
   caster, that is up to three Concentration saves (DC 10 each) from
   one 2nd-level slot.
4. Upcast: +1 ray per slot level (four rays at 3rd).
5. Potent Cantrip does **not** apply — leveled spell; misses miss.

### At the table

Mechanically: "Scorching Ray, all three at the hag — 16, 22, 11: two
hits, 2d6 fire each, and she's concentrating on something, so that's
two separate DC 10 Constitution saves."

Verbally: *"Three lines of fire, fired the way Joe asks questions: one
to test, one to confirm, one for the record."*
