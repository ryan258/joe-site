Two cheap control spells, one unwinnable stretch of ground.

### When to use it

- A single chokepoint must hold against numbers for several rounds.
- The party needs time — for a ritual, a barricade, an escape, a rescue.

### Mechanics

1. Round one: *Web* (2nd level, Concentration — play 2) filling the
   chokepoint. Round two: *Grease* (1st level, no Concentration — play
   25) on the ground at the web's near edge.
2. Enemies who escape the webs (Strength check vs DC 15, their Action)
   step out into the grease: Dexterity save vs DC 15 or Prone in
   difficult terrain.
3. Failure chain: an enemy can lose two or three full turns crossing 20
   feet — while Joe's cantrips (play 6) and the party's ranged damage
   collect.
4. Keep *Ray of Frost* (not *Fire Bolt*) as the follow-up: fire ends the
   web layer.
5. Cost check: one 2nd, one 1st, one Concentration. This holds a corridor
   for less than the price of a single *Fireball*.

### At the table

Mechanically: "The web's holding the doorway; now Grease on the ten feet
in front of it. Anyone who tears free of the web steps straight into a
DC 15 Dexterity save or goes Prone. Ray of Frost on whoever's furthest
along."

Verbally: *"Joe has turned twenty feet of corridor into a syllabus:
silk, then oil, then frost — and every raider is repeating a grade."*
