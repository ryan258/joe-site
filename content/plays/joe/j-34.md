Eight hours of AC 15 for a 1st-level slot. This is not a decision; it
is a standing order.

### When to use it

- Cast at the start of any day where danger is reasonably expected —
  which, for an adventurer, is the definition of a day.
- Re-cast timing matters more than whether: see mechanics.

### Mechanics

1. 1st-level, Action, touch, **8 hours**, no Concentration: Joe's base
   AC becomes 13 + Dex = **15**.
2. Cast it when danger *starts* being plausible, not reflexively at
   dawn: an 8 a.m. casting dies at 4 p.m., which is exactly when
   dungeon days get interesting. Leaving a safe town at noon? Cast at
   noon.
3. It ends early if Joe dons actual armor; he won't.
4. Budget: one 1st-level slot of the day's four is pre-spent on this.
   The Free Shield (play 3) stacks on top: 15 → 20 on reaction.
5. It can also be cast on a willing unarmored ally — the barbarian who
   lost their gear, the rescued prisoner (touch, same 8 hours).

### At the table

Mechanically: "We're leaving the gates at midday, so Mage Armor now —
that's AC 15 until eight tonight, one 1st-level slot, no Concentration."

Verbally: *"Joe rolls his sleeves down and the air over his skin
stiffens faintly, like a page protected under glass. Ready for
fieldwork."*
