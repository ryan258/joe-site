The enemy's smartest play is killing the cleric. Joe's job is
making that play expensive enough to be stupid.

### When to use it

- Standing assignment in any party with a healer — and doubly
  the round enemies start *ignoring* the fighter to dive the
  backline.

### Mechanics

1. Geometry first: healer and Joe hold **different corners** of
   the backline (one Fireball shouldn't solve both — the enemy
   evoker exists too), 20–30 feet apart, each with an exit
   square pre-seen (play 38's habit, taught to the cleric).
2. The interception kit, by diver type: fast melee eats **Ray
   of Frost** (-10 makes the dive arrive a round late — a round
   the fighter uses); jumpy types (wolves, assassins) meet
   **Grease** on the healer's doorstep — the pre-laid square
   (play 69's pre-laying logic, with a slot) that makes the
   healer's 5-foot radius a save-or-Prone; the flying diver
   re-reads play 102.
3. The treaty applies (play 132): the healer's Concentration
   usually outranks Joe's — his *Shield* is personal, so the
   loaner is **Mirror Image's positioning trick**: Joe standing
   *near* the healer makes Joe (three of him, play 41) the
   more inviting wrong answer on the same doorstep.
4. The paid version: *Counterspell* covers the healer against
   the enemy caster's *Hold Person* (a Held healer is a dead
   healer — deny it at reaction speed, play 4), and *Dispel
   Magic* un-Holds them when denial missed (play 53). Priority
   spending, pre-authorized in the round-one script.

### At the table

Mechanically: "Standing assignment: I hold the well corner,
you hold the cart — twenty-five feet, separate Fireball
problems. Grease is pre-laid on your doorstep before they
know where you are. Anything fast that comes for you gets
frosted at sixty feet, and if their mage so much as gestures
at you, that's what my reaction's been waiting for all night."

Verbally: *"The assassins have a plan for the healer, and it's
a good one. It does not survive contact with the dry, patient
man by the well who has been pricing exactly this dive since
initiative — and whose floor, frost, and held word were all
purchased in advance, in her name."*
