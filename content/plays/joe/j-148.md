The first two hours in any settlement, spent turning gold and
daylight into capability.

### When to use it

- Every town, on arrival — before rest, before the tavern,
  while shops are open and rumors are fresh.

### Mechanics

1. **The standing shopping list, checked in order**: incense
   for *Find Familiar* (two-portion minimum, play 33 —
   non-negotiable); potions to a two-count (play 68);
   oil flasks to two (play 69); paper, ink, and wax (the
   file eats them, plays 83, 145).
2. **The information sweep runs parallel** (the party
   splits): taverns take Charisma (not Joe); Joe takes the
   *institutions* — the archive, the temple rolls, the
   notice board *read as a document* (what's missing from
   it, play 140's absence-eye), and the local calligrapher
   or bookseller, who knows every literate stranger in town
   (colleagues gossip, play 141's access logic).
3. **The garden gets watered** (play 143): existing sources
   in this town get their walk-bys within the first day —
   networks die of silence, not betrayal.
4. **Exits before inns**: the two roads out, the wall's bad
   gate, the river's boats — walked or Cow-flown before
   sundown (play 5), because play 97's retreat script wants
   its rally points *before* anyone knows the party's here,
   and *Dimension Door*'s 500-foot visualized targets (play
   61) are bought by exactly this stroll.

### At the table

Mechanically: "Two hours, standard split: Tam has the
taverns, I have the archive and the jeweler — incense first,
and a second diamond if the price is honest, because the one
in my coat is a single point of failure. Senna walks the walls with the bird
and names our two exits. We meet at the noticeboard; bring
me what *isn't* posted on it."

Verbally: *"Other parties arrive at towns; Joe *indexes*
them — and two hours later, resupplied, referenced, and
already owning three ways out, he finally sits down to
dinner in a place that has quietly stopped being unknown."*
