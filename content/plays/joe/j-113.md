Their reaction is a 60-foot bubble. Joe's spells work fine from
outside it.

### When to use it

- An enemy caster has countered once (or Arcana pegs them as the
  type) — every big spell of Joe's now has a tax until solved.

### Mechanics

1. *Counterspell*'s trigger needs the caster **within 60 feet** of
   the countering enemy: Joe's *Fireball* reaches 150, *Ice Storm*
   300, *Dispel Magic* 120 — the entire artillery kit operates from
   past the bubble. Step back; cast the same spell; their reaction
   never legally fires.
2. They must also **see** the casting: full cover between
   Joe and the counterspeller at the moment of casting (the pillar
   two-step) achieves the same immunity at closer range.
3. Bait the reaction when range won't work: a 1st-level *Grease*
   cast inside the bubble is cheap tuition — their 3rd-level
   counter on it is a trade Joe wins, and the *Fireball* follows
   uncontested this round.
4. The mirror match (play 51) stays loaded: if Joe must cast inside
   60, his own reaction guards the spell — but outside 60 is free,
   and free beats clever (the ladder rule, applied to tactics).

### At the table

Mechanically: "She countered my Web from across the yard — noted,
she's a counterspeller. So I take eight steps back: I'm now

spell, same target, and her reaction is a nice thought she can
have quietly."
