Following someone through a city for an afternoon: the
surveillance stack, assembled from parts the sheet already
owns.

### When to use it

- A suspect worth knowing more about walks somewhere — and the
  case wants their route, contacts, and drops, not their
  arrest.

### Mechanics

1. **The bird does the following**: Cow at altitude (60 fly,
   the parallel track — play 123's chase logic at walking
   pace) holds the target while Joe trails *out of sight* —
   inside 100 feet where possible for the live link, on the
   remembered-fragments plan where not (play 87's advance
   brief: "the door they enter; the faces they greet; hold").
2. Joe's ground game covers the gaps: rotate look (cloak off,
   different pace), use reflections and stall-fronts
   (Investigation reads a route's *pattern* — where they'll
   turn — better than eyes track the back), and **Message to
   the second follower** (play 11) runs the classic two-man
   rotation without a single visible signal.
3. Counter-surveillance runs both directions: the target's own
   tail-check habits are *evidence* (a clerk who dry-cleans
   like a spy is a finding all by itself — log it, +10 on the
   pattern later); and Joe's own backtrail gets the same
   audit (play 114's paranoia, ambulatory).
4. The drop is the payoff: when the target *leaves* something —
   a package, a chalk mark, a word with a stranger — the tail
   forks: Cow stays on the person, Joe collects the drop
   (Study before touching, play 76; Alarm on it if it stays,
   play 32 — the drop's *collector* is the case's next name).

### At the table

Mechanically: "He's moving — Cow takes the sky and I take the
market side, out of his sightline, inside Cow's hundred feet.
Senna, you're my rotation: Message keeps us linked, we trade
the eye every two blocks. If he drops anything, the bird
stays on him and the package is mine — Studied, then warded,
then left exactly where its owner expects it."

Verbally: *"The fence checks his tail twice, professionally,
and finds the street clean — because the street is clean. The
thing following him has wings, a hundred feet of sky, and the
patience of a toy that has sat on a shelf for thirty years."*
