*Fire Bolt* targets objects, and it ignites flammables. That is a
120-foot lighter.

### When to use it

- Cut a rope bridge or hoist, light a distant brazier or signal fire,
  detonate spilled oil, burn away webs or vines — all from range.
- Any time the environment is more dangerous to the enemy than 2d10.

### Mechanics

1. Ranged spell attack against the object (+7; the DM sets object AC —
   inanimate, unattended objects are often hit automatically).
2. 2d10 fire damage to the object, and flammable objects ignite.
3. Objects have HP and may have damage thresholds; a rope is a turn, a
   drawbridge is a plan.
4. Warn allies about what will be on fire in one round.

### At the table

Mechanically: "I'm not shooting the ogre — I'm shooting the rope on the
chandelier above it. Fire Bolt, 2d10 to the rope, and it's burning."

Verbally: *"Joe never looks at the ogre. He looks at the knot above it,
and the knot catches fire."*
