Cow's spirit cannot be killed, only inconvenienced — if the incense
holds out.

### When to use it

- After Cow's owl form drops to 0 HP (it vanishes; the spirit is
  unharmed).
- Changing operational needs — the ritual can also just re-summon the
  same owl form Joe always uses.

### Mechanics

1. Ritual (no slot, no preparation): casting time 1 hour + 10 minutes
   ritual, and it **consumes** one 10 GP portion of incense per casting.
   Current stock: **two portions** — track them like ammunition and
   restock in every town.
2. When the form drops to 0 HP, Cow vanishes and leaves no body; the
   Fey spirit is safe. Re-summoning restores the same companion — Cow,
   in the owl stat block, housed as ever in Mr. Big.
3. Temporary dismissal needs no ritual at all: play 89.
4. Doctrine: never spend Cow's 1 HP casually (play 91's risk note), and
   never leave town with fewer than two portions of incense.

### At the table

Mechanically: "Camp's set, so I start the hour-long ritual and burn one
of my two incense portions. Cow's form re-knits by the fire — same owl,
same everything. Add 'incense, 10 gold, two portions' to the shopping
list."

Verbally: *"The incense smoke curls into the shape of wings, and
something old and gentle settles back into the little toy cow, like a
reader finding their page."*
