A neighborhood's worth of witnesses, processed like evidence:
wide first, then deep.

### When to use it

- After the incident, before the trail cools: many people saw
  fragments; the case wants the fragments *collated*.

### Mechanics

1. Structure the sweep like a search grid, not a stroll:
   Investigation (+10) says **who** to ask before any asking —
   sightlines (which windows face the alley), routines (who's
   *always* at the well at dawn), and stakes (who profits from
   silence) rank the door-knocks.
2. The party fans out; Joe assigns by instrument — the
   party-face takes the frightened (Persuasion), the
   intimidating one takes nobody (a canvass poisoned is
   poisoned for good), Joe takes the *detailed* (his +10
   converts a rambling account into times and inches), and
   Prestidigitation's small comforts open doors first (play
   19).
3. **Collation is the actual play**: each account logged (ink,
   journal, play 83's discipline) with source, confidence, and
   time — then the Study action on the *set*: where accounts
   agree is the spine; where they *disagree* is either honest
   parallax or a liar, and the one witness whose story fits
   too well gets the second interview (play 85, with the
   contradictions pre-loaded).
4. Message mops up the shy (play 11): the witness who wouldn't
   talk at their door will whisper across a market — a private
   channel, deniable, and Joe's specialty.

### At the table

Mechanically: "Grid first: the alley's overlooked by five
windows and the well. Tam, you charm the baker's row; I take
the lamplighter — he'll have times, and I want times. Sunset,
we collate: every account in the book, and I Study the set as
one document. Whoever's story has no rough edges gets tea
with me tomorrow."

Verbally: *"By dusk the neighborhood's memory — forty loose,
warm, contradictory fragments of it — lies flat in Joe's
journal like assembled type, and somewhere in the third
paragraph, one sentence is lying. He underlines it, gently,
twice."*
