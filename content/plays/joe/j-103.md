Fire is the off-switch, and Joe is the party's most reliable source
of it.

### When to use it

- Trolls and their kin: wounds close unless fire (or acid) says
  otherwise. The party learns this the round the "dead" one stands up.

### Mechanics

1. Joe's role is not the damage race — it is the **seal**: regenerators
   typically don't regain HP on a turn they've taken fire damage, and
   only stay down if the *killing* wound (or the corpse) gets fire.
   *Fire Bolt* every round it matters (2d10, and half on a miss via
   Potent Cantrip — even the misses keep the wounds open).
2. Efficiency order: let the martials pile the damage; Joe's cantrip
   is the metronome that stops the healing. *Burning Hands* or
   *Fireball* when several regenerate at once.
3. The oil pre-soak (play 10) adds 5 Fire damage when the oil first
   ignites before it dries; treat any ongoing burning damage as a
   DM ruling, not a bonus on every fire hit.
4. When it drops: torch or Fire Bolt the body, out loud, before the
   party moves on — "it stays dead" is a declared action, not an
   assumption.
5. Know the variant: some things regrow unless **acid** — Chromatic
   Orb covers it once prepared (play 24 — the diamond is bought, so the
   costly component is no longer the blocker).

### At the table

Mechanically: "It's a troll, so new rules: your job is damage, my job
is arson. Fire Bolt on it every single round — even my misses burn for
half — and when it drops, nobody move until I've burned the body."

Verbally: *"The troll knits itself shut around every axe-wound with
wet, patient sounds — until Joe's small fires start holding the pages
of it open."*
