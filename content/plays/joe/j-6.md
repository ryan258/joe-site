The attrition baseline: with Potent Cantrip, Joe's cantrips never deal
zero damage.

### When to use it

- Any round without a better play — no cluster for *Fireball*, no
  control worth a slot, no reaction bait.
- Long adventuring days when slots must be rationed.
- Against high-AC enemies where a miss would normally waste the turn.

### Mechanics

1. *Fire Bolt*: ranged spell attack +7, range 120 feet, **2d10** fire at
   level 7. It ignites flammable objects not worn or carried.
2. *Ray of Frost*: ranged spell attack +7, range 60 feet, **2d8** cold,
   and the target's Speed drops by 10 feet until the start of Joe's next
   turn.
3. Potent Cantrip: when Joe **misses** with a cantrip attack roll or a
   target **succeeds** on a save against one of his cantrips, the target
   still takes **half damage**.
4. Expected damage vs AC 15: Fire Bolt averages roughly 9 per round even
   counting misses at half. Cantrips are the floor; slots are the spike.

### At the table

Mechanically: "Fire Bolt at the captain — 14 to hit. A miss? Potent
Cantrip: he still takes half of 2d10 fire."

Verbally: *"The bolt goes wide — and splashes anyway, heat washing over
him like Joe had budgeted for the miss. He always budgets for the miss."*
