Thirty feet of distance between Joe and everything designed to hurt the
person standing in the wrong place.

### When to use it

- A found trigger — pressure plate, tripwire, suspicious chest, lever —
  after Investigation (+10) has said what it probably does.
- Standard procedure: nothing gets opened by hand that can be opened by
  the hand.

### Mechanics

1. Cantrip, Action, range 30 feet, duration 1 minute. A spectral hand
   that can manipulate objects, open unlocked doors and containers, and
   carry up to **10 pounds**.
2. As a Magic action, move the hand up to 30 feet and use it: pull the
   lever, lift the chest lid, tug the wire, drag the idol off the
   pedestal.
3. The hand cannot attack, activate magic items, or carry more than 10
   pounds — a portcullis winch is beyond it; a latch is not.
4. Everyone stands outside the blast-radius guess *before* the hand
   pulls. State the guess out loud; that is what the Investigation was
   for.

### At the table

Mechanically: "Investigation said the plate likely triggers darts from
the west wall, so everyone stacks east. Mage Hand presses the plate from
30 feet."

Verbally: *"A hand of pale light settles onto the plate as gently as a
librarian closing a book. The wall spits its darts at nobody."*
