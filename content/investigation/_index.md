## ⚖️ Core Rule: Search vs. Study

* **Search and Perception** find or notice evidence.
* **Study and Investigation** interpret evidence that Joe can access.
* The DM decides whether a check is needed, which ability and skill apply, and what a success reveals.
* D&D Beyond displays Passive Investigation 20. The 2024 rules do not define that value as a universal, always-on clue detector.
* A DM may use Passive Investigation as a table convention, but Joe never claims that the displayed 20 forces a discovery.

## 🧭 Joe's Four-Step Method

<div class="investigation-step-card">
  <div class="step-indicator">Step 1</div>
  <h3 class="investigation-step-title">1. State The Goal</h3>
  <p style="color:var(--text-med);margin-bottom:8px;">Say what Joe wants to learn.</p>
  <div class="play-table-phrase">
    <span class="table-phrase-label">Table Script</span>
    "I want to determine whether this room contains space that the visible layout does not account for."
  </div>
</div>

<div class="investigation-step-card">
  <div class="step-indicator">Step 2</div>
  <h3 class="investigation-step-title">2. State The Method</h3>
  <p style="color:var(--text-med);margin-bottom:8px;">Describe how Joe approaches the problem.</p>
  <div class="play-table-phrase">
    <span class="table-phrase-label">Table Script</span>
    "I compare the interior dimensions with the corridor and exterior wall, then inspect any mismatch for a mechanism."
  </div>
</div>

<div class="investigation-step-card">
  <div class="step-indicator">Step 3</div>
  <h3 class="investigation-step-title">3. Choose A Suitable Tool</h3>
  <p style="color:var(--text-med);margin-bottom:8px;">Use the skill, feature, or spell that directly addresses the uncertainty:</p>
  <ul style="color:var(--text-med);padding-left:18px;margin-top:6px;line-height:1.6;">
    <li><strong>Perception or Search:</strong> for concealed objects, movement, sounds, and visible hazards.</li>
    <li><strong>Investigation or Study:</strong> for traps, ciphers, riddles, mechanisms, and design inconsistencies.</li>
    <li><strong>Insight:</strong> for behavior and motives.</li>
    <li><strong>Survival:</strong> for tracks, travel signs, and animal behavior.</li>
    <li><strong>Arcana:</strong> for magical theory and effects.</li>
    <li><strong>History:</strong> for historical context.</li>
    <li><strong>Mage Hand:</strong> to probe or trigger suspicious contact points from a distance.</li>
    <li><strong>Cow (Mr. Big):</strong> to scout from angles Joe cannot reach using the Owl stat block.</li>
    <li><strong>Detect Magic:</strong> to identify magical presence within the spell's limits (cast as ritual).</li>
    <li><strong>Arcane Eye:</strong> for sustained remote observation through keyholes and ducts.</li>
  </ul>
</div>

<div class="investigation-step-card">
  <div class="step-indicator">Step 4</div>
  <h3 class="investigation-step-title">4. Separate Evidence From Theory</h3>
  <p style="color:var(--text-med);margin-bottom:8px;">State what Joe knows as fact, then offer a hypothesis.</p>
  <div class="play-table-phrase">
    <span class="table-phrase-label">Table Script</span>
    "The curved scrapes show that something heavy pivots across this floor. Joe suspects the bookshelf may swing open, but he tests that theory before treating it as fact."
  </div>
</div>

## ⚡ Fast Table Phrases

* "What can Joe see or hear before he starts handling anything?"
* "I Search for a concealed trigger or hazard."
* "I Study the mechanism to understand how it works."
* "I compare the room's dimensions with the surrounding structure."
* "I separate what Joe observed from what he currently suspects."
* "Can Arcana or History identify the tradition behind this?"
* "I send Cow to observe from a safer angle."
* "I use *Mage Hand* to test the suspected contact point from range."
* "I cast *Detect Magic* as a ritual if we have time."
* "What would Joe need to verify before acting on this theory?"

---

## 🛡️ Safety And Cooperation Doctrine

* Share suspected hazards before anyone advances.
* Do not send a companion forward merely to trigger a trap.
* Let characters with better senses, tools, or relevant knowledge contribute.
* Use remote tools when they reduce risk without creating a new one.
* Accept that a high modifier improves a roll; it does not make Joe infallible.
* Joe's flaw may cause him to overestimate his control after understanding a mechanism. Let that be a roleplaying complication, not authority over the DM's adjudication.
