# 2024 D&D (5.5e) Rules Arbitration Guide

This document records the exact mechanical adjudications and 2024 Player's Handbook (5.5e) rules-as-written (RAW) decisions implemented across the **Joe, Keeper of the Cow** tabletop companion site.

---

## 1. Action Economy & Spellcasting

### One Spell Slot Per Turn
* **Core Rule:** On any single turn, a creature can expend only **one spell slot** to cast a spell (2024 PHB p. 237).
* **Implication for Bonus Actions:** Casting a bonus action spell using a slot (e.g. *Misty Step*) prevents casting any action spell that expends a slot on that same turn. Casting a cantrip (such as *Fire Bolt*) or taking a non-spell action remains legal.
* **Implication for Reactions on Joe's Turn:** If Joe casts a spell using a slot as an Action (e.g. *Fireball*), he **cannot** cast *Counterspell* using a slot on that same turn, even if an enemy counterspells his *Fireball*.
* **Reactions on Other Turns:** Reactions taken on another creature's turn (such as an enemy's turn or an ally's turn) occur on a separate turn. Joe can freely cast *Counterspell* or *Shield* on other turns, provided his Reaction and spell slot are available.
* **Magic Items:** Activating a magic item (e.g., *Wand of Magic Missiles*) uses the Magic Action and does **not** expend a spell slot. Therefore, casting *Misty Step* with a 2nd-level slot and activating the wand as an Action is legal.

### Counterspell Adjudication
* **Trigger:** You see a creature within 60 feet casting a spell that has **Verbal, Somatic, or Material components**. Spells or abilities lacking components cannot be perceived as casting and cannot be counterspelled.
* **Saving Throw Mechanic:** The target makes a **Constitution saving throw** against Joe's spell save DC (DC 15). On a failure, the spell fails and has no effect.
* **Slot Preservation:** If the interrupted spell required a spell slot, the caster does **not** lose the spell slot; only their action/bonus action/reaction is expended.
* **No Upcast Benefit:** The 2024 *Counterspell* has no higher-level effect. A 4th-level slot does not make lower-level spells fail automatically (that was the 2014 version). The target always makes the Constitution save.

### Dimension Door
* **Passenger Capacity:** Joe can teleport himself and **one willing creature** within 5 feet.
* **Size Restriction Removed:** Under the 2024 rules revision, the passenger is no longer restricted to Joe's size or smaller; any willing creature within 5 feet may be transported.
* **Arrival:** The passenger arrives within 5 feet of Joe's destination space.

## 2. Evoker Subclass Features

### Sculpt Spells
* **Eligibility:** Wizard Evocation spells that force other creatures to make a saving throw (e.g., *Fireball*, *Thunderwave*, *Lightning Bolt*, *Ice Storm*, *Burning Hands*).
* **Target Cap:** Joe can choose up to **$1 + \text{Spell Level}$ other creatures he can see** in the spell's area.
* **Restrictions:**
  * **Joe Cannot Sculpt Himself:** The feature specifically designates *other* creatures; Joe is never a valid target for Sculpt Spells.
  * **Objects Are Not Creatures:** Inanimate objects, vehicles, structures, and cargo cannot be protected by Sculpt Spells and take normal spell effects/damage.
* **Effect Scope:** Protected creatures automatically succeed on their saving throws and **take 0 damage** when a successful save would normally deal half damage.
* **Rider Effects Not Negated:** Sculpt Spells does not negate secondary environmental effects that do not depend on taking damage (e.g., difficult terrain created by *Ice Storm*).

### Potent Cantrip
* **Missed Attack Rolls:** Cantrips requiring attack rolls (*Fire Bolt*, *Ray of Frost*) deal **half damage** on a missed attack roll. Riders (such as *Ray of Frost*'s movement reduction) do not apply on a miss.
* **Successful Saves:** Cantrips requiring saving throws deal half damage when the target succeeds on its save.

## 3. Cow (Mr. Big) Fey Familiar Mechanics

### Statblock Template & Senses
* **Statblock Base:** Revised 2024 Owl statblock.
* **Stats:** AC 11, 1 HP, Speed 5 ft land / 60 ft fly, Darkvision 120 ft, Perception +5, Stealth +5, Passive Perception 15.
* **Keen Hearing and Sight:** The revised Owl statblock removed the *Keen Hearing and Sight* trait; Cow does not have passive advantage on perception checks relying on sight/hearing.

### Movement & Staying Aloft
* **Shared Movement Budget:** Cow's 60 ft flying speed is the total movement available on its turn. The approach to a target and subsequent egress share this single 60 ft budget (e.g., 25 ft approach leaves 35 ft for egress).
* **Staying Aloft vs. Hover:** Cow can remain aloft in a stationary position without needing the Hover trait. However, lacking Hover means Cow falls if it is knocked **Prone**, becomes **Incapacitated**, or has its **Fly Speed reduced to 0**.

### Flyby Trait Scope
* **Opportunity Attacks Only:** Flyby prevents Opportunity Attacks when flying out of a hostile creature's reach.
* **Vulnerabilities:** Flyby provides **no protection** against ranged attacks on the enemy's turn, readied actions, or area-of-effect spells. Cow's destination after egress must be chosen with high cover in mind.

### Action Restrictions
* **No Attacks:** As a familiar, Cow cannot take the Attack action, nor can it replace an attack to douse a creature or object with oil.
* **Help Action:** Cow can take the Help action on its independent turn to grant an ally Advantage on their next attack roll against a creature within 5 feet. Help is tactical and optional, evaluated against exposure risk.
* **Touch Spell Delivery:** Cow uses its **Reaction** to deliver a touch spell cast by Joe (Action) when within 100 feet.

## 4. Equipment & Inventory Adjudications

### Wand of Magic Missiles
* **Action Economy:** Activating the wand uses the **Magic Action**. It requires no Verbal, Somatic, or Material components.
* **Expending Charges:** The wand holds 7 charges, but a single casting can spend at most **3 charges** (5 darts).
* **7th Charge Destruction Risk:** Expending the 7th charge triggers a 1d20 roll; on a 1, the wand turns to ash and is destroyed. Doctrine strictly prohibits spending the 7th charge except in a TPK emergency.

### Weapon Mastery
* **Restriction:** Pure Wizards lack the Weapon Mastery class feature. Even though character sheet tools may show weapon masteries (*Nick* on daggers, *Topple* on quarterstaffs), Joe cannot apply mastery properties to attacks.
