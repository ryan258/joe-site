Flyby Touch Spell Delivery

Action + Cow Reaction
When to use: Target is out of Joe's reach or high danger, but within Cow's 60ft fly range.

Joe casts a touch-range spell. Cow (within 100ft) uses its Reaction to deliver the touch. With Flyby, Cow moves into reach, touches the target, and flies away without provoking Opportunity Attacks.

Table Phrasing "Cow swoops in with 60ft Flyby, uses its Reaction to deliver my touch spell, and circles back 30ft high without provoking opportunity attacks."
